# World Building: The Last of Us

## The Cordyceps Infection
- **Cause**: Mutated Cordyceps fungus originating from South American crops
- **Transmission**: Bites from infected, contact with spores (airborne in enclosed spaces), tendrils (TV show)
- **Incubation**: Can turn within minutes depending on bite location; by day two, full transformation
- **Immunity**: Extremely rare; Ellie Williams is the only known immune individual
- **Nature**: The fungus takes over the host's brain, destroying identity and higher function

## Infected Stages (Critical for Gamer System Point Values)

### Stage 1 — Runner (Days to weeks)
- Still look and sound human
- Fast, aggressive, attack in groups
- Weakest stage; rely on speed and numbers
- **Story relevance**: Most common; MC will farm these early for points

### Stage 2 — Stalker (Weeks to months)
- Beginning fungal growth
- Hunt strategically; hide, flank, ambush
- More dangerous than Runners due to unpredictability
- **Story relevance**: Higher point value; require caution

### Stage 3 — Clicker (Months to a year)
- Blind — fungal growth covers entire head
- Use echolocation (distinctive clicking sounds)
- One-hit kill danger in close quarters; extremely strong
- **Story relevance**: Significant point value; major threat even for experienced MC

### Stage 4a — Bloater (Years)
- Massive, heavily armored in thick fungal plates
- Throw spore bombs/mycotoxin projectiles
- Incredibly durable; requires heavy weaponry
- **Story relevance**: Boss-tier encounters; very high point value

### Stage 4b — Shambler (Years, wet environments — Part II)
- Release corrosive acidic gas
- Explode on death, creating area-of-effect damage
- Control space; punish close-range combat
- **Story relevance**: Environmental hazard; moderate-high point value

### Unique — Rat King
- Multiple infected bodies fused together over decades
- Enormous health pool, multiple attack phases, can split apart
- Found in long-abandoned, heavily-infected zones
- **Story relevance**: Ultimate boss encounter; massive point reward

## Major Factions

### FEDRA (Federal Disaster Response Agency)
- **Role**: Military government that took control during outbreak
- **Structure**: Authoritarian martial law; controls quarantine zones
- **Methods**: Brutal enforcement, execution of infected, curfews, rationing
- **Status by 2033**: Most QZs have fallen; Boston QZ is one of the last standing
- **Public perception**: Hated by civilians but provides some order

### Fireflies
- **Leader**: Marlene (until killed by Joel in 2034)
- **Goal**: Restore democratic government; find a cure for Cordyceps
- **Methods**: Guerrilla warfare, propaganda ("Look for the light"), smuggling
- **Symbol**: Firefly pendants; graffiti marks
- **Status**: Weakening throughout Part I; remnants form in various cities; rumors of regrouping in California by Part II
- **Motto**: "When you're lost in the darkness, look for the light"

### WLF (Washington Liberation Front) — "Wolves"
- **Location**: Seattle, WA
- **Leader**: Isaac Dixon
- **Composition**: Former Fireflies and recruits; well-armed, organized
- **Enemy**: Seraphites
- **Status**: Dominant military force in Seattle; at war with Seraphites

### Seraphites — "Scars"
- **Location**: Seattle area, primarily on an island
- **Structure**: Religious cult following a prophet
- **Methods**: Ritualistic scarring, archery, silent communication (whistling)
- **Notable members**: Yara, Lev (who defected)

### Hunters / Bandits
- **Various groups**: Pittsburgh hunters, David's cannibals, Rattlers (Santa Barbara)
- **Methods**: Ambushes, fake distress signals, slavery, cannibalism
- **No central organization**: Independent groups controlling territories

## Key Locations

### Austin, Texas
- Joel and Sarah's home; where the outbreak begins
- Site of Sarah's death (canonically)
- **Story relevance**: MC's entry point; where Sarah is saved

### Boston Quarantine Zone
- One of the last functioning FEDRA-controlled zones
- Where Joel works as a smuggler with Tess
- Ellie grows up here under Firefly protection

### Jackson, Wyoming
- Tommy and Maria's community
- Self-sufficient settlement; uses a hydroelectric dam for power
- Patrols, farming, hunting, families — closest thing to pre-outbreak normalcy
- **Story relevance**: Model for MC's community; eventual meeting point with Joel/Tommy

### Salt Lake City — St. Mary's Hospital
- Firefly base; location of the surgeon's operating theater
- Where Joel killed Dr. Anderson and Marlene

### Seattle, Washington
- WLF territory; major urban battleground in Part II
- Abandoned buildings, Seraphite island nearby

## Survival Details (Important for Gamer System Economy)
- **Weapons**: Handguns, rifles, shotguns, bows; all scarce. Melee: pipes, bats, machetes
- **Crafting**: Makeshift items from scavenged parts — molotov cocktails, shivs, med kits, nail bombs
- **Resources**: Bullets are currency; medicine is precious; food is scarce
- **Transportation**: Cars are rare (no gasoline infrastructure); mostly on foot or horseback
- **Communication**: Radios; no internet/cell service
- **Infrastructure**: Electricity only where generators or dams exist (Jackson has hydroelectric)
- **Weather**: All four seasons; winter is especially deadly

## Social Dynamics in the Apocalypse
- **Trust**: Extremely rare; strangers are assumed hostile
- **Trade**: Barter economy; ration cards in QZs
- **Leadership**: Ranges from military dictatorship (FEDRA) to community democracy (Jackson) to cult rule (Seraphites)
- **Morality**: Survival forces brutal choices; the line between "good" and "bad" people is blurred
- **Children**: Rare and precious; communities protect them fiercely
- **Hope**: Jackson represents what's possible; most of the world has given up on rebuilding
