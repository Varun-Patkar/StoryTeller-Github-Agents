# Character: Ellie Williams

## Overview
A 14-year-old girl immune to the Cordyceps infection. In canon, she's the key to a potential vaccine and the emotional center of the series alongside Joel. In our story, she eventually joins the MC's community and is adopted by the MC and Sarah.

## Personality
- **Sarcastic and witty**: Constant jokes, puns, quips — humor as armor
- **Brave to the point of recklessness**: Dives into danger without hesitation
- **Survivor's guilt**: Riley turned; she didn't. Feels her immunity must mean something
- **Fiercely loyal**: Once she bonds with someone, she'll die for them
- **Curious**: Loves pun books, comic books ("Savage Starlight"), and pre-outbreak artifacts
- **Stubborn**: Doesn't take no for an answer
- **Violent when necessary**: Kills without hesitation to protect herself and others, but it costs her emotionally
- **Dreams of space**: Wanted to be an astronaut in a different world

## Age in Our Story Timeline
- **Born 2019** (game timeline)
- **2033**: 14 years old — when Joel meets her in canon
- **Enters our story**: Likely around 2033-2034 when Joel brings her to the community

## Abilities
- **Immune to Cordyceps**: Can walk through spores without a gas mask
- **Knife fighting**: Her primary combat skill; quick and lethal up close
- **Archery**: Learned from Joel
- **Swimming**: Joel taught her (she couldn't swim initially)
- **Guitar**: Joel taught her; becomes her emotional outlet
- **Stealth**: Small, quick, quiet — excellent at sneaking
- **Basic medicine**: Learned to treat wounds out of necessity (nursed Joel back to health)

## Relationships
- **Joel (surrogate father)**: The most important relationship in canon. "Everyone I have cared for has either died or left me. Everyone except for you."
- **Riley (first love, deceased)**: Best friend who turned; the source of Ellie's guilt
- **Marlene**: Knew Ellie's mother; was supposed to protect Ellie
- **Sarah**: In our story, Sarah becomes a mother/older sister figure. ~20-year age gap. They bond over both being "Joel's girls"
- **The MC**: Adoptive parent alongside Sarah; Ellie is initially wary but warms to someone who genuinely cares
- **Dina**: Love interest in Part II canon; may or may not appear depending on story timeline

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Constant puns and jokes: "It runs in your jeans!" (diarrhea/genes joke)
- "Endure and survive" — from her favorite comic book, Savage Starlight
- "I'm scared of ending up alone." — her deepest fear
- Swears casually and frequently
- When nervous: talks more, jokes more
- When angry: goes quiet and cold (like Joel)
- Physical: fidgets with her switchblade; picks at things; bounces her leg
- Uses "dude" a lot
- When hurt/emotional: pushes people away, isolates, then comes back

## Key Quotes
- "Everyone I have cared for has either died or left me. Everyone except for you. So don't tell me I would be safer with somebody else, because the truth is I would just be more scared."
- "I'm just a girl. Not a threat."
- "After all we've been through... everything that I've done... it can't be for nothing."
- "I don't think I can ever forgive you for that. But I would like to try." (to Joel about the hospital lie)
- "Swear to me. Swear to me that everything you said about the Fireflies is true."

## Arc in Our Story
1. **Pre-community**: Lives in Boston QZ; bitten, discovers immunity; meets Joel via Marlene's deal
2. **Journey with Joel**: Some canon events may still play out (adapted)
3. **Arriving at the community**: Joel brings Ellie to the MC's settlement
4. **Finding family**: Adopted by MC and Sarah; for the first time, she has stability
5. **Immunity dilemma**: The community must decide what to do about Ellie's immunity — without killing her
6. **Growing up**: Becomes a capable defender of the community

## Relevance to Our Story
Ellie represents the next generation and the possibility of a cure. Her arrival adds both hope and danger (Fireflies and others want her). The MC's meta-knowledge of Ellie's importance creates tension between protecting her and potentially saving humanity.
