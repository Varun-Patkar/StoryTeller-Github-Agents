# Story Summary: New Game Plus: The Last of Us

## Arc 1: Outbreak Day

- **Chapter 1**: The MC wakes up in the body of Marcus Webb, a stranger in a north Austin apartment, on the night of September 27, 2013 — Outbreak Day. The Gamer System activates with a bare-bones tutorial, granting access to a Status screen (all average stats, zero points) and the Mall shop (which he can't afford yet). As sirens and chaos escalate outside, the MC realizes where and when he is. He encounters his first Runner, barely escaping over a fence. The system issues a quest — "Save Sarah Miller" — marked URGENT with no quest marker. He finds the Miller house empty (Joel, Tommy, and Sarah have already fled) and sprints toward the highway overpass where he knows the shooting will happen.
