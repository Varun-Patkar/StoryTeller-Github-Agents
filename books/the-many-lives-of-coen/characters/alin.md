## Personality & Traits

Alin runs where he should walk, copies older children, and boasts until something truly frightens him. He sulks when excluded and calls for Mama when hurt or scared. His speech is simple because he is nine and has had no schooling.

## Story Function

Alin's canon death in a frozen river becomes the story's first changed tragedy. Coen's dormant fragment warns him in time to save Alin, and the terror of the near loss triggers his first memory awakening. Alin remains part of the living family and proof that remembered canon can be changed.
