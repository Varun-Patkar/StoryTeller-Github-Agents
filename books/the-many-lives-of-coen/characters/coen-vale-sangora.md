## Appearance

A miner's build shaped by work rather than training. Silver exposure later leaves visible signs of argyria. He is gentle with his youngest siblings and rougher with Alin, who is old enough to roughhouse with him.

## Personality & Traits

Family-first, practical, and accustomed to swallowing frustration. Before awakening he dreams of leaving Vale Sangora but refuses to abandon the people who depend on him. Restored modern memories make him more strategic and occasionally impatient, but they do not erase his local love, guilt, faith, or habits.

## Backstory

Born into Pieter and Esme's family in Laslea. His younger brother Alin died in a frozen river in canon. In this story, Coen's dormant fragment warns him an instant before the ice breaks. He saves Alin, and the fear tears open his memories of a quiet modern life spent loving games and popular fiction.

## Motivations & Goals

He first wants to stop every disaster he remembers: keep Esme from breaking, get the family out of Vale Sangora, and avoid Brencis's trap. After the family is captured, he intends to use Lunka's knowledge of Greifberg Castle for a stealth rescue and kill Brencis before the vampire can turn her. He needs to accept that knowledge is not control and that his family members are people, not pieces in a route he can optimize.

He never reveals future knowledge, reincarnation, or the collective. He gives ordinary reasons for informed choices and accepts that secrecy sometimes limits how directly he can warn people.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

As a village child, Coen uses short, plain phrases and argues through repetition rather than clever replies. He and his siblings are illiterate before Anca teaches him. After awakening and growing older, his thoughts become more complex than his spoken language. He remains plainspoken around family and only uses modern phrasing in private thought or the Council.

> "I don't need you to believe all of it. I need you to pack before the road closes."

> "Knowing a bad thing is coming doesn't tell you how to make people move."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A dutiful local young man carrying unexamined resentment and love.
- **Key turning points:** Saving Alin restores modern memory; Brencis's turning establishes the collective; Witcher Coen answers; Anachora fails to claim the whole soul.
- **Projected state:** First among equals in the Council, responsible for coordinating lives he cannot own.
