## Appearance

Lean from missed meals, quick on his feet, and dressed in repaired clothes chosen for movement and hidden pockets. He keeps his hair short with a stolen kitchen knife because lice are harder to fight than guards.

## Personality & Traits

Watchful, funny when he feels safe, and fiercely protective of the few people he considers his. He knows when to disappear, when to lie, and which adults will trade a child for coin. Ciri brings out the thirteen-year-old beneath that caution.

## Backstory

An orphan raised in Cintra's streets. He becomes one of the children Ciri plays with when she escapes palace duties. He does not know that his instincts come from a damaged soul fragment or that he has latent magical potential.

## Motivations & Goals

Before awakening, he wants food, a secure sleeping place, and another afternoon with Ciri before the palace takes her back. After the sack begins, he wants to keep them both alive without turning Ciri into someone who only obeys him.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Short, irreverent sentences around Ciri; careful politeness around dangerous adults. He uses jokes to make fear manageable, not to perform cleverness.

> "Your Highness can order the wall to move. Coen says we climb it."

> "I'm scared too. Run anyway."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A self-reliant child who treats attachment as a temporary luxury.
- **Key turning points:** Friendship with Ciri; awakening during Cintra's sack; first Council meeting; Chaos training; intervention before Ciri joins the Rats.
- **Projected state:** Ciri's partner and a magical specialist who can disagree with the first Coen without breaking collective unity.
