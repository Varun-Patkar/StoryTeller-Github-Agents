## Appearance

Anca dresses for herb gathering and work around a smoky hut rather than village display. Foundational witchcraft scars mark her forearm, so she keeps them beneath long sleeves, wraps, or gloves around other people.

## Personality & Traits

Patient, warm, and curious about how another person reaches an answer. Anca teaches Latin by asking Coen to infer meaning from grammar, sentence structure, and familiar roots, then guides him when a guess misses. She can slip into the clipped severity of the teacher she hated at St. Tyna's abbey, especially when frightened or pressed for time. When she hears it, she usually softens or turns the lesson into a question. She helps people without pretending herbs can fix every hurt and remains careful about what she reveals.

## Backstory

Anca once served at St. Tyna's abbey before returning to the Laslea area as a healer and herbalist. She disliked its strict instruction and the authority behind it, even though traces of that teaching remain in her habits. She possesses forbidden knowledge beyond ordinary remedies, including the foundational scar pattern that opens a practitioner to witchcraft and the manuals used to learn and prepare hexes through it.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants useful knowledge kept alive and used with care. She will teach a determined child to read, but she does not expose witchcraft merely because he asks clever questions.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Anca speaks plainly to villagers. As a teacher, she offers clues and asks students to reason from structure rather than recite blindly. Her humor is gentle and occasional. She does not humiliate mistakes, deliver polished speeches, or explain secrets before trust is earned.

> "Start with the words you know. What do they tell you about the rest?"

> "Close. Look at the ending and try once more."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A private healer willing to teach Coen letters but not her dangerous craft.
- **Key turning points:** Coen proves patient and discreet; Anca identifies her scars as the witchcraft conduit and gives him conditions to meet; she eventually carves his foundational gate and teaches him to prepare and pay for hexes.
- **Projected state:** Coen's first magical teacher and a trusted ally who never learns why he began preparing so early.
