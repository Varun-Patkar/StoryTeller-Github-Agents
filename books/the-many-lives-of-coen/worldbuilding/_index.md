# Worldbuilding

## World Overview

Reality appears as countless separate worlds. A soul from the unnamed primary universe normally passes whole into the world that answered its strongest attachment. Coen's passage fails: his soul tears, and its fragments take root in many worlds as distinct lives. The opening follows Vale Sangora in 1347 and the Continent around the fall of Cintra.
