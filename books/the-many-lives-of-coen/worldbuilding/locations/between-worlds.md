## Description

An apparent dark expanse filled with distant spheres of light. Each sphere is a complete world, though the opening never explains this directly. A dead soul is drawn toward the world that matches its strongest attachments.

## Story Function

The modern man's soul crosses this expanse in the prologue. An unseen failure tears it apart and scatters fragments into many spheres. The location is not presently reachable by any incarnation and is distinct from both the Council space and Anachora.
