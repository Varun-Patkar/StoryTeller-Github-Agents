## Description

The capital ruled by Queen Calanthe before Nilfgaard's invasion. Its palace and wealthy streets sit close enough to crowded working districts for Princess Ciri to escape supervision and play among common children.

## Canon Constraints

The story follows book continuity. Nilfgaard defeats Cintra at Marnadal, attacks the under-defended capital, and breaks the keep after days of resistance. Ciri escapes the slaughter and later reaches Yurga's household before reuniting with Geralt. Coen's presence may change who survives around Ciri, but Ciri remains an active participant rather than cargo to be protected.
