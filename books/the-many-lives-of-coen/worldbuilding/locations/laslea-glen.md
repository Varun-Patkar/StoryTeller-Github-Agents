## Description

A poor farming and mining village in Vale Sangora. The household economy depends on the nearby silver mines. By 1347, plague, Brencis's rule, and the compulsory Blood Mass have reduced ordinary family decisions to questions of survival.

## Canon Constraints

In game canon, Alin's death is the first loss behind Esme's withdrawal; plague and Vrakhiri rule worsen it until she barely speaks, eats, or leaves bed. Lunka later catches plague and Brencis cures her with vampire blood, but she remains herself through captivity and can write to Coen. Brencis only turns her into a vrakhir near the finale.

In this story, Alin survives. Esme remains emotionally scarred by later events but does not become physically weak or nearly catatonic. Mirto and Lunka are very young when Alin falls through the river and would not remember his canon death. Coen still develops silver poisoning through mine work, survives Brencis's failed turning, and faces the seizure of his family.
