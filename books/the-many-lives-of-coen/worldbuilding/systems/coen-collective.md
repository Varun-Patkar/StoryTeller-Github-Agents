## Overview

Coen's original soul was torn into fragments during transmigration. Each fragment entered a different world, became a different body, and lived a full local life. They are the same person at the root, but none is a disposable copy. Experience, relationships, biology, and culture produce real differences between them.

## Rules & Limitations

1. **Dormant intuition:** Before awakening, a Coen has no explicit past-life memories. Familiar people, places, and dangers may produce instincts or unexplained preferences.
2. **First awakening:** A severe personal event can restore memories of the modern life to a dormant fragment. This alone does not create contact with other worlds.
3. **First connection:** The collective begins only when Dawnwalker Coen survives Brencis's turning. That metaphysical transformation reaches the other fragments. Witcher Coen is the first to answer during the sack of Cintra.
4. **Council space:** Connected Coens can call or be called into a neutral shared space. On entering, their memories synchronize up to that moment. There is no constant live link outside the Council.
5. **Council names:** Inside the shared space, each incarnation uses a world or nature based name such as Dawnwalker Coen, Witcher Coen, or Cyber Coen. These are practical identifiers, not ranks or separate true names.
6. **Autonomy:** Synchronization adds memories; it does not overwrite personality, affection, loyalties, or local identity. No Coen may command another like a puppet.
7. **Power bleed:** Bodily and spiritual traits pass as small, world-compatible echoes. The share grows through time, use, additional connected lives, and improved compatibility.
8. **Translation:** A world decides how foreign power can exist. Witchcraft can strengthen Witcher Coen's Chaos talent. In a world without usable magic, the same pattern may express through technology, cyberspace, biology, or not at all.
9. **Skills travel best:** Learned timing, languages, tactics, pain tolerance, and craft knowledge transfer more cleanly than supernatural output. Bodies still require conditioning.
10. **Collective reinforcement:** Every connected life strengthens the others' baseline body and soul by a meaningful but non-explosive amount. Growth compounds as the collective expands.
11. **Costs also bleed:** Blood hunger, corruption, magical strain, traumatic memories, and other weaknesses can cross in reduced or translated form.
12. **Death:** A connected Coen's memories and developed soul remain within the collective, but death still ends that local life and relationship. The collective cannot casually recreate a lost body.
13. **No interface:** The collective has no numbers, notifications, shop, quests, administrator, or external guiding intelligence.

## Anachora Interaction

Anachora recognizes and can torment the Vrakhir corruption in Dawnwalker Coen. It cannot claim his entire soul because most of that soul exists beyond its world and its metaphysical jurisdiction. This is resistance, not immunity: blood hunger and corruption remain real, and Anachora can attack the connected portion until later worlds provide a way to sever or defeat that bond.

## Narrative Guardrails

- Modern canon memory is incomplete and becomes unreliable after divergences.
- New Coens enter because their world and personality add a real problem, cost, or capability, not merely another power set.
- Ciri's Elder Blood may eventually let incarnations meet physically, but it does not create the collective and should not reduce her to transportation.
