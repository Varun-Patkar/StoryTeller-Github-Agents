# Plot Structure

## Story Structure

**Model:** Sequential world arcs with controlled interludes. Each world has its own local conflict and character arc while synchronization events advance the larger evolution story. Early chapters stay primarily with Vale Sangora Coen; other Coens enter when their awakenings matter.
