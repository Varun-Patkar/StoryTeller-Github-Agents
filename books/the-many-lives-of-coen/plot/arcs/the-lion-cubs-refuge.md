# The Lion Cub's Refuge

## Setup

Thirteen-year-old Coen is a street orphan and Ciri's trusted escape from palace life. Nilfgaard's sack turns their familiar city into a trap.

## Escalation

Synchronization awakens Coen's modern memories, traces of Dawnwalker hunger, and latent Chaos talent. He can improve Ciri's odds, but every intervention changes the route remembered from the books.

## Long-Term Direction

Coen grows beside Ciri rather than replacing Geralt or Yennefer. Their childhood trust develops into the sole romance after both are old enough. His clearest future intervention is preventing the isolation and abuse that led Ciri to the Rats.
