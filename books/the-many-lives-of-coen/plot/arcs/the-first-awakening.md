# The First Awakening

## Setup

A modern man dies in his sleep and his soul tears apart between worlds. Years later, the Vale Sangora fragment warns Coen that Alin is about to fall through a frozen river. Coen saves him, and the extreme fear awakens his memories without granting an interface or contact with other incarnations.

## Escalation

With his family intact and Esme still active, Coen spends the remaining years learning witchcraft from Anca, conditioning his body, and preparing for Brencis. He tries to prevent the coming disaster, but Brencis eventually takes the family despite those preparations.

Do not add extra childhood chapters or delay the first collective connection beyond Chapter 8 unless the user explicitly changes this schedule.

## Central Turn

Brencis turns Coen. The failed transformation creates the Dawnwalker and establishes the first connection across the scattered soul. On the night Cintra falls, a second Coen answers.

Unlike the game's endings, Coen does not plan to storm Greifberg Castle. Lunka's letters show that she remains herself during captivity and can report on its defences. Coen intends to turn that intelligence, Dawnwalker stealth, and years of witchcraft preparation into a quiet infiltration, family extraction, and assassination before Brencis can turn Lunka in retaliation.

## Promises

- Modern memory helps but never functions as a perfect walkthrough.
- Anachora can hurt and corrupt Coen but cannot absorb the complete multiversal soul.
- Vale Sangora Coen begins as Council leader through experience and strength, not metaphysical ownership.
