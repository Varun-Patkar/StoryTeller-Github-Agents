# Canon and Crossover Ledger

## Source Policy

Use primary or licensed sources first, then established reference wikis and post-release guides for quest details. Treat unsupported summaries as leads, not authority. Record fanfiction changes as explicit divergences.

## The Blood of Dawnwalker

### Confirmed Spine

- Official site: 14th-century Europe; Coen is human by day and vampire by night; his goal is saving his family.
- Official and publisher material: Vale Sangora is a Carpathian region shaped by plague and vampire rule; quests differ between day and night.
- Post-release game references: the opening includes Laslea, Esme's treatment, the Blood Mass, Pieter in the silver mine, Brencis, and Coen's Dawnwalker transformation.
- Post-release lore references: Anachora is a separate realm tied to Vrakhiri death and corruption. Defeated Vrakhiri souls can be invited into Coen and retain voices without displacing him.
- Witchcraft uses persistent foundational scars as a conduit or gate. Individual hexes are learned through manuals, instruction, and ancient glyph sites, then prepared through that conduit rather than requiring a separate full scar pattern for each hex. Casting consumes health or blood. Visible marks expose practitioners to religious persecution and therefore require concealment.
- Latin is not established as the language of hexes. Uriashi sacred stones, glyphs, a Uriashi witchcraft vendor, and the Uriashi term "skin-signs" tie important magical knowledge to their traditions. Sources do not confirm one universal magical language or a canon name for it; keep the story's terminology at "glyphs" unless stronger evidence appears.
- Canon family detail: Alin is Coen's younger brother and died at a frozen river. The exact presentation is partly recovered through a later memory sequence.
- Coen's in-game glossary names Alin's death as the first loss in Esme's withdrawal, followed by plague and the Vrakhiri takeover. Her physical decline follows her refusal to eat, sleep, speak, or get out of bed; it is not established as a pre-existing bodily weakness.
- Mirto and Lunka are the two youngest siblings. In this story's childhood chronology they are too young to understand or retain a clear memory of the river event.
- Lunka catches the Black Plague and Brencis cures her with vampire blood. She remains herself during captivity and sends replies about Pieter, Brencis, and Greifberg Castle's defences through the Letters to Lunka quest.
- Post-release ending accounts agree that Brencis turns Lunka and that her fate is unchanged across the standard finale routes. The turning occurs off-screen and is revealed after the confrontation.

### Fanfiction Divergences

- Coen's dormant fragment warns him before the ice breaks, allowing him to prevent Alin's canon death. The extreme fear restores his modern-life memories years before the game prologue.
- With Alin alive, Esme does not enter the withdrawal and self-neglect that made her weak in canon. She remains an active parent even though plague and occupation still traumatize her.
- Coen begins learning from Anca earlier and spends the intervening years preparing for Brencis.
- Coen reads Lunka's continued correspondence as proof that Brencis has not yet turned her during most of the countdown. He rejects the game's loud castle-assault routes and plans a stealth infiltration that kills Brencis before the vampire can use Lunka as a final act of spite. This causal prevention is a fanfiction inference, not an explicit canon route.
- Coen's distributed soul prevents Anachora from owning or absorbing his complete being.
- The Dawnwalker transformation initiates a cross-world collective.

## The Witcher

### Continuity Choice

Book canon governs the saga through *The Lady of the Lake*. CD Projekt Red's games may continue from the books later. Netflix-only events and magic rules do not apply unless added explicitly.

### Confirmed Spine

- Cintra falls during the First Nilfgaardian War after defeat at Marnadal. Calanthe dies and Ciri escapes the slaughter.
- Ciri later reaches Yurga's household and reunites with Geralt in the events surrounding "Something More."
- Ciri is a Source and carrier of Elder Blood. Her later ability to cross worlds and time is distinct from ordinary trained sorcery.
- Book magic is drawn and shaped through training; uncontrolled Sources require guidance. Do not import Netflix's one-spell-one-sacrifice visual rules into book continuity.

### Fanfiction Divergences

- A thirteen-year-old orphan named Coen is one of Ciri's established childhood friends.
- Coen awakens during the sack and remains in Ciri's orbit.
- Synchronization catalyzes Coen's own latent Chaos potential; it does not give him Elder Blood.
- Coen will act to prevent Ciri's later abuse and time with the Rats while preserving her choices and central role.
