# Laslea

## Overview

Laslea is a small hamlet in northern Vale Sangora, close to a lake, wooded
slopes, abandoned workings, and active silver mines. Its families survive
through mining, farming, herding, household crafts, and mutual obligation.
Distance from Svartrau offers no real protection once Brencis decides to make an
example of the settlement.

## Physical Description

Timber-and-wattle homes cluster along muddy paths beneath the Carpathian slopes.
Smoke hangs low in cold weather. Pieter's household has a yard for splitting
wood and practicing chores; Martin's later forge adds a steady hammer note and
an evening glow. The nearby lake freezes in winter but remains treacherous where
springs and currents thin the ice.

## History

Silver shaped Laslea before Brencis outlawed it. The village elder Zdislav
preserved stories of pale blood-drinkers, Uriashi, likhos, ancient ruins, and
warlocks long before the Vrakhiri openly seized Vale Sangora.

In the original game history, Alin drowned in the lake, Esme withdrew into
grief, and Vladimir's planned retaliation helped bring Brencis's wrath upon the
hamlet. This story diverges when Coen prevents Alin's fall.

## Culture and Daily Life

Children contribute as soon as they can carry water, mind younger siblings,
sort fuel, weed, or tend animals. Skill is respected when it feeds a household.
Privacy is scarce, so sustained secret training requires ordinary explanations
and useful chores around it.

Villagers know the mountains contain dangerous things but discuss them through
warnings, saints, and old stories rather than open scholarship.

## Notable Features

- **Pieter and Esme's home:** Coen's family center and first place he changes.
- **Martin's forge:** Henry's home, workplace, and a source of tools and training
  weapons.
- **The lake:** Site of Alin's canonical death and the first decisive divergence.
- **Silver workings:** Livelihood, source of Coen and Henry's poisoning, and the
  material that later interrupts both transformations.
- **Road to Anca's hut:** Link between village life, herbs, Latin, and forbidden
  witchcraft.

## Current State

At the opening, Laslea is poor but functioning under human rule. Brencis's later
takeover, the silver ban, plague, Blood Mass, and peasant resistance remain
future pressures rather than everyday facts.
