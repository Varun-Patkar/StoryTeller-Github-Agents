# Blood Between Worlds

## Synopsis

A modern gamer dies and awakens as the infant Coen of Laslea, carrying complete knowledge of The Blood of Dawnwalker and a system modeled on its mechanics. By saving Alin and growing beside Henry of Skalitz, another child displaced by a Conjunction, he changes the fate of two families before both young men become Dawnwalkers and challenge Brencis's rule.

## Tone & Style

Accessible webnovel prose with a grounded medieval texture, direct emotional
reactions, and precise system messages. Family life and earned safety should be
allowed to feel warm. Horror comes from plague, Anachora, blood magic, and the
cost of vampiric power rather than constant suspicion between allies.

Coen is the primary viewpoint. Henry may receive occasional viewpoint chapters
when his witchcraft path or displaced-family experience reveals something Coen
cannot witness. Knowledge of game events gives Coen preparation and options,
not automatic mastery or perfect certainty after the timeline changes.

## Notes

- Planning only. Do not draft chapters until the canon ledger and opening arc
  are approved through use, not through revealing a scene-by-scene outline.
- Canon research and AU boundaries are recorded in
  [research/canon-and-crossover-ledger.md](research/canon-and-crossover-ledger.md).
- The story preserves The Blood of Dawnwalker's day/night duality, silver-based
  failed transformation, blood-rune witchcraft, Anachora risk, and major sequel
  hooks while changing Coen's childhood and giving him a co-protagonist.
- The game does not have one route that combines every favorable outcome. This
  story will build a coherent best-outcome synthesis and show the extra work
  required to earn it.
