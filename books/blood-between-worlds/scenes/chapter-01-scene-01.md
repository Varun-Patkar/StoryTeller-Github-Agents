# A Life Already Lost

## Purpose

Establish Coen's reincarnation, emotional investment in his new family, the
system's strict limits, and the first concrete tragedy he intends to prevent.

## Continuity Notes

- Coen recognizes Pieter and Esme from game memories immediately after birth.
- Esme and Pieter do not know Coen retains an adult mind or sees an interface.
- The system cannot identify the source of reincarnation.
- Coen begins with zero skill points, Corruption, and Infamy.
- The objective `A Brother Still Living` has no system reward.
- Alin has not been born by the end of the chapter.
