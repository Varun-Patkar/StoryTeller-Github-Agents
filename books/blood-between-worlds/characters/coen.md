# Coen

## Appearance

Born into Pieter and Esme's family in Laslea, Coen begins as a sturdy,
dark-haired infant. As he grows, mine work leaves him lean, strong through the
back and shoulders, and marked by a persistent silver cough. After his failed
turning, his mismatched eyes and night form make concealment difficult.

## Personality & Traits

Coen is practical, curious, dutiful, and intensely attentive to danger. His
adult memories make him patient with repetitive work but impatient with tragedies
he believes he can prevent. He loves his new family as people rather than quest
objectives, though he sometimes slips into measuring problems as routes, checks,
and resources.

He enjoys training and visible progress. He dislikes helpless waiting, wastes
little, and automatically counts exits, tools, and people in a room. When plans
break, his first instinct is to protect everyone himself.

## Backstory

In his first life, Coen was a modern gamer who loved grounded medieval games and
dark fantasy, especially *Kingdom Come: Deliverance*, *The Witcher*, and *The
Blood of Dawnwalker*. He completed every route and ending in Dawnwalker but
remained troubled by the suffering no ending could repair.

He died peacefully in his sleep and woke with an adult mind in the body of the
newborn Coen. A restrained version of the game's interface appeared with him.
It records growth and recognizes earned abilities, but does not supply equipment,
money, prophecy, or effortless competence.

## Motivations & Goals

Coen wants to save Alin, preserve Esme, prepare his family for Brencis, and
create an outcome better than any route he played. He needs to accept that
people are not fixed NPCs and that changing one event removes the certainty on
which his plans depend.

His deepest conflict is between protecting his humanity and welcoming the power
of his future night form because it lets him protect more people.

## Location References

- **Laslea:** birthplace, home, and the family he refuses to treat as expendable.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Coen speaks plainly and asks precise questions. As a child, he deliberately
simplifies his vocabulary but occasionally sounds too old when frightened or
focused. He does not make speeches when a short answer will do.

> "Again. Slower this time. I want to know where my foot went wrong."

> "I don't know that it will happen. I know what happens if we do nothing."

> "Power isn't clean. Neither is leaving people to die."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A frightened adult mind treats canon knowledge as a plan
  that can prevent every loss.
- **Key turning points:** Alin's survival breaks the known timeline; Henry's
  arrival proves other worlds are involved; Pieter turns secret exercise into
  real training; Anca teaches that every spell opens a risk; becoming a
  Dawnwalker makes power personal rather than theoretical.
- **Ending state:** Coen protects his family without trying to own their choices
  and accepts the Augurs' invitation to confront threats beyond Vale Sangora.
