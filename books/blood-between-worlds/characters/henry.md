# Henry

## Appearance

Henry arrives in Laslea as a small, brown-haired boy with lively eyes and hands
already accustomed to charcoal, bellows, and fetching tools. He grows broad and
strong from forge work, mine labor, and training with Coen. Nicks and small burns
collect on his hands before Anca's deliberate rune scars join them.

## Personality & Traits

Henry is sociable, stubborn, quick to laugh, and quicker than Coen to trust a
person who treats him fairly. He learns by doing and asks blunt questions when
abstract explanations lose him. He is less disciplined at the start but hates
being left behind, which makes him return to difficult practice on his own.

He likes well-made tools, food after hard work, and proving that a supposedly
ordinary skill can solve an extraordinary problem. He complains openly but
rarely quits.

## Backstory

Henry is the young son of Martin and his wife from Skalitz. A Conjunction-like
rupture takes the family from their world before the catastrophe that would
have defined Henry's original life. They emerge in the Carpathians and settle
near Laslea when Coen is about three, relying on Martin's smithing to earn a
place in the community.

Henry remembers fragments of the crossing but initially accepts his parents'
claim that it was a terrible journey. He does not possess Coen's modern memories
or system knowledge. His strengths come from work, instruction, judgment, and a
particular aptitude for patterns in runes and crafted objects.

## Motivations & Goals

Henry wants to become capable enough that his family will never need to flee
helplessly again. He also wants Laslea to become a chosen home rather than a
temporary refuge.

He needs to recognize that competence does not require taking every burden onto
himself. Witchcraft tempts him because preparation can make the impossible
manageable, but its costs can turn self-sacrifice into self-destruction.

## Location References

- **Laslea:** adopted home and site of Martin's forge.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Henry is informal, direct, and emotionally readable. He teases Coen's solemnity,
uses workshop comparisons, and admits confusion without embarrassment before
trying again.

> "You can call it balance. I call it falling more slowly."

> "A bad blade and a bad spell have the same cure. Stop pretending it will hold."

> "This is my home too. You don't get to save it without me."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A displaced child depends on his parents and treats Laslea
  as another place they may have to leave.
- **Key turning points:** Friendship with Coen gives him roots; Martin and Pieter
  turn play into practical craft and combat; Anca recognizes his aptitude;
  silver exposure and Brencis's tooth make him a second Dawnwalker.
- **Ending state:** Henry claims both his lost origin and chosen home, using
  witchcraft without allowing Anachora to define what he is.
