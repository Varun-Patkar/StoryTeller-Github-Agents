# Alin

## Appearance

Alin is a lively younger boy who grows fast enough to mistake new height for
invulnerability. His winter clothes are frequently damp at the cuffs from going
closer to streams, drifts, and lake edges than instructed.

## Personality & Traits

Affectionate, adventurous, competitive, and initially convinced Coen worries
too much. He wants inclusion more than safety and copies his elder brother's
stances without understanding the discipline behind them.

## Backstory

In canon, Alin fell through lake ice and drowned before Pieter could reach him.
In this story, Coen recognizes the first crack and moves immediately, pulling
Alin away before the ice opens.

## Motivations & Goals

Alin wants to prove he is not the little brother everyone must protect. After
the lake, he needs to live as more than Coen's rescued person or evidence that
foreknowledge works.

## Location References

- **Laslea:** birthplace, playground, and community.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Alin speaks quickly, protests restrictions, and asks questions that expose the
parts of Coen's explanations built from fear rather than logic.

> "If you knew it would break, why did you let us come here?"

> "I can carry it. I didn't say I could carry it far."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A loved younger brother unknowingly approaching his fixed
  canonical death.
- **Key turning points:** Coen saves him; he learns the rescue was not luck; he
  insists on contributing to the family's preparations.
- **Ending state:** Alin becomes a capable young man with goals not defined by
  the tragedy he escaped.
