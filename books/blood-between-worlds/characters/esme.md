# Esme

## Appearance

Esme has strong working hands, dark hair usually bound out of her face, and an
expression that changes quickly when one of her children is hiding something.
At the opening she is tired from childbirth but alert, affectionate, and fully
engaged with her household.

## Personality & Traits

Warm, capable, sharp-eyed, and unwilling to let solemn men make every decision
in silence. Esme uses humor to puncture unnecessary drama and anger when humor
would excuse real danger. She remembers each child's preferences and notices
changes in appetite or sleep before anyone else.

## Backstory

Esme built a family with Pieter after his sellsword years. Canon reduced much of
her later life to grief after Alin's drowning, followed by plague and depression.
This story meets her before that loss and allows her choices to shape the home.

## Motivations & Goals

She wants her children alive, decent, and able to enjoy more than survival. She
needs to understand why Coen prepares for dangers no child should anticipate,
without allowing fear of those dangers to consume the family early.

## Location References

- **Laslea:** the home and network of neighbors she actively maintains.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Esme is direct and expressive. She asks the second question when someone avoids
the first and uses names pointedly when patience runs out.

> "You answered what you wanted me to ask. Try the question I did ask."

> "The roof can fall tomorrow. Today, you still finish your soup."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A busy mother concerned with ordinary scarcity and joy.
- **Key turning points:** Coen's unusual vigilance becomes undeniable; Alin
  survives the lake; plague and Brencis threaten the family by other means.
- **Ending state:** Esme remains an active family decision-maker rather than a
  symbol of what Coen failed to save.
