# Pieter

## Appearance

Pieter is bald, broad through the chest, and worn by mine work. Old scars and
the economical way he moves reveal more martial experience than his neighbors
usually discuss. He keeps his clothes plain and his tools in good order.

## Personality & Traits

Reserved, observant, and practical. Pieter shows love through food, repairs,
lessons, and standing between danger and his household. He dislikes discussing
his years as a sellsword and does not confuse harshness with instruction.

## Backstory

Pieter once fought as a sellsword and led armed men. He earned enough to settle
with Esme in Laslea, then worked the silver mines to support their growing
family. He intended his sword years to remain behind him.

## Motivations & Goals

He wants his children fed and free of the violence he knew. He needs to accept
that refusing to prepare them will not keep Vale Sangora's violence away.

## Location References

- **Laslea:** home, mine, and the peaceful life he purchased at lasting cost.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Pieter uses short instructions and dry observations. Praise is specific and
rare enough to matter.

> "Your hands know the motion. Your feet don't. Again."

> "A sword is work. Anyone who tells you otherwise has not cleaned one."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** A veteran hides his former life to protect his family.
- **Key turning points:** He discovers Coen's secret practice, recognizes real
  fear behind it, and resumes teaching; Brencis proves preparation necessary.
- **Ending state:** Pieter trusts his children as capable partners without
  surrendering his place as their father.
