# Canon and Crossover Ledger

## Research Scope

This ledger separates established Dawnwalker canon, launch-guide reporting, and
the story's alternate-universe decisions. It was prepared before any chapter
prose was drafted.

Primary fan reference reviewed:

- <https://dawnwalker.fandom.com/wiki/Dawnwalker_Wiki>
- All content pages listed by the wiki's `Special:AllPages` index as of
  2026-09-14, including Coen, Brencis, the Court, Anca, Lacra, Dawnwalkers,
  Vrakhiri, Sorcery, Anachora, Isbrand, Kallias, Laslea, Svartrau, Vale Sangora,

## Canon Baseline

### Setting and History

- The game takes place in 1347 in Vale Sangora, an isolated, silver-rich valley
  in the Carpathian Mountains. Narrow gorges make access difficult and seasonal.
- Svartrau is the valley's wealthy, multilingual capital. Greifberg Castle and
  the cathedral dominate it. Silver drew miners, traders, and mercenaries from
  across Europe and Central Asia.
- Knyaz Skender Dragosti was a cruel human ruler who spent mining wealth on
  repression. Brencis later usurped him and outlawed silver as contraband.
- The Black Death reaches the valley immediately before the game's crisis.
  Brencis's blood can cure disease, which helps him legitimize Vrakhiri rule.
- Laslea is a small mining hamlet. Its oral history remembers prehuman ruins,
  pale blood-drinkers, likhos, Uriashi, warlocks, and other supernatural beings.
- Saint Mihai was secretly a Dawnwalker. After fighting the Anachora-corrupted
  Vrakhiri Kallias, Mihai was betrayed and bound in silver by Tobias, who then
  rewrote the story to make both men Christian saints.

### Coen and His Family

- Coen is Pieter and Esme's eldest child. The current wiki material names or
  identifies Yanna, Mirto, Alin, and Lunka as his younger siblings.
- Pieter is a retired sellsword and former leader of armed men. Canon already
  establishes that he eventually teaches Coen swordsmanship.
- Coen works in Laslea's silver mines while still a child. Prolonged silver-dust
  exposure causes argyria and a persistent cough.
- Coen is intelligent and curious, dreams of leaving the valley, and stays
  because his family depends on him.
- Canon Alin falls through lake ice. Coen runs for Pieter, but they return too
  late. Coen's guilt persists into the game.
- Alin's death devastates Esme. Plague and Vrakhiri rule worsen her condition
  until she barely eats, speaks, or leaves bed.
- In the prologue, Anca gives Coen herbs for Esme. Hot water and exactly three
  spoonfuls are required. An incorrect preparation can lead to Esme's death at
  the Blood Mass.
- Pieter speaks with Vladimir about peasant retaliation. After the uprising and
  prologue disaster, Brencis destroys Laslea and takes Coen's family.

### Dawnwalkers and Vrakhiri

- A Vrakhiri creates another vampire by extracting one of its own teeth and
  embedding it in the victim's heart. A tooth placed elsewhere creates a failed,
  feral Murohn.
- Coen's silver poisoning prevents a complete turning. He becomes human by day
  and vampiric by night: a Dawnwalker.
- Dawnwalkers are not unique to Coen. Confirmed examples include Saint Mihai and
  Neberu. Heterochromia may be a marker, but the precise conditions remain
  uncertain.
- Dawnwalkers age very slowly but remain mortal. Their known abilities include
  claws and fangs, superhuman strength, Shadowstep, wolf transformation, and
  powers learned through feeding or Vrakhiri blood.
- Dawnwalkers are vulnerable to silver. At sunrise, sunlight hurts briefly
  before they revert to human form.
- Vrakhiri are ageless and immortal rather than invincible. Silver and sunlight
  can destroy them. Their fangs continue growing throughout their existence.
- Vrakhiri blood heals disease and wounds but can cause addiction and madness.
- A Dawnwalker is not both forms at full strength simultaneously. Day and night
  should change available powers, vulnerabilities, tactics, and identity.

### Sorcery and Anachora

- Vale Sangora knows witchcraft and Uriashi shamanism. Both appear to use marks
  or "skin-signs," though their traditions and effects differ.
- Witchcraft is blood magic. A caster carves a rune into their skin and draws
  their own blood to activate it.
- Known effects include fire, astral communion, compelling the dead, raven
  attacks, speed and resilience enhancements, life drain, and influence.
- Witchcraft is forbidden and socially dangerous. Practitioners may be exiled,
  imprisoned, or executed.
- Anachora is a dark realm tied to vampirism and blood magic. Dead Vrakhiri and
  related corrupted beings are drawn there and consumed into a collective by a
  feared entity.
- Red vines, red moths, visions, whispers, and corrupted creatures can cross or
  reflect the boundary. Blood magic invites something of Anachora into the soul.
- Full Vrakhiri regeneration prevents lasting carved runes. A Dawnwalker's human
  day form offers a plausible opening for witchcraft, but the exact interaction
  is not settled canon and must carry risk.

## Major Character Baselines

### Coen

Curious, dutiful, and constrained by poverty and family obligation. Vampirism
terrifies him but also offers freedom. The reincarnated Coen keeps those family
attachments; foreknowledge should not turn him into an emotionally detached
speedrunner.

### Pieter

Retired sellsword, miner, emotionally reserved father, and loving husband. He
has the skill and background to recognize disciplined practice and become Coen's
real instructor once the child's secrecy stops being useful.

### Esme

Canon's broken state is consequence, not her whole personality. Saving Alin
allows this story to develop the mother she was before grief and plague erased
her daily life. Her response to a strangely driven son should remain active.

### Anca

Ex-nun, herbalist, Coen's friend, and Latin teacher. Canon Coen learns blood-rune
witchcraft from her. Teenage apprenticeship is therefore an expansion of an
existing relationship, not a replacement for it.

### Brencis

Born Caeso Burrienus Laurentius in AD 131 to a wealthy Roman family. He became a
senator and sought the consulship before Isbrand turned him. Loss of status and
his wife Helvia hardened, rather than erased, his hunger for power.

Brencis rejects the old law that Vrakhiri must hide. He studies Anachora despite
Isbrand's warnings, seizes Vale Sangora, uses plague relief and terror to rule,
and maintains a Court of three vassals.

### Ambrus

The youngest Court vampire: once a humiliated, tortured mercenary, now charming,
ambitious, theatrical, and eager to prove himself. His scars predate turning and
therefore do not heal.

### Bakir

A Central Asian raider who loved bloodshed before becoming Vrakhiri. He wants
recognition and terror, resents centuries of hiding, and is the Court's most
openly destructive member.

### Xanthe

An ancient former priestess from a forgotten Greek island. She hears voices
from beyond, understands more of the world's hidden nature than the others, and
is deeply afraid. Her blood-whip power was decisive in Isbrand's defeat.

### Lacra

Brencis's spawn and an agent of the Augurs. Once an abused Egyptian wife, she
embraces feeding as a practical necessity without sharing the Court's sadism.
She knows Vrakhiri, Dawnwalker, and Anachora lore. Isbrand punished Brencis's
forbidden research by having him tear out Lacra's fangs and exiling her.

### Crake and the Manumits

Crake leads the resistance route used to restore Catalin Dragosti. The route can
defeat Brencis, but Catalin's resulting government is harsher than an unqualified
"best ending." The story must not equate anti-vampire with automatically good.

### Neberu and the Augurs

Neberu is a Dawnwalker who appears in the epilogue with an offer from the
Augurs. Lacra already serves this influential group. Their invitation is the
cleanest canonical bridge from the Vale Sangora story into wider adventures.

### Lunka

Coen's sister is turned into a Vrakhiri in every full ending reported by the
launch guides. Coen hides her in a cart when he leaves with Neberu. Her turning
must be consciously preserved, prevented, or reassigned; silently forgetting it
would remove the principal sequel hook.

## Game Progression Spine

1. Prologue quests establish Esme's medicine, Pieter and Vladimir's planned
   resistance, the Blood Mass, Laslea's destruction, Coen's failed turning, and
   the abduction of his family.
2. Coen receives roughly thirty in-game days to save them. Time advances through
   selected activities rather than every minute of exploration.
3. The open campaign raises Infamy by disrupting the Court and defeating
   Ambrus, Bakir, and Xanthe.
4. Anca's early `Smoke and Ashes` material unlocks foundational witchcraft.
   Lacra and Crake then provide the two major ally-driven routes.
5. Maximum Infamy triggers Brencis's `A Deal with the Devil` and the endgame.
6. Brencis's final defeat extends into Anachora and his Grotesque Ascendant form.
7. The Laslea epilogue varies with side choices, but full endings retain Lunka's
   turning, Neberu's invitation, and Coen's departure.
8. The post-credits scene jumps to the modern era. An older, injured Coen drinks
   a delivered blood bag while an unknown vampire and Anachora corruption remain
   active, explicitly leaving the world open for sequels.

## Ending Map

- **The Turncoat:** Coen betrays the Manumits or an ally to Brencis. His family
  can survive, but Brencis and the regime remain.
- **The Folk Hero:** Coen defeats Brencis without Lacra or Crake as final ally.
- **The Patricide:** Coen completes Lacra's chain and defeats her sire with her.
- **The Knyazmaker:** Coen and the Manumits defeat Brencis and install Catalin
  Dragosti. Crake and Catalin can survive, but Catalin rules harshly.
- **A Hero They Deserve:** Coen leaves Vale Sangora and abandons his family.
- **Together Forever:** Coen remains willingly trapped in Xanthe's false family
  dream during `Home Sweet Home`.
- **Time Runs Out:** Brencis kills the family after the deadline; Coen's goal
  changes from rescue to revenge.
- **Prologue victory:** A mechanically possible but untracked early Brencis kill
  that functions as a shortened Folk Hero outcome.

No route is a universal perfect ending. Side decisions independently alter
Esme, Anca, Lacra, Crake, Father Florin, the Uriashi, Svartrau, and the plague.

## Crossover Decisions

### Reincarnated Coen

- He dies peacefully in the modern world after exploring all game routes and
  awakens as infant Coen, not as a separate body replacing an adult.
- The system uses recognizable game categories: attributes, skills, XP, skill
  points, manuals, recipes, day/night powers, Corruption, Infamy, Focus, and
  tracked conditions.
- It provides measurement and unlocks, not a shop full of unrelated powers,
  omniscient quest arrows, or immunity to consequences.
- Foreknowledge degrades as soon as Alin lives, Esme remains active, and Henry's
  family enters Laslea. Coen knows likely pressures and hidden locations, not a
  fixed future.

### Childhood Divergence

- Coen begins safe physical conditioning at five, helps with household labor,
  and practices with age-appropriate wooden weapons.
- Pieter discovers the training and teaches fundamentals in his free time. This
  accelerates a canon father-son practice rather than inventing expertise.
- At the lake, Coen reacts to the first crack and tackles or pulls Alin away
  before the ice gives way. Alin lives, and Esme does not collapse from grief.
- Saving Alin does not remove plague, poverty, silver exposure, Brencis, or the
  family's reluctance to abandon their home and livelihood.

### Henry of Skalitz

- Martin, his wife, and young Henry arrive near Laslea when Coen is about three.
  They crossed worlds through a Conjunction-like event.
- Henry is the Kingdom Come version adapted at a younger age. Martin remains a
  blacksmith and father in daily life; secrets about Henry's birth should not be
  imported unless they serve this story.
- Coen overhears enough to infer a Conjunction of the Spheres. He cannot prove
  that theory immediately.
- Henry trains beside Coen, works around the mines and forge, and develops a
  stronger affinity for witchcraft and practical crafting.

### Geralt and Yennefer

- Ciri sends them through a controlled portal after Toussaint as a vacation.
- Their arrival works best after Coen and Henry have meaningful local training,
  preferably near or after their transformations. Earlier instruction would
  trivialize Pieter and Anca.
- Geralt can refine monster fighting and Dawnwalker sword tactics; he should not
  replace Pieter as Coen's formative teacher.
- Yennefer can diagnose and correct local blood magic while treating Anachora as
  a dangerous external source. She should not make its costs disappear.
- They may shelter the families outside Vale Sangora during the conflict, but
  extraction must be earned around Brencis's surveillance and the sealed valley.

### Dual Dawnwalkers

- Coen and Henry both acquire heavy silver exposure through mine work before
  Brencis attempts to turn them by placing Vrakhiri teeth in their hearts.
- Their transformations require a concrete reason for Brencis or a Court member
  to target both. An accidental loose fang is not sufficient under canon rules.
- Coen trends toward vampiric abilities and the temptation of direct power.
- Henry trends toward blood-rune witchcraft, preparation, alchemy, and crafted
  tools. He still receives a dangerous night form and cannot remain the morally
  uncomplicated "human" half of the pair.
- Two protagonists expand the number of simultaneous interventions but do not
  erase the deadline, political aftermath, plague, or Anachora.

## Best-Outcome Synthesis

The target route combines compatible favorable choices without claiming the
game normally permits all of them:

- Save Alin in childhood and prepare Esme's medicine correctly if the Blood Mass
  still occurs.
- Keep both families alive and remove them from Brencis's immediate reach.
- Complete Anca's, Lacra's, and Manumit material where their goals can coexist.
- Defeat Ambrus, Bakir, and Xanthe while preserving information and possible
  nonlethal outcomes only when supported by their actions and the situation.
- Refuse Brencis's betrayal bargain.
- Defeat Brencis with a coalition rather than handing Vale Sangora to Catalin by
  default.
- Give humans, Uriashi, surviving Vrakhiri, and local communities a negotiated
  transition while addressing the returning plague.
- Preserve the Augur invitation as the bridge to the extended story.

## Open Canon Decisions

- Decide whether Lunka is still turned. Preventing it gives the family a cleaner
  victory but removes a powerful reason for Coen to accept the Augurs' help.
- Identify which Vrakhiri tooth turns Henry and why the turning attempt occurs.
- Decide whether Coen remembers exact system UI values or only routes and lore;
  exact recall must not produce effortless mastery after divergence.
- Define how daytime rune scars behave at sunset when Dawnwalker regeneration
  activates. This is AU mechanics and should be consistent from first use.
- Choose whether Geralt and Yennefer arrive shortly before the Blood Mass, during
  the thirty-day campaign, or after Brencis falls. The current preference is
  during the campaign, late enough not to solve the childhood story.
- Decide whether the Conjunction that brought Henry's family and Ciri's later
  portal share a cause or are separate events.
