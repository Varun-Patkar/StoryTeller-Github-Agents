# A Childhood Rewritten

## Setup

A modern gamer awakens as newborn Coen with memories of every tragedy awaiting
the family holding him. He has years before Brencis's crisis but almost no power,
language, or control over his own body.

## Rising Action

1. Coen learns the system measures real effort and cannot skip childhood.
2. Martin's displaced family settles nearby, giving Coen a friend and evidence
   that the Conjunction of Spheres is more than fiction.
3. Coen begins useful chores and safe training at five; Pieter discovers him and
   replaces imitation with real fundamentals.
4. Coen's growing competence cannot persuade either family to abandon its home
   on the word of a child.
5. The remembered winter arrives, and Coen must distinguish the fatal moment
   from all the ordinary days around it.

## Climax

Coen hears the lake ice crack and acts before panic can repeat canon, driving
Alin back toward solid ice and taking the physical consequences himself.

## Resolution

Alin lives. Esme remains herself, and the family's future changes immediately.
Coen gains proof that fate can be altered but loses confidence that later game
events will occur in a predictable order.
