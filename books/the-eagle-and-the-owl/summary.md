# Story Summary: The Eagle and the Owl

## Arc 0: The Player (Prologue)
- **Ch1 — The Last Experiment.** Theron (modern billionaire-engineer, surname Ambrose, dead at 51 running a solo experiment on an Isu sliver) wakes drowning as an ~8-year-old on a Kephallonia beach. The System opens: 1.0% Isu ancestry, Eagle Vision flickers once then reads dormant, and a stat panel names him Theron (INT far above the child's other attributes). He recognizes the island but cannot place the year, privately chooses "visions" as cover if he slips, and follows a goatherd's spoken directions to Sami. There he helps Markos expose a merchant's leaking jar; Markos takes the shipwrecked boy home to his vineyard, disguising kindness as a practical arrangement where "everybody benefits."
