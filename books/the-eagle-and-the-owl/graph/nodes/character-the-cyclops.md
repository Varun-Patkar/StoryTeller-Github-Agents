# Character: The Cyclops

> id: character-the-cyclops | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A one-eyed Kephallonian brute and moneylender to whom Markos is deep in debt.

## Canon
His debts and thugs pressure Markos and push the misthios into mercenary work. Humiliated when his prized obsidian eye is stolen (stuffed in a goat), then killed by Kassandra.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: blustering, thin-skinned, hates the word "Cyclops."

## AU Divergence
Set up as a childhood threat (Ch3), then paid off after the time-skip: in Ch6 the adult pair steal his obsidian eye (the goat gag, as in canon) and kill him, freeing Barnabas in the process. That public spectacle is how Elpenor notices them and offers the "Wolf of Sparta" contract — the onramp to the Cult.

## Notes
Comic early-antagonist; his death is the hinge from the Kephallonia childhood into the Cult plot.
