# Event: Saving Phoibe

> id: event-saving-phoibe | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
In Athens, Theron uses exact foreknowledge of Phoibe's canonical death to get her clear of the Cult before the Odeon.

## Canon
Diverges from Odyssey, where Phoibe is murdered during the plague.

## AU Divergence
She lives — the first big, unmistakable proof that this is a save-everyone story. Emotional high point of the Athens arc.

## Notes
Tonal pivot; flagship fix-it victory.
