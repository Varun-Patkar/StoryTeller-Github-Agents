# Character: Brasidas

> id: character-brasidas | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A principled Spartan general who fights the Cult of Kosmos from within Sparta's structures, preferring justice to vengeance.

## Canon
Ally in Korinth, Arkadia, and the Cult hunt; dies at Amphipolis in canon.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: measured, honourable, dryly warm; the model Spartan.

## AU Divergence
Theron aims to keep him alive at Amphipolis (part of "no meaningless deaths") using foreknowledge to break the ambush that kills him in canon.

## Notes
Save-target; embodiment of the balance theme.
