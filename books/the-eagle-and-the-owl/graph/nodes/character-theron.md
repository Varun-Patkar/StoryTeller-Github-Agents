# Character: Theron

> id: character-theron | type: character | canonicity: original

## Overview
Theron was a technology prodigy whose progressive mitochondrial disease confined him to hospitals from age twelve. At sixteen he released Lantern, an open diagnostic system for rare diseases. He died on his eighteenth birthday and woke in the body of a drowned six-year-old native boy on Kephallonia. The body has modest Isu ancestry. Theron becomes Kassandra's strategist, partner, lover, and immortal husband.

## Canon
Original character. His foreknowledge covers the broad plots of games from his first universe and general history, with gaps in detail and declining reliability after divergence.

## Voice & Mannerisms

### Baseline
- Dry, observant, precise, and occasionally too certain of his own models.
- Treats problems as systems and prefers preparation, traps, stealth, and technology.
- Learns the local register so his modern habits do not expose him.
- Physically lighter and weaker than Kassandra, though he trains into an elite assassin.
- Loves games, learning, travel, and the ordinary experiences his first life denied him.

### Voice Evolution
- Prologue: curious and difficult as a healthy child, then increasingly patient and inward during six years of illness. Uses work and dry humor to contain fear.
- Chapter 1 baseline: an eighteen-year-old mind wakes in a six-year-old body with no native personal memories. He is frightened, physically disoriented, and focused on immediate survival.

## AU Divergence
Theron arrives shortly after seven-year-old Kassandra reaches Kephallonia. His passive status panel only measures demonstrated attributes and skills. It grants no quests, rewards, inventory, or advancement. His native body's Isu blood supports Eagle Vision and interfaces but not Kassandra's raw strength.

Theron hides reincarnation and foreknowledge until Aletheia is contained. He then tells Kassandra that his first universe observed fragments of hers as stories and offers the multiverse as his best explanation. He never treats her as fictional.

## Notes
The mind of the pair. Kassandra is the stronger direct fighter and sometimes saves him when instinct succeeds where his technology or prediction fails. His long arc moves from fearing too little time to learning how to live well with almost unlimited time.
