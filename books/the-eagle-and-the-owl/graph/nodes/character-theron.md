# Character: Theron

> id: character-theron | type: character | canonicity: original

## Overview
Theron is a modern billionaire inventor (Tony-Stark archetype: electrical genius, coder, engineer) with a photographic memory and a lifelong love of the Assassin's Creed games. His modern name/company was Ambrose; he died at 51 running a solo experiment on an unearthed Isu sliver. He "learns his lesson," and is reincarnated as an 8-year-old boy who washes up on Kephallonia one year before Kassandra. He becomes her pillar, partner, lover, and finally immortal husband.

## Canon
Original character — no canon. He is inserted into the canon Odyssey timeline at Kephallonia and treats his game-knowledge as literal foreknowledge.

## Voice & Mannerisms

### Baseline (source-grounded to the modern-genius register, not the Greek cast)
- Register: dry, quick, modern-minded but learns to speak like a Kephallonian so he blends in. Engineer's precision; jokes to deflect.
- Tics: reframes problems as systems; narrates trade-offs to himself; understates danger. Never grandiose out loud (that is the Cult's tell).
- What he never does: reveal that this is a game. He calls his knowledge "visions." That secret goes to his grave.
