# Concept: Isu DNA

> id: concept-isu-dna | type: concept | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The triple-helix Isu genetics that grant the sixth sense and compatibility with Pieces of Eden.

## Canon
Kassandra and Alexios carry strong Isu DNA as descendants of Leonidas.

## AU Divergence
The substance Theron's System literally infuses and levels; the biological basis of Hybrid Ascension and the genetic-memory lock.

## Notes
The scientific heart of the power system.
