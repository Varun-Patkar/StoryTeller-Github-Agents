# Location: Kephallonia

> id: location-kephallonia | type: location | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The Ionian island where Markos raises Kassandra and Theron; setting of the opening arc and Odyssey's start.

## Canon
Kassandra's childhood home after Taygetos; the Cyclops rules the local underworld.

## AU Divergence
The divergence point: Theron washes up here at 8, a year before Kassandra (7). Their whole partnership begins here.

## Notes
Contains the Isu cache Theron unearths (see location: The Old Quarry Cache).
