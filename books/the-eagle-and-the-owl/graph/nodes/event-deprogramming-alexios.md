# Event: Deprogramming Alexios

> id: event-deprogramming-alexios | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
Rather than a fragile talking-down, Theron reverse-engineers Isu memory technology (Sage/Animus-adjacent principles) to physically undo the trauma-conditioning that made Alexios into Deimos.

## Canon
Diverges from Odyssey's fragile, choice-dependent redemption.

## AU Divergence
Alexios is genuinely restored and returned to the family. The cure is the proof-of-concept that launches Theron's full science pivot and the memory-tech toolkit reused later.

## Notes
Turns a tragedy into a rescue; seeds later tech.
