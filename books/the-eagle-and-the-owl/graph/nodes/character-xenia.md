# Character: Xenia

> id: character-xenia | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The imperious pirate queen of Keos who trades information and treasure maps for coin.

## Canon
Sends Kassandra on treasure hunts; once sailed with Myrrine.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: grand, greedy, amused; treats everyone as a supplicant.

## AU Divergence
Her treasure hunts become Theron's income engine — orichalcum and relics to feed the System.

## Notes
Links treasure-hunting to the System economy.
