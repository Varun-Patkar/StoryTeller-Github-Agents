# Character: Leonidas

> id: character-leonidas | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
King of Sparta and hero of Thermopylae; grandfather of Myrrine and great ancestor of Kassandra and Alexios. Original wielder of the Spear of Leonidas.

## Canon
Died at Thermopylae; his broken spear passes down the bloodline as an Isu artifact.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: plain, fatalistic, warm beneath the iron.
- Source Quotes: "I would have liked to have gone fishing with my son." / "Who rules the Greek world right now? ... Then my answer is yes."

## AU Divergence
Appears in flashback/vision framing; his legacy grounds Kassandra's identity.

## Notes
Bloodline anchor; appears via the Spear's memories.
