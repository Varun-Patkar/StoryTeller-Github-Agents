# Location: Mount Taygetos

> id: location-mount-taygetos | type: location | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The Spartan mountain where the sickly or condemned were cast down; the site of the family's founding trauma.

## Canon
Kassandra was thrown here as a child; infant Alexios was taken by the Cult afterward.

## AU Divergence
The wound that Theron's fix-it campaign ultimately heals.

## Notes
Emotional origin point; recurs in flashback.
