# Character: Darius

> id: character-darius | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The mythic assassin who first used a hidden blade to kill Xerxes, now an aging fugitive fleeing the Order of the Ancients through Greece.

## Canon
Central to the Legacy of the First Blade DLC alongside his son Natakas; canonically Kassandra romances Natakas and bears Elpidios.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: weary, lethal, haunted; speaks little, means all of it.

## AU Divergence
The Natakas romance and the child Elpidios are CUT — Kassandra loves Theron. Darius is reframed as an optional ally and mentor whose first hidden blade fascinates the tech-minded Theron; the Order of the Ancients remains a shared threat.

## Notes
Bridges Odyssey to the deep Assassin/Hidden Ones lore without the canon romance.
