# Item: Glaux

> id: item-glaux | type: item | canonicity: original

## Overview
A mechanical owl Theron builds from Isu-cache components — a clockwork-and-Isu-tech scout and companion, the deliberate counterpart to Kassandra's eagle Ikaros.

## Canon
Invented (owl of Athena imagery); an original mirror of Ikaros.

## AU Divergence
Serves the same recon role Ikaros does for Kassandra, but for Theron. Phoibe carves two little statues — Ikaros and Glaux — cementing the eagle-and-owl motif of the title.

## Notes
Title motif made literal; symbol of the pair (body + mind, eagle + owl).
