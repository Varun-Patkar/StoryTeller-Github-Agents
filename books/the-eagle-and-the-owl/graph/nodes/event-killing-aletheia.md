# Event: Killing Aletheia

> id: event-killing-aletheia | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
By chapter 30, Theron and Kassandra confront and kill the Isu Aletheia, taking over the Atlantis cache and its knowledge.

## Canon
A major divergence — canonically Aletheia guides the Keeper rather than dying.

## AU Divergence
Removes an Isu hand on the scales, hands the pair the deepest Isu tech (immortality mechanism, genetic lock), and clears the road to found the Eclipse and travel the world.

## Notes
First-act climax and the hinge into the post-30 story.
