# Character: Kleon

> id: character-kleon | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The populist firebrand who seizes Athens after Perikles, secretly a Cult Sage.

## Canon
Rules Athens post-Perikles; killed at Amphipolis.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: loud, populist, sneering; a demagogue who flatters the mob.
- Source Quote: "I will make Athens great again!"

## AU Divergence
Theron exposes and out-maneuvers him earlier, folding his defeat into the family-reunion climax.

## Notes
Political face of the Cult.
