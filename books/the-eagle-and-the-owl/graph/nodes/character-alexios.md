# Character: Alexios (Deimos)

> id: character-alexios | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Kassandra's younger half-brother, taken by the Cult of Kosmos as an infant after Mount Taygetos and tortured into their living weapon, "Deimos." Carries strong Isu DNA.

## Canon
Believed dead after the mountain, secretly raised and indoctrinated by the Cult. As Deimos he wields the Sword of Damokles (a Piece of Eden). Canonically he murders Perikles during the Plague of Athens and kills for the Cult; redemption is fragile and choice-dependent.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register (as Deimos): cold, grandiose, wounded; oscillates between arrogance and buried confusion.
- Source Quotes: "[I'm] a demigod. Worshipped and feared by the Cult and all the Greek world." / "Have you not heard of Deimos? The Demigod of Terror... I am he."
- Post-cure (our AU): warmer, halting, relearning how to be a brother.

## AU Divergence
He is genuinely saved: Theron reverse-engineers Isu memory technology to actually undo the Cult's conditioning (see event: Deprogramming Alexios). Restored to himself and the family in Ch28.

**Redeemability rule (hard constraint):** because he must be redeemed, Deimos NEVER kills a major character. No major-character blood is ever on his hands. Any canon Deimos-kill is reassigned to the plague, other Cultists, or fate (e.g., Perikles dies of the plague/Cult, not by Deimos). He can menace, wound mooks, and clash with the leads, but never lands a killing blow on someone who matters.

## Notes
Flagship save-target; his cure seeds the memory-rewriting toolkit used later. Keep his hands clean of major deaths so the Ch28 redemption stays earned.
