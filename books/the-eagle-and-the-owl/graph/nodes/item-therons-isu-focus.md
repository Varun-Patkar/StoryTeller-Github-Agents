# Item: Theron's Isu Focus

> id: item-therons-isu-focus | type: item | canonicity: original

## Overview
The Isu-tech artifact Theron recovers from the Old Quarry Cache — a programmable focus/gauntlet that interfaces with Isu systems and Pieces of Eden. His strategist counterpart to Kassandra's Spear.

## Canon
Invented, consistent with Isu-tech.

## AU Divergence
Grows with his Isu-blood level: from a simple scanner and Eagle-Vision amplifier to a device that can manipulate Pieces of Eden and Isu machinery. The tool that makes the "techno-mage" real.

## Notes
Parallels the Spear; central to the tech spine and later the owl and genetic lock.
