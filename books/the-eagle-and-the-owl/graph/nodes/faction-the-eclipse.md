# Faction: The Eclipse

> id: faction-the-eclipse | type: faction | canonicity: original

## Overview
The secret order Theron founds — the true Hidden Ones standing above both Assassins and Templars, working from the background to keep the world in balance and, eternally, to stop the recurring solar catastrophe (the sun death).

## Canon
Invented for this story.

## AU Divergence
Creed: "No truth is absolute, no order eternal." They hold the line between chaos and order — too much chaos breeds conflict (but chaos drives progress); too much order breeds stagnation (but order sustains civilization). Founded around chapter 30 after Atlantis; the org's work is the engine of the post-30 story.

## Notes
Payoff of the whole first act; echoes Kassandra's canon line "Order, Chaos... if either triumphs alone, the world dies."
