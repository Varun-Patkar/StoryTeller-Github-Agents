# Thread: Theron's Ascension

> id: thread-therons-ascension | type: thread | canonicity: original

## Overview
The measurable power-fantasy spine, in two braided strands:
1. **Isu-blood climb** (visible in System panels): the percentage rising from 1% toward 50%, sharpening Eagle Vision and every attribute.
2. **The Focus evolving** from a scanner and Eagle-Vision amplifier into a device that manipulates Pieces of Eden and Isu machinery.

Running under both from Chapter 1 is his **human engineering** \u2014 Theron is a modern inventor first. Before the cache he improvises tools, traps, and clever mechanisms (Ch3-4); at the cache he builds Glaux the mechanical owl by hand (Ch5); aboard the Adrestia he turns the ship into a workshop and testbed (Arc 2 on). His ingenuity is present in every arc, not just his blood level.

## Notes
Keep progress concrete and earned. Show the maker at work early (Arc 1-2), well before the later Science Pivot makes research his main engine. Pair grind with invention.
