# Location: The Old Quarry Cache

> id: location-the-old-quarry-cache | type: location | canonicity: original

## Overview
A collapsed limestone quarry north of Sami hiding a small Isu cache. Theron, guided by his "visions," digs it out during the childhood arc.

## Canon
Invented, but consistent with Odyssey's many Ancient/Isu ruins.

## AU Divergence
Yields Theron's first Isu-tech artifact (parallel to the Spear), raw orichalcum, and the components he later builds into the mechanical owl, Glaux. First proof his foreknowledge pays.

## Notes
Payoff of the childhood arc; seeds the tech spine.
