# Concept: The Creed

> id: concept-the-creed | type: concept | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The Assassin doctrine — "Nothing is true, everything is permitted" — a philosophy of free will.

## Canon
The ideological core of the Brotherhood, opposed to Templar control.

## AU Divergence
One pole of the balance the Eclipse holds; their own creed ("No truth is absolute, no order eternal") answers both Assassin and Templar mottos.

## Notes
Philosophical throughline into the Eclipse.
