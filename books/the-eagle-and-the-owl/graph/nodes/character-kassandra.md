# Character: Kassandra

> id: character-kassandra | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Born 458 BCE, a Spartan-descended mercenary (misthios) and the Eagle Bearer. Granddaughter of Leonidas; wields the Spear of Leonidas, an Isu artifact. Strong Isu DNA. In this story she is the second protagonist and Theron's partner and love, and through the Staff of Hermes she becomes immortal.

## Canon
Daughter of Myrrine and Pythagoras, half-sister of Alexios/Deimos. Thrown from Mount Taygetos as a child, raised by the merchant Markos on Kephallonia. Rises as a misthios, dismantles the Cult of Kosmos, becomes the "Keeper" guided by Aletheia, and gains longevity from the Staff of Hermes. Canonically dies 2018 on Thera after passing on the Staff.

## Voice & Mannerisms

### Baseline (source-grounded)
- Register: terse, blunt, wry, dark humor. Greek profanity ("malaka", "malakes"). Never flowery. Soldier's tenderness hidden under sarcasm.
- Tics: deadpan comebacks; threats that are grimly funny; weary of other people's nonsense.

## AU Divergence
Raised beside Theron as the two orphans Markos takes in; they grow up partners then lovers (fast, not slow burn). Her canonical romance with Natakas and son Elpidios are CUT entirely — she loves Theron. Her family is healed, not just avenged (Alexios cured, Nikolaos/Stentor reconciled, Phoibe lives). Becomes immortal via the Staff, but not alone: Theron becomes immortal too.

## Notes
