# Item: Staff of Hermes Trismegistus

> id: item-staff-of-hermes-trismegistus | type: item | canonicity: canon

## Overview
An Isu staff (a Piece of Eden) that grants its keeper agelessness.

## Canon
Passes from Hermes to Pythagoras to Kassandra, who becomes the immortal Keeper.

## AU Divergence
Grants Kassandra immortality as in canon; Theron reverse-engineers its mechanism to bless himself too, making them the Twin Immortals.

## Notes
Engine of the immortality thread and the whole eternal-couple premise.
