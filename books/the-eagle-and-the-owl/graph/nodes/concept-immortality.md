# Concept: Immortality

> id: concept-immortality | type: concept | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The ageless life conferred by the Staff of Hermes.

## Canon
Kassandra lives 2,400+ years as the Keeper before passing the Staff.

## AU Divergence
Theron reverse-engineers the mechanism so BOTH become immortal — but as a couple who keep their curiosity and spark, not the jaded "seen everything" trope. Immortality = vast accumulated knowledge and power, still hungry to learn.

## Notes
Defines the whole eternal-couple premise and the tone of the far future.
