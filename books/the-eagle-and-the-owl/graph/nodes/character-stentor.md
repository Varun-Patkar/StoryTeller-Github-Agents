# Character: Stentor

> id: character-stentor | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A rigid, loyal Spartan officer, adopted son of Nikolaos and Kassandra's step-brother, initially hostile.

## Canon
Antagonistic through Megaris and Boeotia; reconciles once Nikolaos reveals he lives.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: barking, proud, brittle under the armour; contemptuous of mercenaries.

## AU Divergence
Reconciliation smoothed by Theron's mediation; joins the found-family reunion.

## Notes
Family-reunion payoff character.
