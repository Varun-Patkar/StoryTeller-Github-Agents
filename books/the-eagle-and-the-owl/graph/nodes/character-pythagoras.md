# Character: Pythagoras

> id: character-pythagoras | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The philosopher-sage, Kassandra's true father, and long-lived keeper of the Staff of Hermes before her.

## Canon
Kept Atlantis sealed and lived centuries via the Staff; passes the burden to Kassandra.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: grave, cosmic, distant; speaks of cycles and duty over feeling.

## AU Divergence
Theron's foreknowledge changes how and when the Staff and Atlantis are handled; Pythagoras's long stewardship becomes data for reverse-engineering immortality.

## Notes
Link to the Staff, Atlantis, and the immortality thread.
