# Faction: Assassin Brotherhood

> id: faction-assassin-brotherhood | type: faction | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The order of the Creed, pursuing free will and freedom.

## Canon
Descends from Bayek and Aya's Hidden Ones; the eternal foil to the Templars.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse (Theron's order) secretly sits above both Assassins and Templars, steering them toward balance.

## Notes
One pole of the balance the Eclipse manages.
