# Character: Barnabas

> id: character-barnabas | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The warm, superstitious captain of the ship Adrestia, rescued by Kassandra and devoted to her ever after.

## Canon
Kassandra's ship captain and friend throughout Odyssey.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: booming, pious, story-loving; reads omens into everything.
- Source Quote: "Salvage! There is salvage nearby!" (his beloved recurring call).

## AU Divergence
Crew to the Theron/Kassandra partnership; his tall tales are often accidentally true, which Theron finds useful. The Adrestia becomes a testbed for Theron's Isu-tech upgrades.

## Notes
Enables travel across the Greek world.
