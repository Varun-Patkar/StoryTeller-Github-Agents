# Character: Aletheia

> id: character-aletheia | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
An Isu Dikastes of Atlantis; Loki's lover and the guide who instructs the wielders of the Staff of Hermes.

## Canon
Speaks to Kassandra across Fate of Atlantis, steering the Keeper; an Isu voice with her own agenda.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: cool, ancient, riddling; talks in purpose and inevitability.

## AU Divergence
Theron treats her as a threat from the start. The pair kill Aletheia and seize the Atlantis cache by chapter 30 (see event: Killing Aletheia).

## Notes
Primary Isu antagonist of the first act; her death is a major canon divergence.
