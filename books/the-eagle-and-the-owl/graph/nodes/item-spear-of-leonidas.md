# Item: Spear of Leonidas

> id: item-spear-of-leonidas | type: item | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The broken spear of King Leonidas, secretly an Isu artifact that channels its bearer's Isu abilities.

## Canon
Kassandra's signature weapon and the key to her powers and the Ancient Forges.

## AU Divergence
Explicitly framed as the berserker/physical counterpart to Theron's strategist tech-artifact from the cache — the eagle to his owl.

## Notes
Anchor of Kassandra's power and identity.
