# Character: Aspasia

> id: character-aspasia | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Perikles's sharp, charismatic partner who mentors Phoibe and aids Kassandra — and is secretly the Ghost of Kosmos, founder and hidden head of the Cult of Kosmos.

## Canon
Revealed as the Ghost after the Cult is dismantled; she believed the Cult could bring order but lost control of it. Kassandra chooses to kill or spare her.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: silken, philosophical, always three moves ahead; frames control as mercy. (Pull verbatim lines at the reveal chapter; do NOT invent grandiose one-liners.)

## AU Divergence
Theron knows her identity from the start and plays a long game — using her to dismantle the Cult from within while protecting Phoibe, and confronting the Ghost reveal on his terms rather than as a shock.

## Notes
The Cult's true head; her fate is a deliberate choice, not a twist.
