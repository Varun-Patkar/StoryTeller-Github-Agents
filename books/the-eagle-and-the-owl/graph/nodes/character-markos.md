# Character: Markos

> id: character-markos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A well-meaning, scheming, perpetually-indebted merchant of Kephallonia. Buys vineyards with borrowed money, over-promises, and somehow keeps his little family fed. Took in Kassandra after she washed ashore and (in this story) Theron too.

## Canon
Kassandra's early employer and father-figure on Kephallonia; his debts to the Cyclops kick off her mercenary career.
