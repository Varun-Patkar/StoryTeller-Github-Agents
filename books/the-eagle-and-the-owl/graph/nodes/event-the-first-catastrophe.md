# Event: The First Catastrophe

> id: event-the-first-catastrophe | type: event | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The ~75,000 BCE solar flare (the Great Catastrophe) that nearly annihilated Isu and humans alike.

## Canon
The founding disaster of the setting; drove the Isu to build warnings and vaults.

## AU Divergence
The precedent for the recurring threat the Eclipse exists to stop.

## Notes
Origin of the sun-death goal.
