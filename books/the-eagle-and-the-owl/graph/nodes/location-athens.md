# Location: Athens

> id: location-athens | type: location | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The intellectual and political heart of Greece; site of Perikles's symposium and the devastating plague.

## Canon
Where Phoibe dies and Perikles is assassinated during the Plague of Athens.

## AU Divergence
Theron's flagship save-window: Phoibe lives, and the plague night pivots toward Alexios's rescue.

## Notes
Densest save-everyone arc.
