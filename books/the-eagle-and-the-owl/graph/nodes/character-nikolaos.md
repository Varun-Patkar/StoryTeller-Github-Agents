# Character: Nikolaos

> id: character-nikolaos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
"The Wolf of Sparta." Kassandra's step-father, who raised her before Spartan law and the Cult's manipulation forced him to drop her from Mount Taygetos.

## Canon
Kassandra confronts and (canonically) spares him in Megaris; he later aids Stentor from the shadows and seeks to repair his family.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: gruff, formal, weighed down by guilt and Spartan duty; speaks in the fatalistic Spartan idiom.
- Source lines to be pulled at the Megaris chapter's voice gate.

## AU Divergence
His redemption is deepened; he survives to the family reunion.

## Notes
Central to healing Kassandra's betrayal wound.
