# Character: Hippokrates

> id: character-hippokrates | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The father of medicine, met in Argolis and again during the Plague of Athens.

## Canon
Aids Kassandra in finding Myrrine and fighting the plague.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: rational, humane, precise; trusts observation over the gods.

## AU Divergence
Theron feeds him real (modern) medical insight disguised as observation — germ-adjacent hygiene, quarantine — saving lives and helping engineer Phoibe's survival.

## Notes
First real outlet for Theron's science pivot.
