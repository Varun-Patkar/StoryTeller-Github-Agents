# Character: Myrrine

> id: character-myrrine | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Daughter of King Leonidas of Sparta; mother of Kassandra and Alexios.

## Canon
Reunited with Kassandra during the Peloponnesian War arc; a strong political figure.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: composed, commanding, grief carried with dignity.
- Source Quote: "Sometimes the face we show to the world needs to be one of strength, despite the chaos we harbor beneath."

## AU Divergence
Reunion accelerated by Theron's foreknowledge; the whole family is healed rather than merely found.

## Notes
Emotional keystone of the family-reunion arc.
