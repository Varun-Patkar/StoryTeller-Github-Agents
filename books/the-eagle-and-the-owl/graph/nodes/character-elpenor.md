# Character: Elpenor

> id: character-elpenor | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A disguised Cult of Kosmos agent who manipulates Kassandra into her first big contract and opens the door to the Cult conspiracy.

## Canon
Hires Kassandra to kill Nikolaos, then tries to have her kill more family; assassinated at Delphi.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: oily, superior, evasive.
- Source Quote: "The Cult of Kosmos is everywhere. They are the air we breathe, the shadows we walk in."

## AU Divergence
Theron recognises the setup immediately and plays along to harvest Cult intelligence early, accelerating the campaign.

## Notes
Onramp to the Cult arc.
