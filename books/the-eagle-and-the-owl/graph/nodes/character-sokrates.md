# Character: Sokrates

> id: character-sokrates | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Athens' gadfly philosopher, forever asking uncomfortable questions about right action.

## Canon
Recurring conscience-figure in Athens.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: probing, ironic, relentless; answers a question with a question.
- Source Quotes: "Ask anyone and they'll tell you Sokrates, but a name gives you nothing. Our actions determine who we are, and every action has its pleasures and its price." / "But is it just, what you did? Or merely effective?"

## AU Divergence
Theron enjoys sparring with him — a modern mind against a classical one — and quietly feeds Sokrates's own ideas back at him.

## Notes
Thematic foil for the saving-everyone ethic.
