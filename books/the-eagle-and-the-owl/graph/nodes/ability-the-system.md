# Ability: The System

> id: ability-the-system | type: ability | canonicity: original

## Overview
Theron's game-like power. Shown to the reader as LITERAL panels: stat screens, attribute points, level-up notifications. Attributes rise through combat, grind, AND scientific research (research grants Intelligence, etc.). There is NO quest system.

## Canon
Invented (LitRPG framing over the AC world).

## AU Divergence
Its founding gift is a 1% Isu-blood infusion (see ability: Hybrid Ascension). It is the diegetic reason Theron can grow from ordinary genius to Isu-hybrid superhuman. Panels are visible to Theron only; never to the classical cast.

## Notes
Reader-facing rule: show panels. Keep them punchy, not spammy.
