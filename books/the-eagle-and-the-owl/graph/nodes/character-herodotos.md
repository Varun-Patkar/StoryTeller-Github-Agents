# Character: Herodotos

> id: character-herodotos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The famed historian, fascinated by the Isu relics threaded through Greek myth. Introduces Kassandra to the Spear's true nature and the Ancient Forges.

## Canon
Companion and lore-guide throughout Odyssey.

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: curious, verbose, delighted by mysteries; the eternal note-taker.

## AU Divergence
Becomes Theron's favourite foil: Theron already knows the answers, so he uses Herodotos to launder foreknowledge into "reasoned deductions" without exposing the System.

## Notes
Cover for Theron's meta-knowledge; Isu-lore conduit.
