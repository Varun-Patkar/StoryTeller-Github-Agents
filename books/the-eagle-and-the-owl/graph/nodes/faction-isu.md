# Faction: Isu

> id: faction-isu | type: faction | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The First Civilization that engineered humanity and left the Pieces of Eden behind.

## Canon
Created humans via Project Anthropos; nearly wiped out with them in the First Catastrophe. Their survivors and machines shape all later history.

## AU Divergence
Theron reveals the Isu truth to Kassandra as "visions." The pair's eternal goal is to survive/prevent the recurring solar catastrophe.

## Notes
Root of the metaplot and the sun-death goal.
