# Concept: Genetic Memory Lock

> id: concept-genetic-memory-lock | type: concept | canonicity: original

## Overview
Theron's invention (from the Atlantis cache): a mechanism that locks genes after a period so ancestral/genetic memories can no longer be peeked at through the Animus.

## Canon
Invented, but built on canon Isu-DNA / genetic-memory rules.

## AU Divergence
Protects the immortal pair and their secrets from future Animus-users. A quiet, clever safeguard that also explains, in-universe, why their story stays hidden from the modern-day frame.

## Notes
Meta-clever payoff of the science pivot; ties to the goal of stopping the catastrophe without Desmond's death.
