# Ability: Hybrid Ascension

> id: ability-hybrid-ascension | type: ability | canonicity: original

## Overview
The System's central climb. Theron starts at 1% Isu blood (enough to awaken Eagle Vision) and can raise it, through growth, to a maximum of 50% — a perfect hybrid with the best of both species. Each increment is a comprehensive boost to all attributes.

## Canon
Invented, grounded in canon Isu-DNA rules.

## AU Divergence
The measurable spine of the power fantasy: readers track the percentage climbing. At high levels it unlocks manipulation of Pieces of Eden and Isu machinery via his Focus.

## Notes
Keep the percentage a concrete, earned progress bar.
