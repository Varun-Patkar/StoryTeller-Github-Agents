# Ability: Eagle Vision

> id: ability-eagle-vision | type: ability | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The Isu sixth sense: perceiving intent, threats, and hidden things beyond normal sight.

## Canon
An inheritance of strong Isu DNA (Kassandra has it; it becomes Eagle Vision in later eras).

## AU Divergence
Awakened in Theron by his first 1% infusion; sharpens as his blood level climbs and pairs with his Focus.

## Notes
Shared power that links the two leads.
