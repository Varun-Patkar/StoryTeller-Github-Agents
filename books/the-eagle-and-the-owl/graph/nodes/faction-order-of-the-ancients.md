# Faction: Order of the Ancients

> id: faction-order-of-the-ancients | type: faction | canonicity: canon

## Overview
An ancient order pursuing order and control from the shadows; ancestor of the Templars.

## Canon
Hunt Darius; later (in Origins) kill Bayek's son, sparking the Hidden Ones.

## AU Divergence
A shared threat the pair help fight; a bridge toward the post-30 Origins span and the Eclipse's balance mission.

## Notes
Proto-Templar throughline.
