# Faction: Cult of Kosmos

> id: faction-cult-of-kosmos | type: faction | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A pervasive shadow network pulling strings across the Greek world, seeking control through war and fear.

## Canon
Kassandra dismantles the Cult across Odyssey; its true head is the Ghost of Kosmos (Aspasia). A cousin to the Order of the Ancients.

## AU Divergence
Theron's foreknowledge accelerates the campaign and turns the Ghost reveal into a planned move.

## Notes
Primary human antagonist faction of the first 30 chapters.
