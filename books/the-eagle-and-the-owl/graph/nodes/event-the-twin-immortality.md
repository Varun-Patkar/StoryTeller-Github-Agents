# Event: The Twin Immortality

> id: event-the-twin-immortality | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
The Staff of Hermes grants Kassandra immortality (as in canon); Theron then reverse-engineers its mechanism with cache tech to make himself immortal too.

## Canon
Diverges from Kassandra's lonely 2,400-year vigil — she is not alone.

## AU Divergence
Founds the eternal-couple premise: two immortals who keep their curiosity and spark across the ages, carrying the Eclipse's mission forward.

## Notes
The payoff the whole first act builds toward.
