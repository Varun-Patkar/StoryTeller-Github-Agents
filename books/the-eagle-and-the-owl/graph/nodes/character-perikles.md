# Character: Perikles

> id: character-perikles | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Athens' foremost statesman, host of the famous symposium, and a Cult target during the plague.

## Canon
Canonically murdered by Deimos/Alexios during the Plague of Athens (~429 BCE).

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Register: statesmanlike, patient, tired; measured rhetoric.

## AU Divergence
Perikles still dies (his fate is historically fixed), but NOT by Deimos's hand — Alexios must stay redeemable. In this story he is taken by the plague and the Cult's wider machinery. Theron knows Perikles is doomed and heavily guarded and cannot save both him and Phoibe on the same night; he spends his foreknowledge on Phoibe, and the loss of Perikles pivots the night toward reaching Alexios.

## Notes
Introduces the Athens political cast. His death must never be pinned on Deimos.
