# Character: Phoibe

> id: character-phoibe | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A quick, fearless orphan girl who idolizes Kassandra. Part of the Kephallonia family; later runs errands for Aspasia in Athens.

## Canon
In Odyssey, Phoibe is murdered by the Cult in Athens during the plague — one of the story's gut-punch deaths.

## Voice & Mannerisms

### Baseline (source-grounded)
- Register: chirpy, blunt, kid-slang, brave, wise beyond her years. Hero-worships the misthios.

### Source Quotes (fandom quest transcripts)
- "Why do you let him boss you around?"
- "I'm just a kid. And I've done pretty good myself."
- "No one's gonna buy me from you. They're all poor." (defiant while kidnapped)

## AU Divergence
Phoibe does NOT die. Theron knows the exact time and place of her canonical death and engineers her survival. She lives into the found family. In this story she carves two little statues — Ikaros (Kassandra's eagle) and Glaux (Theron's mechanical owl).

## Notes
Her survival is the tonal pivot that tells readers this is a fix-it story.
