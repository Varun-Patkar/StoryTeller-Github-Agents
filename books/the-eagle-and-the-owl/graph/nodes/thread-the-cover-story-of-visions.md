# Thread: The Cover Story of Visions

> id: thread-the-cover-story-of-visions | type: thread | canonicity: original

## Overview
Every piece of Theron's foreknowledge is explained (if ever) as prophetic "visions." He will eventually reveal the Isu truth, the origins, and the coming catastrophe this way, but NEVER that their world is a game. That truth would shatter Kassandra's sense of reality and free will; he carries it to the grave.

## Timing (important)
- Ch1: Theron privately decides on the "visions" cover. He tells NO ONE.
- Arcs 1-7: Kassandra's suspicion builds slowly (he always knows too much, names Cultists he shouldn't, averts fixed deaths). She does not force the question; he does not volunteer.
- Arc 8, Ch29: Kassandra finally confronts him and demands the truth. He admits the "visions" and reveals the Isu/human origin and the sun-death through them. This is the payoff, not an early confession.
- Never: the fact that it is a game.

## Notes
Dramatic-irony engine; source of Theron's quiet loneliness even in an immortal marriage. Track her suspicion per arc (see plan Canon Checkpoints) so the Ch29 admission is earned.
