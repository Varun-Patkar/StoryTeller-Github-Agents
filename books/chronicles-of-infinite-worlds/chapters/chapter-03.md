# A Place Until Morning

## Outline

1. Alaric travels toward Boston with Joel and Tess while hiding the system and learning how they work together.
2. Joel checks Alaric's injury, and Tess questions what he can contribute besides shooting.
3. Alaric notices signs that the missing bandits returned with allies and warns the group before an ambush.
4. Joel, Tess, and Alaric fight together; Alaric completes Road Hunter I through careful shooting from cover.
5. The system grants exact rewards, and Alaric uses the Basic Close Combat skill book.
6. The group reaches a smuggler safehouse, where Joel and Tess offer Alaric a temporary trial instead of sending him away.

**Arc beats advanced:** Joel and Tess bring Alaric toward Boston, and his competence wins him a temporary place.
**Foreshadowing planted:** Alaric understands weapon maintenance; Tess is already thinking about jobs suited to three people.
**Foreshadowing paid off:** Road Hunter I reaches 10/10 and grants its listed rewards.

## Chapter Text

Joel walked behind Alaric for the first twenty minutes.

He did not say much. He did not need to. Alaric could feel the man watching every step he took.

Tess led them along the edge of the broken road. She stayed close to the trees and avoided patches of loose glass. Alaric copied her path.

His side hurt whenever he took a deep breath. The bullet had only grazed him, but the bandage pulled against the wound as he walked.

**HP: 83/100**  
**Stamina: 39/100**

He closed the display before Joel could notice his eyes moving across empty air.

"You're limping," Joel said.

Alaric tried to walk normally. "I'm fine."

"No, you're pretending to be fine."

Tess glanced back. "He learned that from you."

"I don't limp."

"You complain instead."

Joel ignored her and looked at Alaric. "What happened to your side?"

"One of the men near the ditch had a hidden pistol."

"And you let him use it?"

"I told him I wouldn't shoot if he surrendered."

Joel's expression became even less friendly. "That was stupid."

"I know that now."

"You should've known it before."

Tess stopped beside an overturned van. "Joel."

"What?"

"He's bleeding through the bandage."

Alaric looked down. A small red patch had formed on his shirt.

Joel held out one hand. "Let me see."

Alaric hesitated.

"If I wanted you dead, I would've left you at the station."

That was reasonable. Alaric lifted his shirt.

Joel removed the bandage without being gentle. The wound had stopped bleeding, but walking had pulled part of it open again.

"Graze," Joel said. "Looks clean."

"I used disinfectant."

"You know how to dress a wound?"

"I read a lot."

Joel gave him a look that said reading was not the same as doing.

Alaric agreed, but his bandage had held for most of the night.

Tess took gauze and tape from her pack. Joel cleaned the wound again and wrapped it tighter.

"This will hurt when you move," Joel said.

"It already hurts."

"Then you'll know it's working."

Tess shook her head. "Very comforting."

Joel pulled Alaric's shirt down. "Keep pressure on it if it starts bleeding again."

"Thanks."

Joel stood. "Don't make me do it twice."

They started walking again.

This time Joel moved ahead with Tess. Alaric followed several steps behind them.

He smiled when they could not see him.

Joel had insulted him through the entire treatment, but he had also used clean gauze from his own pack. Alaric understood that kind of care. It was easier to trust than a kind speech.

The sky began to lighten through the trees.

They left the road and followed a narrow trail between old houses. Most had collapsed roofs or trees growing through them. One still had a child's yellow bicycle lying in the front yard.

Alaric looked away from it.

"How far is Boston?" he asked.

"Far enough," Joel said.

Tess gave him a real answer. "We aren't going straight to the zone. We have a delivery first, then a place to rest."

"Who is the delivery for?"

"Someone who paid."

"What's in it?"

"Something they paid for."

Alaric looked between them. "You two are very open with strangers."

Tess smiled. "You'll get used to it."

"He isn't staying long enough to get used to it," Joel said.

"We haven't decided that."

"I decided."

"Good for you."

They spoke like this without slowing down. Joel complained. Tess ignored him. Neither needed to ask where the other would step or which side of the trail they would watch.

Alaric had seen their partnership in the game. Watching it from a few feet away was different. They trusted each other with small things constantly.

Tess stopped near a low stone wall and raised her fist.

Everyone froze.

She pointed toward a house ahead.

Its front door stood open. A strip of blue cloth hung from the handle.

Joel moved beside her. "Wasn't there before."

"No."

Alaric searched the windows. He saw no movement.

"What does it mean?" he whispered.

"Nothing good," Tess said.

Joel drew his revolver. "We go around."

They backed away from the trail and entered the trees.

Alaric kept watching the ground. Mud had collected beneath the leaves after recent rain. Several boot prints crossed their new path.

He crouched.

Tess noticed. "What is it?"

"Tracks."

Joel looked down. "Old."

"Some are. These aren't."

Alaric pointed to a print with sharp edges. Water had not gathered inside it yet. Another print lay beside it, then a third.

"At least four people," he said. "They came from the road behind us and moved ahead through the trees."

Joel studied the marks again.

"Six," he said. "Two are walking in each other's tracks."

Alaric followed the line and saw what he meant. "Right."

Tess looked back toward the road. "The two who left the camp?"

"They might have found friends," Alaric said.

Joel looked at him. "Did you leave anyone alive?"

"Not at the camp. Two left before I got there."

"And they know this route."

"Probably."

Joel's jaw tightened.

Alaric waited for him to say something about bringing trouble with him.

Instead, Joel asked, "What would you do?"

The question surprised him.

Alaric looked around. The trail passed between the open house and a deep drainage channel. Thick brush covered the far side. The people ahead expected them to avoid the marked door and enter the trees.

"They're pushing us toward the channel," Alaric said. "The house is bait. The ambush should be ahead, where the brush gets thicker."

Tess nodded. "Makes sense."

"We can go farther east and avoid them," Joel said.

"They may follow," Alaric replied. "And your delivery has a deadline, doesn't it?"

Neither answered. That was an answer by itself.

Alaric pointed toward the second floor of the open house. "The roof has a clear view of the channel. If they think we're avoiding the house, they won't watch it closely."

Joel looked at the open door. "Could be infected inside."

"I have a mask."

"A mask doesn't stop teeth."

"I'll check before I go upstairs. If the house is clear, I can watch the channel. Tess can lead them into view."

Tess raised an eyebrow. "You already want me to play bait?"

"You suggested it last night."

She looked pleased that he had listened.

Joel did not. "He's been with us half an hour."

"And he found the tracks," Tess said.

Joel stared at Alaric. "You miss a shot, Tess gets hit."

"I know."

"You freeze, Tess gets hit."

"I know."

"You see anything you don't understand, you don't guess. You tell us."

Alaric nodded. "Understood."

Joel looked at Tess.

She checked her pistol and smiled. "Let's see if the stray can hunt."

---

The house smelled of mold and old smoke.

Alaric put on the gas mask before entering. There were no spores in the front room, but he kept it on until he had checked the kitchen and stairs.

A dead runner lay beneath the staircase with an axe buried in its skull. The body was old and dry.

The second floor was clear.

Alaric moved into a bedroom overlooking the drainage channel. Part of the wall had collapsed, giving him a wide view through hanging vines.

He removed the mask and stored it. Then he took the clean 9mm pistol from its holster.

Firearms Aptitude gave him the knowledge to check it quickly. Fifteen rounds in the magazine and one in the chamber. Safety ready. The weapon was new enough that he did not have to worry about rust or a weak spring.

He rested both hands on the broken wall.

Tess appeared below a few minutes later. She walked beside the channel with one hand pressed to her side and her shoulders bent. From a distance, she looked injured and alone.

Joel was nowhere in sight.

Alaric knew that was the point.

A branch moved on the far side of the channel.

One man rose behind the brush with a rifle. Another moved farther left with a shotgun. Alaric spotted two more near a fallen tree.

Four people were visible, but Joel had counted six sets of tracks.

Alaric searched again.

A fifth man was behind Tess, moving through the trees she had already passed. The sixth remained hidden.

Alaric waited.

The rifleman aimed at Tess.

"Drop the bag," he called.

Tess stopped. "I don't want trouble."

"Then drop it."

She slowly removed her pack.

The shotgun carrier stepped into the open.

Alaric recognized the red cloth around his arm.

One of the missing bandits.

"Where's the white-haired kid?" the man asked.

Tess let her pack fall. "What kid?"

"The one who killed our people."

"Haven't seen him."

The man smiled. "Then you won't mind if we check."

He started toward her.

The sixth bandit rose from the channel itself. He had been lying beneath a sheet covered with mud and leaves. His pistol was already aimed at Tess's back.

Alaric had nearly missed him.

"Behind you!" Alaric shouted.

Tess dropped flat.

Alaric fired twice.

The hidden man's head snapped back. He fell into the shallow water.

**ROAD HUNTER I: 7/10**

Gunfire answered at once.

Alaric ducked. Bullets struck brick and sent chips across the room.

Below, Joel fired from behind the stone wall. The rifleman dropped.

Tess rolled behind a concrete pipe and shot the man who had followed her through the trees.

Three remained.

The shotgun carrier fired at Joel's wall. The blast tore leaves from the bushes.

Alaric moved to the next window. He stayed low, counted two seconds, then rose from a different angle.

The shotgun carrier was running toward Tess.

Alaric fired three times. Two shots hit the man's chest. He fell forward and slid down the muddy bank.

**ROAD HUNTER I: 8/10**

The other two bandits moved apart. One fired at Alaric's window while the other ran toward the back of the house.

"One coming to me!" Alaric shouted.

"Move!" Joel called.

Alaric left the bedroom and crossed the hall. A bullet came through the wall behind him.

He reached the stairs as the back door crashed open downstairs.

The bandit entered fast. He wore the same red cloth and carried a short-barreled rifle.

Alaric aimed down the stairs, but the man saw him and fired first.

The bullet struck the railing. Wood burst against Alaric's arms.

Alaric fired three times. Two rounds struck the man's chest, and he fell.

**ROAD HUNTER I: 9/10**

Alaric changed magazines with shaking fingers. The aptitude helped him remember each step, but it did not make his hands calm.

Outside, Tess fired twice.

Then everything went quiet.

Alaric listened.

No footsteps. No voices. No gunfire.

"Alaric?" Tess called.

"I'm here!"

"Stay there."

He heard Joel and Tess moving outside. A minute later, Joel entered through the back door.

He checked the dead bandit, then looked up the stairs.

"You hit?"

"No."

"Come down."

Alaric joined him in the front room. His arms had several small cuts from the railing, but his HP had only dropped by two.

**HP: 81/100**

Tess stood outside near the channel. A bandit lay at her feet with a bullet wound in his side. He was still breathing.

"This is one of the two who left the station," she said.

The man looked at Alaric and tried to reach for a knife.

Tess stepped on his wrist.

"Still making bad choices," she said.

Alaric raised his pistol.

Joel watched him but did not interfere.

The wounded man had helped a gang that kidnapped children and women. He had returned with four more people to hunt Alaric and anyone traveling with him.

There was nothing to think about.

Alaric fired once.

**CHAIN QUEST COMPLETE: ROAD HUNTER I**

**Objective:** Kill 10 hostile bandits.  
**Completion Time:** 18 hours, 46 minutes

**Rewards Granted:**

- 2 Physique Points
- Skill Book: Basic Close Combat
- 100 rounds of 9mm ammunition

Tess looked at the blue screen without seeing it. "You all right?"

"Yes."

She glanced at his pistol. "You don't sound happy about it."

"I don't have to enjoy killing them to know it was necessary."

Joel picked up the dead man's knife. "Good answer."

It was the first kind thing Joel had said to him.

Joel probably did not mean it as one.

Alaric accepted the rewards.

**Physique: Developing, 0/10 → Developing, 2/10**

His muscles warmed. The cuts on his arms did not vanish, and his injured side still hurt, but standing became a little easier.

A thin gray book appeared in his inventory.

**Skill Book: Basic Close Combat**  
**Effect:** Raises Close Combat from Untrained to Novice. Grants safe foundations in stance, balance, guarding, falling, and basic strikes. Practice is required for reliable use.

**Use now?**

Alaric selected yes.

The book vanished.

Knowledge settled into his mind. He understood where to place his feet and how to protect his head. When he tested the stance, his body felt awkward.

**Close Combat: Untrained → Novice**

The skill had given him a starting point. He still needed training.

Joel noticed his feet. "Where'd you learn that?"

Alaric straightened. "FEDRA school."

"You stand like someone explained it five minutes ago."

That was uncomfortably accurate.

"I wasn't a good student."

Tess laughed.

Joel stared at him for another second, then handed him the dead man's knife. "Keep practicing."

Alaric stored it after Joel turned away.

They searched the bodies. The attackers carried ammunition, dried meat, two canteens, rope, and a folded map. Alaric let Joel and Tess divide everything. Hiding the system would be difficult enough without making supplies disappear in front of them.

Tess returned his pistol after checking the chamber.

"Two fights in one day," she said. "You always this much trouble?"

"This is my first day outside the school."

She blinked. "Seriously?"

"Yes."

Joel looked at the bodies. "Hell of a first day."

Alaric smiled. "It could've gone worse."

"Don't say things like that," Tess said. "The world listens."

---

Their delivery point was an abandoned rail depot beside the river.

They reached it after sunrise. Rusted freight cars stood on broken tracks, and weeds reached Alaric's waist between them.

Joel led them to a maintenance shed with a red triangle painted beside the door. He knocked four times, waited, then knocked twice.

A narrow viewing slot opened.

"Late," a man inside said.

"Road trouble," Tess replied.

The slot closed. Several locks opened.

The man took a wrapped package from Tess and handed her a small canvas bag in return. He looked at Alaric but did not ask questions.

Tess counted the ration cards inside the bag.

"We're short."

"You're late."

"That wasn't the deal."

"Take it or carry the package back."

Joel shifted his weight.

The man looked at him and added two cards.

Tess smiled. "Pleasure doing business."

They left before the man could answer.

"You threatened him without saying anything," Alaric said when they were out of hearing.

"Joel has many talents," Tess said. "Most of them involve standing there looking unpleasant."

"Works, don't it?" Joel asked.

"It does."

The safehouse was another mile east, hidden beneath an old riverside warehouse. Tess moved a loose sheet of metal behind a loading dock and revealed a stairway.

The room below had three cots, a small stove, water cans, and shelves filled with tools. It was cleaner than the bandit camp and safer than anywhere Alaric had seen since arriving.

Tess locked the entrance behind them.

"Sit," she told Alaric.

He sat on one of the cots.

Joel placed his revolver on a table and started unloading it. The cylinder stuck halfway open.

"Hand me the cleaning cloth," he said.

Alaric passed it to him. He watched Joel force the cylinder the rest of the way.

"Your ejector rod is loose," Alaric said.

Joel stopped. "What?"

"It can back out when the cylinder turns. That may be why it keeps sticking."

Joel checked it.

The rod moved under his fingers.

Tess leaned against the wall. "You know guns too?"

"A little."

Joel tightened the rod and tested the cylinder. It opened smoothly.

He looked at Alaric with a little less suspicion.

"A little," Joel repeated.

Alaric shrugged. "I like knowing how things work."

Tess opened a cupboard and took out three ration bars. She tossed one to Joel and handed another to Alaric.

Alaric caught it.

"You said until morning," he reminded her.

Sunlight had already reached the cracks around the hidden entrance.

"I did."

Joel sat at the table. "And now it's morning."

Tess ignored him. "You found the ambush before we walked into it. You can shoot, you know basic first aid, and apparently you can fix guns."

"I pointed out one loose part."

"Don't ruin your own argument."

Alaric looked at Joel. "Do I get a vote?"

"No."

"Joel," Tess said.

He ate half his ration bar before answering.

"Three days," he said. "He stays here. Doesn't go near the zone, doesn't meet our contacts, and doesn't touch anything without asking."

"I can agree to that," Alaric said.

"Wasn't asking you."

Tess smiled at Alaric. "That means yes."

Joel pointed the rest of his ration bar at him. "It means three days. You lie to us, steal from us, or bring FEDRA to this door, you're gone."

"Understood."

"And no more clearing camps by yourself."

Alaric looked at the bruises on his hands. "I can agree to that too."

Tess sat on the cot across from him and removed her boots. "Good. Sleep. We can decide what to do with you after you stop looking like you're about to fall over."

Alaric lay back.

The cot was narrow. The blanket smelled dusty. Joel was still watching him from the table.

It was the safest Alaric had felt since waking in this world.

The system displayed one final message.

**WORLD MISSION MILESTONE UPDATED**

**Build the trust needed to join Joel and Tess.**  
**Current Status:** Temporary shelter granted  
**Next Milestone:** Complete one job as part of their team  
**Milestone Reward:** None

This was not an optional quest. It was simply the next step toward changing the future.

**Current HP:** 81/100  
**Current Stamina:** 18/100

Alaric closed the screen and pulled the blanket over himself.

He had arrived with nowhere to go.

Now he had three days.
