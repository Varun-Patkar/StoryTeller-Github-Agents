# Joel Miller

## Appearance

Joel is a broad, weathered man with graying dark hair and a heavy beard. Years of hard work and violence show in the way he watches doors, keeps his hands near his weapons, and positions himself where nobody can approach unseen.

## Personality & Traits

Joel is practical, guarded, and slow to trust. He notices useful details and expects strangers to become problems. He complains when Tess takes risks but usually helps her anyway. His care appears through actions rather than speeches.

## Backstory

Joel lost his daughter Sarah during the first night of the outbreak. Twenty years later, he survives as a smuggler in and around the Boston quarantine zone. The loss remains central to the distance he keeps from other people.

## Motivations & Goals

- Survive and protect the few people he still considers his own.
- Avoid attachments that could leave him grieving again.
- Keep his partnership with Tess working despite the risks they take.

## Location References

Joel operates from Boston and uses routes through the surrounding ruins for smuggling and deliveries.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Joel speaks in short, blunt sentences. He asks direct questions, dislikes long explanations, and often shows annoyance instead of concern.

> "How many people are with you?"

> "We don't know him."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Joel treats survival and distance as the safest way to live.
- **Key turning points:** He reluctantly accepts Alaric, then Ellie, into his life and sees tragedies he expected begin to change.
- **Ending state:** Joel helps build and defend a lasting home instead of living from one job to the next.
