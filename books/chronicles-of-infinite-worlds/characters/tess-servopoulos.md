# Tess Servopoulos

## Appearance

Tess is lean, dark-haired, and hardened by life in the quarantine zone. She moves with confidence, keeps her weapon ready, and studies people closely before deciding how to handle them.

## Personality & Traits

Tess is practical, bold, and better with people than Joel. She can threaten someone one moment and offer help the next if the facts change. She enjoys needling Joel when he becomes too predictable. Her kindness is real, but she hides it behind business and common sense.

## Backstory

Tess has survived long enough to become a respected smuggler in Boston. She and Joel work as partners, handling dangerous deliveries and dealing with people neither of them fully trusts.

## Motivations & Goals

- Keep herself and Joel alive while building enough influence to remain independent.
- Judge people by what they do rather than by where they came from.
- Help when she can do so without pretending the world is safer than it is.

## Location References

Tess operates from Boston and uses smuggling routes through the surrounding ruins.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Tess speaks directly and confidently. Her humor is simple, usually aimed at Joel, and she is comfortable making quick decisions.

> "He's fifteen."

> "Come on, stray."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Tess expects every job to carry danger and accepts that survival may be the best outcome available.
- **Key turning points:** Alaric saves her from the death she never knew was coming, and their group begins creating something better than another day of survival.
- **Ending state:** Tess becomes one of the leaders of a safe, growing community.
