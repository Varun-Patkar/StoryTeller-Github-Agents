# Riverside Smuggler Safehouse

## Description

A hidden basement beneath an abandoned warehouse near the river. A loose metal sheet behind the loading dock covers the entrance. Inside are three cots, a small stove, water cans, shelves of tools, and enough supplies for short stays.

## History

Joel and Tess prepared the safehouse as a place to rest outside the Boston quarantine zone between deliveries.

## Culture & Customs

Anyone using the safehouse keeps its location secret, leaves no visible trail to the entrance, and replaces shared supplies when possible.

## Notable Features

- A concealed stairway behind a loose sheet of metal
- Three cots and spare blankets
- A worktable suitable for cleaning and repairing weapons
- Stored water, tools, and basic food

## Current State

Joel and Tess bring Alaric here after their first fight together. They allow him to stay for a three-day trial.
