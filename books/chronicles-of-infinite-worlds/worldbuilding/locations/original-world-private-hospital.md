# Original-World Private Hospital

## Description

An expensive private hospital designed to conceal illness behind quiet carpets, pale wood, filtered air, and discreet machinery. Alaric's room is large enough for visitors who rarely come. Its most personal feature is a shelf of games sent by his family.

## History

Alaric spends the final stage of his illness here under excellent medical care paid for by his parents.

## Culture & Customs

Staff preserve privacy and calm. Wealth removes inconvenience but cannot manufacture intimacy.

## Notable Features

- A broad window overlooking the city
- A visitor's chair that is usually empty
- A shelf of Alaric's favorite games
- Quiet medical equipment surrounding the bed

## Current State

The hospital continues operating after Alaric's death. Elena is the only relative present during his final night.
