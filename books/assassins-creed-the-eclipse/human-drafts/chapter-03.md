### 75,000 BCE

#### The plan

You do not get an idea like this from nothing. I got it from a wasted first life.

[The seed is meta-knowledge, referenced flat, not fanboyed. Two sources:]
[1. The secret ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla, the one Cain was narrating to an empty villa
the night he died: the Isu, facing extinction, backed copies of themselves into the Yggdrasil
system to be reborn into new bodies across the centuries. Odin, Loki, the rest. `character-basim`
is Loki reincarnated; the Sages (`concept-sages`) are Aita reincarnating. The Isu already solved
"how do I survive my own death." Cain steals their homework.]
[2. Cyberpunk 2077, the engram / soul-on-a-chip idea. Put the two together: don't just back up a
copy, upload yourself entirely and put the meat on ice so a body is waiting on the far side.]

[The plan, laid out plainly to the family, the way Cain always talks:]
- [Upload his mind fully into an Isu network so the flare can't kill what isn't flesh.]
- [Freeze all four bodies (his, Adam's, Eve's, Abel's) in Isu stasis, deep underground.]
- [Wait out the Catastrophe and the dead years, wake into an empty world with a 75,000-year head
  start on every Isu ghost pulling the same trick.]

[The geography, told by map-shape not names, because Cain knows coastlines from loading screens:]
- [The Grand Temple under future New York (`location-grand-temple`): holds the tech to digitize a
  mind fully into the network. This is where Cain uploads and where he hides the bodies. He starts
  calling it the Core.]
- [Yggdrasil in Norway: the reincarnation engine that brings a digital mind back into a living
  body. The family goes to secure it so they aren't just ghosts, they have a way back to flesh.]
- [Eden is in Africa (where they are); nearest vaults Siwa/Giza in Egypt. Distances are
  continental. The only reason any of it is possible: the humans captured Isu travel tech in the
  war. Decide the mechanism, I left it open.]

[The split argument (your narrative choice): Cain to New York, family to Norway, then reunite.
Eve won't want to split with the world ending; Adam won't want to take orders from his changed
son. Cain wins, because Cain always wins, but it should cost something. Let one true thing slip
through his cold when he says goodbye to Abel, the brother he almost killed and never confessed
to. Your call what the last words are.]

#### The long way there

[Cutaway (third person, brief): Adam, Eve, and Abel take the captured transit toward Norway and
Yggdrasil. Keep it to a few paragraphs, or a couple of intercut beats. Flag anything you want to
see on their road.]

[Cain's leg (first person): he rides the stolen Isu transit toward the place that becomes New
York, navigating by river-mouths and coastlines he half-remembers. The world is huge and empty
and wrong and he keeps catching himself in wonder, then getting back to work. Wonder on a short
leash, he'd hate to be caught feeling it.]

[Arrival at the Grand Temple, new and lit and working, not the ruin Desmond will find. Cain knows
what this room becomes: the place a man in a hood dies to stop the second fire, 75,000 years from
now. He's standing in a tomb that hasn't been used yet. Let it land once, then move.]

#### The upload

[The infiltration, earned not walked through. Decide who or what still staffs the Temple this
close to the end (Isu, automatons, a caretaker intelligence). Cain's edges: Apple-immune, holds
his own Apple, knows the building's future layout. His weakness: a mortal teenager with a stone
knife who everything here can kill. Keep him fragile, every win earned.]

[The upload itself. The hard question, straight out of Cyberpunk: how do you know an upload copies
you and doesn't just kill you and make a stranger who wakes up thinking it's you? Cain knows the
question. He climbs in anyway, because the alternative is the fire. Write the moment he lets go of
his body. Consider: does the story treat it as continuity or as a leap of faith the reader is
never fully reassured about? That ambiguity is good, keep it if you like it.]

#### The freeze

[Adam, Eve, and Abel retrieve the reincarnation tech at Yggdrasil and come to the Grand Temple to
meet Cain (now running in the Core). The Catastrophe is almost on them, the sky already wrong.]

[Cain puts the three of them on ice in the vault, bodies safe underground. He shuts himself off,
deliberately, so the solar flare can't reach in and corrupt or disable his running mind, and sets
himself to wake in one year. Last image: the world going to fire above, four bodies cold in the
dark below, and one mind winking out on purpose, on a timer.]

[hook: the timer, and the 75,000 years waiting on the other side of it. This closes Arc I. Next

something that can survive a very long silence.]
