### 2021 (Present Time), Malibu, Cain Mercer's Villa

Sounds of grunting could be heard throughout the villa so much so that someone not in the know might think they had arrived at Tony Stark's villa at night.

It was just a man lying down with a mouse and keyboard in front of a large wall that was projecting the game Assassin's Creed Valhalla. "Oh. So Odin and the rest were Isu scientists who backed up themselves in the 'Yggdrassil' and Loki was a rival who backed himself up as well. Pretty nice twist for a modern AC game." said the man with mild surprise.

The man was Cain Mercer. He was a orphan abandoned at an orphanage. The orphanage and the US Child Services taught him the ways of the world. He never quite fit in with any of his foster families as his siblings described him as cold. He was finally adopted by a fairly rich couple who were incapable of having children. He took their surnames Mercer but he had the godforsaken name of Cain from a letter that was left at the orphanage.

Being an orphan and then a rich parents' son taught him that he had to be ruthless to those who had ill will to him and kind to those who tried to help him. He understood that the way people percieve you is much more important than who you are. So as he did his education he was called a sunny boy who helped others.

One of his biggst hobbies was gaming. He loved to lose himself in those worlds. His favorite game was Vampire The Masquerade Bloodlines. He wanted to be Cain like in the game. A immortal figure that watches and benefits from all the conflict. He did not have any qualms taking advantage of others to further himself. But he always took care of his own people.

Cain had just finished AC Valhalla. And he had to go for a gala in 4 hours. One of the disadvantages of being a CEO was that he had to keep his social image. He could not just slack off all day.

As Cain fell into the jaws of sleep a deep wish echoed through his sleepy voice: "I wish i could be Cain". An unknown voice echoed back: "So be it."
