[The parents' reaction. Eve checks Abel's head. Adam looks at Cain. And here is the important

Here is where I decided to stop being a boy who hit his brother and start being useful.

[Cain's choice: he does NOT deny or grovel. He tells them the truth that matters and buries the

a thing measured in generations. Cain cuts him off.]

[The reveal, in Cain's words, kept concrete, not mystical: the sun is going to open. A fire is
coming out of it, aimed at the whole world. It will burn every Isu body to ash, all their cities,

Catastrophe, ~75,000 BCE, historically misattributed to Toba. Jupiter's line in canon: "less than
ten thousand of your kind still lived." Use that number feel.]

[Why they believe a child: they believe him because of the Apple, not despite it. The Apple is an
Isu artifact. Adam and Eve stole it, they use it, but they barely understand it, they can only
make it steer humans. If it showed Cain something they can't do, then it did something beyond
their knowing, and that is exactly the kind of thing you do not dismiss. Eve is the one who says
it: we cannot un-see a warning just because we do not like the mouth it came from. They decide
they have to act. End the chapter on the family choosing to believe the end of the world.]
