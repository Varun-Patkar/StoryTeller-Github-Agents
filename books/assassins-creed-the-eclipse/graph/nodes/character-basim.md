# Character: Basim

## Overview
Born 844 in Samarra, a Master of the Hidden Ones and the human reincarnation of the Isu Loki. Trapped in the Grey/Yggdrasil simulation by Eivor (host of Odin) in 877, he was freed in 2020 by Layla Hassan via the Staff of Hermes. He seeks revenge on Odin and the return of his children.

## Canon
Loki incarnate; father of Fenrir, Jormungandr, and Hel; lover of Aletheia; imprisoned by Odin, freed in the modern day.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Charming, patient, wounded fury under silk. 'Everything I do, I do for my family.' / 'You wear patience like a crown, Owl. So do I. Let us see whose outlasts the world.'

## AU Divergence
Cain's true intellectual equal and the antagonist of the Game of Gods arc. Both play centuries-long games; neither can act openly. Loki's grief for his children mirrors Cain's grief for Abel, making them dark reflections.
