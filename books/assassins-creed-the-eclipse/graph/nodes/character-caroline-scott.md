# Character: Caroline Scott

## Overview
Edward Kenway's first wife and mother of Jennifer Scott. History records her death in 1720.

## Canon
Estranged from Edward; dies of smallpox (novel canon) while Edward is at sea; Jennifer is their daughter.

## AU Divergence
FIX-IT: secretly rescued by the Eclipse years before her recorded death. The world, and Edward, believe her dead. Only later does the Eclipse reveal the truth, which is what finally wins Edward's loyalty. Must feel earned, not miraculous.
