# Faction: Assassin Brotherhood

## Overview
The order that pursues free will and peace through directed violence, founded as the Hidden Ones by Bayek and Aya. Maxim: 'Nothing is true, everything is permitted.' Led by a Mentor.

## Canon
Reveres Adam and Eve as progenitors; opposes the Templars across history.

## AU Divergence
One of the two 'visible' factions whose eternal war the Eclipse must keep from being won by either side. The Eclipse ensures neither triumphs completely.
