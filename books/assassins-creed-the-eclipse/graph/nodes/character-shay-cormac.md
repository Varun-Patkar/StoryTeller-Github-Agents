# Character: Shay Cormac

## Overview
An Assassin who defected to the Templars after a Precursor artifact he retrieved triggered the 1755 Lisbon earthquake, convincing him the Brotherhood's zealotry was dangerous.

## Canon
His arc is fundamentally about balance and the peril of pursuing artifacts blindly, closely echoing Eclipse philosophy.

## AU Divergence
The Assassin most naturally aligned with Eclipse thinking. A prime recruitment target: Shay already believes neither side should hold absolute power.
