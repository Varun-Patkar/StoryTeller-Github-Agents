# Character: Tessa Kenway

## Overview
Edward Kenway's second wife, mother of Haytham. Historically died in 1747.

## Canon
Wife of Edward at the time of his 1735 murder; raised Haytham after Edward's death.

## AU Divergence
Her survival past 1735 (with a secretly-alive Edward) is one of the delicate continuity problems the Canon Divergence Register must manage.
