# Event: The Fall of Juno

## Overview
Inside the Grand Temple's network (~75,000 BCE), the newly-digitized Cain finds Juno and her husband Aita already present as digital consciousnesses. Juno cannot control the Apple-immune Cain and nearly erases his digital self in the grey, where she is older and far more practiced. Cain wins anyway by being two things at once: his physical body, untouchable by any mind in the grey, dismantles her domain switch by switch while his digital self herds her into the severed rooms. He purges most of Juno, but a cold sliver of her escapes into the Temple's deepest systems (`thread-junos-fragment`). He then deletes Aita's Temple-bound consciousness entirely, and takes ownership of the whole base.

## Canon
INVENTED. Uses canon Isu digital-consciousness tech and Juno's Grand Temple imprisonment as precedent; recontextualizes her canon 2012 emergence as the resurfacing of the fragment Cain missed.

## AU Divergence
The war between Cain and Juno begins here, at the very start of the story, not the modern day. Establishes Cain's signature dual-body method and seeds two long threads: the escaped Juno fragment (pays off in 2012) and Cain's mistaken belief that he fully erased Aita (the Sage seed survives).
