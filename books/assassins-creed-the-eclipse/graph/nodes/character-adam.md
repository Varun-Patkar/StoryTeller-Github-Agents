# Character: Adam

## Overview
One of the first two humans born immune to Isu neurotransmitter control. With Eve he stole an Apple of Eden from the vault of Eden and sparked the Human-Isu War.

## Canon
Adam and Eve are the legendary progenitors the Assassins revere. They rebelled against Isu masters using a stolen Apple.

## AU Divergence
Cain defects to Adam and Eve after awakening; they are the first humans he allies with. He helps their rebellion succeed while knowing it costs civilization itself.
