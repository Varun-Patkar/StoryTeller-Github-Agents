# Location: The Core

## Overview
The hidden Isu computational network into which Cain uploaded his consciousness before the First Catastrophe, and beneath which his original body rests in stasis for tens of thousands of years. From here he studies Isu systems, hunts surviving digital Isu, builds facilities, and prepares humanity.

## Canon
INVENTED, though inspired by the Isu digital realm known as the Grey.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse's true heart. Every Echo uploads its memories here on death. Its location is the Eclipse's most guarded secret.

ESTABLISHED in Chapter 5. Cain built the first Core in a deep pit from the looted consciousness-transfer tech, then sealed the pit shut behind his upload. At its founding it is "a country of one," dark and empty but growing, his own private "grey" under a dead world. His stasis body lies in a stone cradle beside the vessel. This is the seed of what becomes a vast underground network across millennia.

## Notes
At Arc I's end the Core is brand new and small. Its growth over tens of thousands of years is Arc IV material. Location secret from everyone.
