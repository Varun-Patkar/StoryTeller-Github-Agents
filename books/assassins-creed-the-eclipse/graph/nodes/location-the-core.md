# Location: The Core

## Overview
The hidden Isu computational network into which Cain uploaded his consciousness before the First Catastrophe, and beneath which his original body rests in stasis for tens of thousands of years. From here he studies Isu systems, hunts surviving digital Isu, builds facilities, and prepares humanity.

## Canon
INVENTED, though inspired by the Isu digital realm known as the Grey.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse's true heart. Every Echo uploads its memories here on death. Its location is the Eclipse's most guarded secret.
