# Character: Desmond Miles

## Overview
Born 1987, descendant of Altair, Ezio, Edward Kenway, and Connor. Son of William Miles. Kidnapped by Abstergo, he relived ancestral memories via the Animus, then sacrificed himself at the Grand Temple in 2012 to stop a solar flare, which also freed Juno.

## Canon
His fragmented consciousness persists in the Grey as 'The Reader.' Father of Elijah (a Sage).

## Voice & Mannerisms
Reluctant hero, dry, working-class. 'I'm a bartender. Or I was.'

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse has quietly protected Desmond since birth without his knowledge. Arc VII is about ensuring he survives every critical turning point, and grappling with whether to alter his fated sacrifice.
