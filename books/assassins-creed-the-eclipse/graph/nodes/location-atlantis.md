# Location: Atlantis

## Overview
A major Isu city, later sealed away by Pythagoras using the Staff of Hermes. Aletheia served as its Dikastes.

## Canon
Explored in the Fate of Atlantis simulations tied to the Staff and Kassandra.

## AU Divergence
A vault of dangerous Isu tech the Eclipse works to keep sealed or emptied of its most corrupting artifacts.
