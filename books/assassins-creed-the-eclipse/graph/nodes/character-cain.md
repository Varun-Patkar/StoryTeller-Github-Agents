# Character: Cain

## Overview
A man from the early 21st century, a lifelong Assassin's Creed fan, who wakes inside Cain, one of the first humans made by the Isu, in the instant after Cain murders his brother Abel. He is not a clean soul dropped into a stranger's body. The original Cain is still in there: the instincts, the appetite, the native ruthlessness of the first man willing to kill for what he wanted. The modern mind and Cain's mind are braided together, and they agree on more than the modern man is comfortable admitting.

Cain is selfish, greedy, and ambitious. He wants to live, to win, to accumulate: knowledge, power, longevity, leverage, comfort. He is going to save humanity from the catastrophes he knows are coming, but not because he is good. He is going to save it because he lives here, because extinction is the one loss he cannot profit from, and because a dead world is worth nothing to a man who intends to own a piece of everything. Over tens of thousands of years he founds The Eclipse, takes Kassandra as the one person he truly keeps, and hunts the surviving Isu, all of it in service of a long, patient, fundamentally self-interested game.

## Canon
In canon, Cain is only a legend: son of Adam and Eve, he murdered his brother Abel to steal Abel's Apple of Eden, becoming the first murderer. Some Templars call themselves 'Children of Cain.' He never appears as a character with dialogue. Our Cain keeps that legend intact as a fact of record while being something far larger underneath it.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Dry, sardonic, calculating. Unsentimental to the point of coldness, and honest with himself about it. He talks like a man running numbers behind his eyes even while he smiles. He owns his own selfishness rather than hiding from it, and that candor is half his charm. Gallows humor. Rarely raises his voice, because he rarely needs to. Meta-knowledge referenced flatly, as leverage, never as fanboy awe.
- "I'm not a hero. Heroes die on schedule. I intend to be inconvenient about it."
- "Everyone keeps asking what I want. I want everything. I'm just patient about the order I take it in."
- "I didn't kill him for you, or for a cause. I killed him because a leash told me to. That's exactly why I cut the leash."
- "Freedom, order, pick a poison. Push either one far enough and the whole thing burns, and I happen to live in the thing."
- "Grief is a luxury. I've priced it. I can't afford the good kind, so I'll take the cheap kind and keep walking."
- "Trust me because I'm greedy. A greedy man keeps you alive as long as you're worth more breathing."
- "I don't want a throne. Thrones are targets. I want the room the throne is in, and the door, and the man who thinks he owns both."

## AU Divergence
ORIGINAL protagonist layered onto the canon Cain legend, but NOT a saintly self-insert. The reincarnator's knowledge sits on top of the original Cain's ruthless, hungry temperament, and the two have merged. He does not grieve Abel; he never knew Abel, and the part of him that was Cain never cared. What remains is appetite and calculation.

His mission to save humanity is real but self-interested at the root: enlightened greed, not altruism. He keeps himself out of history because anonymity is armor and advantage, not penance. The Balance philosophy he will later codify is pragmatic, absolutism collapses civilizations, and collapse costs him everything he is building. Kassandra becomes the rare genuine exception to his transactional view of people.

## Notes
Power comes from knowledge, patience, preparation, and (later) accumulated Echo lifetimes, never invincibility. Every victory must be earned. Keep him likable through candor and competence, not virtue. He is allowed to do cold, ugly things for good long-term reasons.
