# Character: Cain

## Overview
A lifelong Assassin's Creed fan from the early 21st century who reincarnates as Cain, one of the first humans made by the Isu. He kills Abel while still mentally enslaved, then his modern mind awakens. Realizing where and when he is, he defects to Adam and Eve, survives the First Catastrophe by uploading his consciousness into an Isu computational network (the Core), and spends roughly seventy-five thousand years quietly guiding humanity from the shadows.

## Canon
In canon, Cain is only a legend: son of Adam and Eve, he murdered his brother Abel to steal Abel's Apple of Eden, becoming the first murderer. Some Templars call themselves 'Children of Cain.' He never appears as a character with dialogue.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Measured, patient, self-deprecating about his own myth. Speaks like someone carrying an impossible amount of memory. Rarely raises his voice. Dry gallows humor about being remembered only as a killer. Example beats: 'History gave me one line. I have spent seventy thousand years writing the rest in the margins.' / 'I do not want a throne. Thrones fall. I want the people under them to still be breathing when they do.' / 'Balance is not the absence of a choice. It is the discipline to keep making it.' / 'I killed my brother before I was myself. Everything after has been the apology.' / 'Do not mistake my patience for mercy, Loki. I simply take the long way to the same knife.'

## AU Divergence
ORIGINAL SELF-INSERT overwriting canon Cain. Instead of remaining a mere legend and villain, our Cain becomes the hidden architect of human survival. He founds The Eclipse, romances Kassandra across millennia, and opposes the surviving Isu. History still records only 'Abel's murderer' — his anonymity is deliberate and is his greatest protection.

## Notes
Power comes from knowledge, patience, and accumulated Echo lifetimes, never invincibility. Every victory must feel earned.
