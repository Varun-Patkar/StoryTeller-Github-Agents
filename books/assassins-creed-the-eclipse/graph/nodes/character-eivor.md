# Character: Eivor

## Overview
A Viking raider and Hidden One, host to the memories of the Isu Odin. In 877 Eivor helped seal Basim (Loki) inside the Yggdrasil/Grey network.

## Canon
Carries Odin's Isu consciousness; ally of the Hidden Ones; buried in Vinland (the New World).

## AU Divergence
The historical figure who first 'won' against Loki. Cain studies how Eivor's imprisonment of Basim held for over a thousand years, and dreads the day it fails.
