# Character: Kassandra

## Overview
Born 458 BCE, a Spartan-descended mercenary (misthios) and the Eagle Bearer. Granddaughter of Leonidas, she wields the Spear of Leonidas, an Isu artifact. She carries strong Isu DNA and, through the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus, lives for over 2,400 years, dying at Santorini in 2018 after passing the Staff on.

## Canon
Daughter of Myrrine and Pythagoras, half-sister of Deimos/Alexios, mother of Elpidios (ancestor of Aya). Destroyed the Cult of Kosmos, became 'Keeper' guided by Aletheia. Longevity granted by the Staff of Hermes.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Blunt, wry, warrior's directness with a soldier's tenderness underneath. Greek oaths, mercenary pragmatism. 'By the gods, you talk too much for a dead man.' / 'I have buried everyone I loved twice over. Do not ask me to do it a third time and mean it.' / 'You think too far ahead, Owl. Sometimes the knife in front of you is the whole world.' / 'Malaka. Fine. Show me this Balance of yours.'

## AU Divergence
The first person Cain truly trusts after awakening. Their romance unfolds over 2,000+ years through repeated reunions rather than constant companionship. She is his equal and the only one who reliably challenges his decisions after millennia of burden. The Staff's longevity lets their love actually span history.
