# Concept: The Creed

## Overview
The Assassin doctrine devised by Bayek and refined over centuries: 'Nothing is true, everything is permitted,' with tenets forbidding harm to innocents, requiring discretion, and protecting the Brotherhood.

## Canon
Core philosophy of the Assassins/Hidden Ones.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse respects the Creed but refuses to make Freedom absolute, hence Balance rather than membership in the Brotherhood.
