# Character: Altair

## Overview
Born 1165, Mentor of the Levantine Assassins at Masyaf. He reformed the Brotherhood, wrote the Codex, and long studied an Apple of Eden.

## Canon
Reformed the Order after Al Mualim's betrayal; his memories are relived by Desmond. Maxim: 'We work in the dark to serve the light.'

## Voice & Mannerisms
Terse, philosophical, self-corrected pride. 'Nothing is true, everything is permitted.'

## AU Divergence
During his era the Eclipse quietly steers Apples away from the worst hands. Altair never learns the Owls exist, though he senses a hidden pattern in events.
