# Location: Santorini

## Overview
The Greek island where Kassandra, after 2,400+ years, finally dies in 2018 having passed the Staff of Hermes to Layla.

## Canon
Kassandra's final meeting with Layla and her death.

## AU Divergence
The emotional endpoint of Cain and Kassandra's millennia-long romance, and a fixed continuity anchor the story must honor or deliberately, carefully divert.
