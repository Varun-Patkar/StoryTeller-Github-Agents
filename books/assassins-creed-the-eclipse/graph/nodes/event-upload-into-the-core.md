# Event: Upload into the Core

## Overview
Shortly before the First Catastrophe, Cain infiltrates an Isu city, uploads his consciousness into a computational network (the Core), places his original body in hidden stasis, establishes an autonomous underground base, and erases all evidence of his survival.

## Canon
INVENTED, using canon Isu digital-consciousness technology as precedent.

## AU Divergence
The act that makes Cain effectively immortal and anonymous. Humanity remembers only Abel's murderer; that anonymity becomes his greatest protection.

DEPICTED in Chapter 5. As the sky began to turn, Cain finished building the Core deep in a pit from the tech he looted in Chapter 4. He uploaded ONLY himself (his lifelong goal: to be the deathless, marked Cain), laid his body in a stone stasis cradle, and let the vessel "pour" his mind into the dark. Before uploading he destroyed the tools that could copy the process, scoured the readable marks, and built the pit to seal shut behind him, so nothing above or below would say the Knife did anything but die in the cataclysm. He did NOT upload anyone else. The world (including the survivors) believes Cain died in the Catastrophe.

## Notes
Arc I finale. From here Cain exists as consciousness in the Core with tens of thousands of years ahead before Arc II (Greece).
