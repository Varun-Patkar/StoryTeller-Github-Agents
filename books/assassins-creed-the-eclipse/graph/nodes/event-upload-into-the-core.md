# Event: Upload into the Core

## Overview
Shortly before the First Catastrophe, Cain infiltrates an Isu city, uploads his consciousness into a computational network (the Core), places his original body in hidden stasis, establishes an autonomous underground base, and erases all evidence of his survival.

## Canon
INVENTED, using canon Isu digital-consciousness technology as precedent.

## AU Divergence
The act that makes Cain effectively immortal and anonymous. Humanity remembers only Abel's murderer; that anonymity becomes his greatest protection.
