# Faction: Cult of Kosmos

## Overview
A secret cabal manipulating the Greek world during the Peloponnesian War, wielding Isu artifacts and shaping politics from the shadows.

## Canon
Kassandra dismantles the Cult; it is an ideological ancestor of the Order of the Ancients/Templars.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse's first major operation with Kassandra: dismantling the Cult while learning how surviving Isu tech corrupts human power structures.
