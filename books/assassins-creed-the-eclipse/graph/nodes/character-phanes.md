# Character: Phanes

## Overview
The Isu who led Project Anthropos, the creation of the human worker species. He fathered Eve with a human, producing an immune hybrid.

## Canon
Head of the human-creation project; his hybrid daughter Eve became a rebellion leader.

## AU Divergence
A source of forbidden knowledge Cain quietly studies. His research notes on hybrid DNA later underpin Cain's Echo bodies.
