# Concept: Project Anthropos

## Overview
The Isu program, headed by Phanes, that created humans as a controllable workforce, complete with neurotransmitters keyed to Pieces of Eden.

## Canon
The origin of humanity in the setting.

## AU Divergence
Cain's foreknowledge of Anthropos lets him understand human 'programming' well enough to protect people from Isu control tech.
