# Concept: Isu DNA

## Overview
The Isu possessed triple-helix DNA granting a sixth sense. Hybrids (Adam, Eve, Kassandra, Desmond) carry high concentrations, enabling PoE compatibility, Eagle Vision, and resistance to control.

## Canon
The genetic backbone of the setting's 'chosen bloodlines.'

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse deliberately engineers maximal Isu DNA into its Echoes, and can edit Animus-readable DNA records to hide bloodlines from Abstergo.
