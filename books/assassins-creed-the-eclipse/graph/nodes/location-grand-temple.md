# Location: Grand Temple

## Overview
An Isu temple built to save the world from the recurring solar catastrophe. Desmond sacrificed himself here in 2012, stopping the flare but freeing Juno.

## Canon
Climax site of Desmond's arc.

## AU Divergence
Cain SEIZES the Grand Temple at the very start of the story (~75,000 BCE). He digitizes himself here, defeats Juno and deletes Aita in the Temple's network (`event-the-fall-of-juno`), and hides his family's bodies in its deep stasis vault through the First Catastrophe. From then on the Temple is his: base, hiding place, and the machine his long game runs on. (Note: an earlier "the Core" node was an error and has been removed; the Grand Temple is the one and only base.) The tragic canon future still stands on the surface: a man in a hood, Desmond, dies here in 2012, and that is exactly when the sliver of Juno that escaped Cain finally resurfaces (`thread-junos-fragment`).
