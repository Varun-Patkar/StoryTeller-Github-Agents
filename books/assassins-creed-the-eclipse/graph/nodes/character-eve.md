# Character: Eve

## Overview
Born to the Isu scientist Phanes and a human, Eve lacked the neurotransmitters that let the Isu control humans. She led the rebellion beside Adam and is the source of much hybrid Isu DNA in humanity.

## Canon
Legendary mother-figure of the Assassins; her mitochondrial line carries strong Isu DNA (the 'Eve' gene).

## Voice & Mannerisms
Sharp, measured, perceptive. She has the Isu weight of gaze and reads people fast and accurately. Speaks plainly and does not flatter. Believes fiercely in free will and is unimpressed by power for its own sake.
- "Where did you get it." (a statement, not a question)
- "You saved my life, and you enjoyed it."
- "And you know all this, how."

## AU Divergence
An early, wary counterweight to Cain rather than a warm ally. Established in Chapter 2: Cain saves her life by severing an Isu thrall's control mid-strike, but she immediately reads him for what he is, a self-interested wolf who enjoyed winning, not a believer in her cause. She does not trust him and says so. Her belief in free will sits in direct tension with Cain's willingness to use the Apple to puppet people, foreshadowing the Assassin creed he will later refuse to fully embrace. Useful ongoing foil: the person who names Cain's selfishness out loud.

## Notes
Do not soften her into gratitude. She values what Cain can do and distrusts why he does it.
