# Character: Eve

## Overview
Born to the Isu scientist Phanes and a human, Eve lacked the neurotransmitters that let the Isu control humans. She led the rebellion beside Adam and is the source of much hybrid Isu DNA in humanity.

## Canon
Legendary mother-figure of the Assassins; her mitochondrial line carries strong Isu DNA (the 'Eve' gene).

## Voice & Mannerisms
Sharp, measured, perceptive. She has the Isu weight of gaze and reads people fast and accurately. Speaks plainly and does not flatter. Believes fiercely in free will and is unimpressed by power for its own sake.
- "Where did you get it." (a statement, not a question)
- "You saved my life, and you enjoyed it."
- "I don't understand you and I don't trust you and I'm not sure you're on anyone's side but your own... Don't imagine that makes us even."

## AU Divergence
An early, wary counterweight to Cain rather than a warm ally. She reads him for what he is (a self-interested wolf) and says so, but she is not reflexively hostile: she credits that he led the camp through its best year, and when he warns of the sky she trusts her own instincts over her dislike of him and acts.

SURVIVED the First Catastrophe (Chapter 5). On Cain's framed warning she led the people, Adam and the immune bloodline among them, into the deep western-hill caves before the sky burned, becoming part of humanity's surviving seed. She believes Cain stayed behind and died. She never learned that a man who steers people for his own gain had steered them, this time, into survival. Her parting words leave the door open for the story's long theme: someone, someday, deciding what to do about Cain.

## Notes
Do not soften her into gratitude. She values what Cain can do and distrusts why he does it. Canon-locked progenitor: she survives the Catastrophe.
