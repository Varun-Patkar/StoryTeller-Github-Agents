# Character: Bayek

## Overview
Born 85 BCE in Siwa, the last Medjay of Egypt. After the murder of his son Khemu by the Order of the Ancients, he and his wife Aya founded the Hidden Ones, precursor of the Assassin Brotherhood, and devised the Creed.

## Canon
Co-founder of the Hidden Ones; devised the tenets of the Creed.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Grave, protective, principled. KEY quote (embodies Eclipse Balance): 'There is freedom, and there is the life of innocents. Both must be balanced on the scale.'

## AU Divergence
His independently arrived-at philosophy of balance stuns Cain, who realizes the Eclipse's creed can grow organically in worthy people. Cain aids the Hidden Ones' birth without ever revealing himself. Bayek's journey remains fully his own.
