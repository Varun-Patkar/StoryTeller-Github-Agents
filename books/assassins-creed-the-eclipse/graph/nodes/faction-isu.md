# Faction: Isu

## Overview
The technologically advanced precursor species (c. 77,000 BCE) who created humans via Project Anthropos as a controlled workforce, using Pieces of Eden that exploited human neurotransmitters. Their triple-helix DNA gave them a 'sixth sense.' Cities included Atlantis, Eden, and Feyan. Motto: 'We simply came before.'

## Canon
Nearly wiped out with humanity by the First Catastrophe; survivors persist digitally (Juno) or as reincarnated Sages/consciousnesses (Aita, Loki).

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse's true, oldest enemy is not the Assassins or Templars but the surviving Isu remnants. Cain's mission is to erase Isu influence from history entirely.
