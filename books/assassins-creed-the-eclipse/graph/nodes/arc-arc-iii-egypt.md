# Arc III: Egypt

## Overview
The rise of the Hidden Ones under Bayek and Aya. The Eclipse intervenes against Isu influence behind the Order of the Ancients while leaving Bayek's grief and heroism untouched.

## Canon
Set against the canon Egyptian founding of the Hidden Ones.

## AU Divergence
Shows the Eclipse aiding history without stealing it, and links Kassandra's bloodline (via Aya) forward.
