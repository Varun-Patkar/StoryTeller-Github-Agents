# Event: The First Catastrophe

## Overview
A massive coronal mass ejection (solar flare) around 75,000 BCE that devastated Earth, killing most humans and Isu. Jupiter noted 'less than ten thousand of your kind still lived.' Historically misattributed to the Toba supervolcano.

## Canon
The event that ended Isu civilization and nearly ended humanity.

## AU Divergence
Cain knows it cannot be prevented. Instead he infiltrates an Isu city beforehand, uploads into the Core, hides his body, and erases evidence of his survival, letting the world believe he died in the rebellion.

DEPICTED in Chapter 5. The sky "started lying" days before (green auroral wrongness on the north horizon), then opened into fire on the fourth day. Cain survived by upload deep underground. Crucially, using foreknowledge, he steered the rebellion's survival for his own self-interested reasons (a dead world is worthless to a man who plans to outlast it): he sent Adam, Eve, and roughly three hundred people, including the immune hybrid bloodline, into deep western-hill caves before the burn, framing it as strategy rather than revealing the exact truth. That sheltered remnant is humanity's surviving seed (consistent with canon's <10,000 survivors and Adam & Eve as progenitors).

## Notes
Canon-locked: cannot be prevented; Adam and Eve survive. In our story their survival was quietly engineered by Cain out of self-interest.
