# Ability: Echoes

## Overview
Because Cain's true body cannot safely wake, he engineers hybrid bodies called Echoes. Each Echo has exceptionally high Isu DNA (naturally syncs with Pieces of Eden), carries a copied instance of Cain's consciousness, IS Cain while active, and uploads all memories back to the Core on death. Multiple Echoes can operate worldwide at once.

## Canon
INVENTED, grounded in canon Isu genetic engineering and consciousness-transfer tech.

## AU Divergence
The mechanism by which one man accumulates thousands of years of firsthand experience. Crucially, Echoes can die, so stakes remain real; power is breadth of experience, not invincibility.

Seed planted in Chapter 4: during the Isu-city raid, Cain passed a gallery of humans held whole and unmarked in clear columns, the masters' "larder" of spare bodies, and deliberately filed its location. That glimpse of Isu body-stock, alongside the consciousness-transfer vessel he looted, is the origin of the idea that will one day become the Echoes.

## Notes
Echoes come much later (post-Core). Keep the Chapter 4 body-larder as inspiration/seed only, not the finished technique.
