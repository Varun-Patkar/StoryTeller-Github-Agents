# Character: Ezio Auditore

## Overview
Born 1459 in Florence, the Italian Mentor called 'the Prophet.' He opposed the Borgia, recovered Isu artifacts, and delivered Minerva's message meant for Desmond.

## Canon
Ancestor of Desmond; relives no memories himself but is relived by Desmond; unlocked the Vatican Vault.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Warm, charismatic, grows from hot-blooded youth to weary wisdom. 'When I was a young man, I had liberty but I did not see it...' 

## AU Divergence
A key bloodline node: protecting Ezio protects Desmond's future existence, a fact the Eclipse guards jealously.
