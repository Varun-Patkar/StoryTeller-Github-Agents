# Character: Layla Hassan

## Overview
Born 1984, a former Abstergo researcher turned Assassin, called 'Heir of Memories.' She wielded the Staff of Hermes after Kassandra, freed Basim in 2020, and later sacrificed herself into the Grey alongside the Reader (Desmond).

## Canon
Relived Kassandra's and others' memories; her choices freed Loki into the modern world.

## AU Divergence
Her freeing of Basim triggers the climax of the Game of Gods. The Eclipse must decide whether to stop her, and lives with the consequences of not doing so in time.
