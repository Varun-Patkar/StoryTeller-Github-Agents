# Event: The Digitization

## Overview
Shortly before the First Catastrophe, Cain infiltrates the Grand Temple beneath what will become New York and uses its consciousness-transfer machine on himself. Crucially he does NOT abandon his body: he runs a digital COPY into the Temple's network while keeping his original flesh at the controls. This dual existence (a ghost in the machine plus a hand on the switches) is what lets him defeat Juno (see `event-the-fall-of-juno`). He then hides all four family bodies in the Temple's deep stasis vault, powers his digital self down on a one-year timer to ride out the flare, and sets himself to wake into an ash world he now owns.

## Canon
INVENTED, using canon Isu digital-consciousness technology as precedent.

## AU Divergence
The act that makes Cain effectively immortal. There is NO separate "Core"; he simply seizes the Grand Temple itself. The dual-body method (light + flesh at once) is his signature edge: the surviving Isu poured themselves fully into the grey and so meet each other as equals, while Cain kept a hand in the physical world no ghost could touch.
