# Event: Cain's Awakening

## Overview
In the instant Cain is about to kill his brother, the modern superfan's consciousness seizes control of Cain's body, bringing full knowledge of Assassin's Creed history, the Isu, Pieces of Eden, and both Catastrophes. His hand stops; Abel is spared. Holding the Apple, Cain also receives a vision of the First Catastrophe.

The true beginning of the story. His foreknowledge is his only real advantage, and it immediately turns him from a jealous fratricide into a planner racing a doomsday clock.
