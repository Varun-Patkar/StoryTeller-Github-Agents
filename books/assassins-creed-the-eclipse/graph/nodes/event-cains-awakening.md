# Event: Cain's Awakening

## Overview
Immediately after murdering Abel, the modern superfan's consciousness seizes control of Cain's body, bringing full knowledge of Assassin's Creed history, the Isu, Pieces of Eden, and both Catastrophes.

## Canon
INVENTED.

## AU Divergence
The true beginning of the story. His foreknowledge is his only advantage, and his guilt his only master.

Established in Chapter 1: the awakened, foreign consciousness inside Cain can resist the Isu neurotransmitter control leash. Where every other enslaved human obeys the masters' commands without knowing they obey, Cain can feel the leash pull and refuse it (at a physical cost: pressure behind the eyes, nausea, the taste of metal). This refusal is what first draws Isu attention to him. He keeps the Apple from his masters and flees toward Adam and Eve rather than deliver it. He has NOT yet defected formally or uploaded (those come later).

## Notes
Depicted in Chapter 1 ("The Weight of the Apple"). Hard rule reaffirmed on-page: the Apple of Eden commands only the living and cannot revive or undo the death of Abel. Cain grasps this and it becomes the root of his mission.
