# Arc II: Greece

## Overview
Cain's first Echo walks the world during the Peloponnesian War. He meets Kassandra, earns her trust, dismantles Isu-corrupted power (Cult of Kosmos), and formally founds the Eclipse.

## Canon
Set against the canon Greek era (Kassandra, Cult of Kosmos, Atlantis).

## AU Divergence
Births the Eclipse and the central romance.
