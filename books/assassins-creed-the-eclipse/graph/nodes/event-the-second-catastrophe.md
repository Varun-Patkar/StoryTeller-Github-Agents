# Event: The Second Catastrophe

## Overview
The recurrence of the catastrophic solar event that the Isu (and later the Grand Temple) tried to guard against. Preventing or surviving it is the axis of the modern-day story.

## Canon
The looming threat behind the Grand Temple and Desmond's sacrifice.

## AU Divergence
Cain's entire existence is oriented around this: 'To ensure humanity never again falls before the Sun.' The Eclipse's ultimate purpose.
