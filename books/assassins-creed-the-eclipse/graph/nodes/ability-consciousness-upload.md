# Ability: Consciousness Upload

## Overview
The Isu technology that allows a mind to be preserved and run within a digital network (the Grey, and Cain's Core). Used by Juno, Aita's Sage process, and Cain himself.

## Canon
Canon tech (Juno in the Grey; the Sage/Mead process; Aita's transfer attempts).

## AU Divergence
Cain's mastery of this tech, refined over millennia, exceeds the surviving Isu's, which is one of his few decisive advantages over Juno and Loki.

Established in Chapter 4: Cain raided a lightly-held Isu city's consciousness-transfer facility (a small vault with a "vessel," a basin of light that reads a whole mind and writes it somewhere a body isn't) and took the *knowing* of how it works, the map to build his own version deep underground (the Core). He did not upload yet (that is Chapter 5). At its heart the tech does one clean thing: lift a mind out of a failing body and set it down somewhere that does not fail.

**Exclusivity (user-locked):** Cain intends to upload ONLY himself. His whole goal is to become the deathless, marked Cain of legend, the killer who cannot be killed, immortal and alone. He will not grant true consciousness-immortality to anyone else. Others he values (like Nara) he may save physically, but the vessel has room for exactly one, and the one is always him. This is by design, not limitation.

## Notes
Keep the upload as Cain-only. Physical rescue of others is a separate, lesser thing and is allowed. The "deathless marked Cain" motive is central to his character.
