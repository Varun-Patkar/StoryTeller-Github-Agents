# Arc I: The First Rebellion

## Overview
Human-Isu War; Cain awakens after murdering Abel; allies with Adam and Eve; the rebellion; the upload into the Core; the First Catastrophe. Ends with humanity believing Cain dead and history remembering only Abel's murderer.

## Canon
Built atop canon First Civilization / rebellion / Catastrophe lore.

## AU Divergence
Establishes Cain, his guilt, his foreknowledge, and the Core.
