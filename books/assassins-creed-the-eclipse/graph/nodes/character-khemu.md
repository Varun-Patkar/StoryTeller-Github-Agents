# Character: Khemu

## Overview
Bayek's young son, accidentally killed during a confrontation with the Order of the Ancients in 49 BCE.

## Canon
His death is the inciting tragedy of Bayek's crusade.

## AU Divergence
A tragedy Cain deliberately does NOT prevent, because Bayek's grief forges the Hidden Ones. A hard line the Eclipse will not cross: some pain is load-bearing for history.
