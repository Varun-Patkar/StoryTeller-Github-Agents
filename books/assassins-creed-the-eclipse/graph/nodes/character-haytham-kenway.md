# Character: Haytham Kenway

## Overview
Born 1725, son of Edward and Tessa. Raised by Templars after Edward's murder, he became Grand Master of the Colonial Rite. Father of Connor; killed by Connor's blade in 1781.

## Canon
Charismatic Templar who genuinely wanted order and once hoped to unite Assassins and Templars.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Elegant, cutting, weary idealism turned to control. 'I would have shown you wonders.'

## AU Divergence
A perfect Eclipse candidate philosophically (he sought a synthesis of order and freedom). Whether Cain can reach him before Connor's blade is a central tension of the Kenway sub-arc.
