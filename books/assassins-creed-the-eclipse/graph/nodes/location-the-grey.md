# Location: The Grey

## Overview
The Isu digital realm, a network where consciousnesses can persist after death. Juno survived here; Basim/Loki was imprisoned here as 'Yggdrasil'; Desmond and Layla end up here.

## Canon
Central to the modern-day metaphysics of the setting.

## AU Divergence
The public, corrupted cousin of Cain's private Core. Cain navigates the Grey to hunt digital Isu, always risking exposure of his own similar existence.
