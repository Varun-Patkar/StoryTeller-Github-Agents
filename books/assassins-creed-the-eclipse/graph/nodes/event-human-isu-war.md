# Event: Human-Isu War

## Overview
The decade-long uprising of humanity against Isu masters, led by Adam and Eve with a stolen Apple. Humanity technically won, but the war coincided with the coming Catastrophe.

## Canon
Foundational conflict of the setting.

## AU Divergence
Cain fights on the human side after awakening, steering the rebellion while secretly preparing for the catastrophe he knows is coming.
