# Item: Staff of Hermes Trismegistus

## Overview
A powerful Piece of Eden forged around 2200 IE for Hermes, granting immortality, regeneration, energy projection, and command of Isu systems. It stores Aletheia's consciousness. Wielders: Hermes, Pythagoras, Kassandra (c. 421 BCE-2018), Layla (2018-2020), then Basim.

## Canon
Source of Kassandra's and Pythagoras's longevity; used to seal Atlantis and later to free Basim.

## AU Divergence
The mechanism that makes a 2,000-year romance possible. Cain's relationship with the Staff is fraught: it keeps Kassandra alive, but its passage to Basim helps free his greatest enemy.
