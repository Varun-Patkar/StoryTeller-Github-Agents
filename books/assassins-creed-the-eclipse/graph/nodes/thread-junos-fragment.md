# Thread: Juno's Fragment

## Overview
When Cain purges Juno from the Grand Temple in 75,000 BCE, a small, cold, patient sliver of her escapes into the Temple's deepest systems and hides, folding itself small and going still. Cain knows it is loose, cannot follow it, and files it as a problem for later.

## Canon
INVENTED as the in-story cause of Juno's canon persistence. Reconciles the AU (Cain takes the Temple and beats her in Arc I) with canon (Juno is present in the Grand Temple and is freed in 2012).

## AU Divergence
The one piece of the first world's cruelty that Cain failed to catch. It waits out the entire span of human history and finally resurfaces when Desmond dies at the Temple in 2012 (`event-desmonds-sacrifice`). Much of the Eclipse's modern-era work is, secretly, cleanup for this single failure. Payoff target: Arc VII / VIII.

## Notes
Time-sensitive: dormant from 75,000 BCE until 2012. Keep it entirely off-page for most of the story; its existence is Cain's private debt, not public knowledge.
