# Arc IV: The Long Silence

## Overview
A montage-scale arc spanning thousands of years: the Eclipse grows, recovers Isu knowledge, expands across civilizations, recruits exceptional individuals, and builds hidden sanctuaries beneath the world.

## Canon
Bridges the ancient and medieval/early-modern eras.

## AU Divergence
Demonstrates the scale of Cain's patience and the Eclipse's reach; seeds figures paid off in Arc V.
