# Item: Spear of Leonidas

## Overview
A broken spear of Isu origin, heirloom of Leonidas, wielded by Kassandra. It grants enhanced abilities and connection to Isu tech.

## Canon
Kassandra's signature weapon and family relic.

## AU Divergence
One of the first Isu artifacts Cain sees in worthy hands rather than corrupting ones, part of why he trusts Kassandra.
