# Character: Edward Kenway

## Overview
Born 1693 in Wales, a privateer-turned-pirate who fell into the Assassin-Templar conflict and captained the Jackdaw. Father of Jennifer Scott (with Caroline) and Haytham (with Tessa).

## Canon
Historically believed murdered in 1735 in London. Father of the Kenway line that runs to Connor and ultimately Desmond.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Roguish, quick, haunted by lost chances. 'Perhaps there's a middle ground... I didn't see it before.'

## AU Divergence
PRIMARY FIX-IT: instead of dying in 1735, Edward is secretly rescued by the Eclipse after his apparent death. He later joins willingly once shown Caroline was also saved. The Kenway bloodline becomes one of the Eclipse's greatest families.
