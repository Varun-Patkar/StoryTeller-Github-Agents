# Character: Alexios

## Overview
Kassandra's sibling, raised and warped by the Cult of Kosmos into their enforcer 'Deimos.'

## Canon
Can be redeemed or lost depending on the player's Greek journey; carries strong Isu DNA like Kassandra.

## AU Divergence
A quiet 'fix-it' target: Cain and Kassandra work to pull him back from the Cult without erasing his struggle.
