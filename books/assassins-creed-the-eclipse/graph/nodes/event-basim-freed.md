# Event: Basim Freed

## Overview
In 2020 Layla Hassan, wielding the Staff of Hermes, frees Basim (Loki) from his imprisonment in the Grey/Yggdrasil.

## Canon
Sets up the modern return of Loki as a major player.

## AU Divergence
The detonation point of the Game of Gods arc. The Eclipse's failure to prevent it forces the final confrontation with Loki.
