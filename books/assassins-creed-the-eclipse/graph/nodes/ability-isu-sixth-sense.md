# Ability: Isu Sixth Sense

## Overview
The heightened perception and analytical sense granted by Isu triple-helix DNA, which manifests in high-DNA humans as Eagle Vision and related gifts.

## Canon
The precursor of the Assassins' Eagle Vision; tied to Isu 'Knowledge.'

## AU Divergence
Cain's Echoes, engineered for maximal Isu DNA, wield refined forms of this sense, letting them read rooms, lies, and battlefields with uncanny clarity.
