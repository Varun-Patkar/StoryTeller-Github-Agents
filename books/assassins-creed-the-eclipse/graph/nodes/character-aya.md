# Character: Aya

## Overview
Bayek's wife and co-founder of the Hidden Ones, later known to the Brotherhood as Amunet. Descendant of Kassandra's line through Elpidios.

## Canon
Establishes the Roman bureau of the Hidden Ones; revered founder-figure.

## AU Divergence
Living proof that Cain's Greek investment paid off across centuries: Kassandra's bloodline seeds the Assassins themselves.
