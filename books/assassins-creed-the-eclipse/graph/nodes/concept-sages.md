# Concept: Sages

## Overview
Humans born bearing the face and memories of the Isu Aita, produced by Juno and Odin's 'Mead' process after Aita's failed transfer. They recur throughout history (e.g., Desmond's son Elijah).

## Canon
A recurring identity that surfaces across eras, always Aita.

## AU Divergence
A slow-burn threat and mystery the Eclipse tracks: each Sage is a potential vector for Aita's or Juno's influence.
