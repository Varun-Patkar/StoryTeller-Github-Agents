# Character: Juno

## Overview
An Illuminat-caste Isu born in Feyan, wife of Aita. She survived as a digital consciousness in the Grand Temple network, was briefly freed in 2012, and schemes to subjugate humanity and restore Isu dominance.

## Canon
Trapped in the Grey/Grand Temple; freed by Desmond's sacrifice in 2012; her Instruments of the First Will worship her; her physical clone is destroyed in 2018 in Gramatica's Australian lab.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Imperious, seductive, contemptuous of humans as beasts. 'You are cattle who learned to wear crowns.' / 'I was here before your first word. I will be here after your last.'

## AU Divergence
Cain's oldest and most dangerous foe. Their conflict spans from the First Rebellion to the modern day. The Eclipse works for millennia to ensure her freedom, if it comes, is short-lived.
