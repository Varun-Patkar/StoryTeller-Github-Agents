# Character: Juno

## Overview
An Illuminat-caste Isu born in Feyan, wife of Aita. She survived as a digital consciousness in the Grand Temple network, was briefly freed in 2012, and schemes to subjugate humanity and restore Isu dominance.

## Canon
Trapped in the Grey/Grand Temple; freed by Desmond's sacrifice in 2012; her Instruments of the First Will worship her; her physical clone is destroyed in 2018 in Gramatica's Australian lab.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Imperious, seductive, contemptuous of humans as beasts. 'You are cattle who learned to wear crowns.' / 'I was here before your first word. I will be here after your last.'

## AU Divergence
Cain's oldest and most dangerous foe, and their war STARTS in Arc I, not the modern day. When Cain digitizes into the Grand Temple (~75,000 BCE) he finds Juno already poured into its network. She cannot control him (he is Apple-immune even as light) and she nearly erases his digital self in the grey, until his physical body dismantles her domain switch by switch and corners her. Cain purges most of her, but a cold, patient SLIVER of Juno slips into the Temple's deepest systems and hides (`thread-junos-fragment`). That surviving fragment is what canon later sees: it waits 75,000 years and finally resurfaces when Desmond dies here in 2012. The Eclipse's whole long game is, in part, cleanup for the one piece of her Cain failed to catch.
