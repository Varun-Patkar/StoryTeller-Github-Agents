# Character: Abel

## Overview
One of the first humans, brother to Cain. In legend he is slain by Cain, who takes his Apple of Eden.

## Canon
Son of Adam and Eve; killed by Cain, the first murder in human history.

## AU Divergence
Killed by our Cain while still Isu-enslaved (before the modern mind awakens). His death is the wound that defines Cain's entire mission. Cain can never undo it, which is why he saves everyone else he can.
