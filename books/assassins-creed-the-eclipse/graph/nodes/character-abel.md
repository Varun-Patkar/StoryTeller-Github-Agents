# Character: Abel

## Overview
One of the first humans, brother to Cain, son of Adam and Eve. In legend he is slain by Cain. In this story he is knocked unconscious by a jealous Cain who is stopped, at the blade, by the reincarnation, and survives.

## Canon
Son of Adam and Eve; in the legend, killed by Cain in the first murder in human history.

## AU Divergence
Abel LIVES. Cain, mid-fratricide, is interrupted by the modern mind arriving and lowers the blade. Abel wakes with only a lump on his head, unaware how close he came (he was knocked out before the knife). His survival opens the door to a family that endures the Catastrophe together rather than a lone wound driving Cain. He is an Apple-immune hybrid like Cain, one of the overseers.
