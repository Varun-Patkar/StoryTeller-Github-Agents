# Faction: Templar Order

## Overview
The order that pursues peace through control and order, descended from the Order of the Ancients. Front organization in the modern day is Abstergo Industries.

## Canon
Some members call themselves 'Children of Cain'; seek Pieces of Eden to guide humanity.

## AU Divergence
The other visible faction. Ironically they venerate 'Cain' as a founding symbol, never knowing the real Cain runs a third order that opposes their absolutism.
