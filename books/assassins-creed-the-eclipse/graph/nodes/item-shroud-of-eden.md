# Item: Shroud of Eden

## Overview
A Piece of Eden capable of healing grievous wounds and prolonging life. Critically, it cannot truly resurrect the dead, it can only reanimate a corpse imperfectly.

## Canon
Sought across eras for its healing power.

## AU Divergence
Central to the Eclipse's 'fix-it' rescues: it can save the nearly-dead (Edward, Caroline) but its inability to revive the truly dead is the hard rule that keeps rescues earned, not miraculous. Abel stays dead; the Shroud cannot undo that.
