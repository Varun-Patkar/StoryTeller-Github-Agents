# Faction: Human Rebellion

## Overview
The uprising led by the immune hybrids Adam and Eve during the Human-Isu War (c. 75,010-75,000 BCE), fought with a stolen Apple of Eden.

## Canon
Technically won, but at the cost of civilization when the Catastrophe struck mid-conflict.

## AU Divergence
Cain defects to the Rebellion after awakening and helps it succeed, all while knowing it precedes near-extinction.
