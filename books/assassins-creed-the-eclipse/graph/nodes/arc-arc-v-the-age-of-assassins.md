# Arc V: The Age of Assassins

## Overview
During the classic Assassin eras, the Eclipse quietly prevents greater catastrophes while letting history unfold. Hidden fix-it missions save lives (Edward, Caroline) without erasing the protagonists' struggles. Readers slowly realize many famous tragedies were never quite what history claimed.

## Canon
Overlays the canon games' eras.

## AU Divergence
The heart of the fix-it-in-disguise premise; the Kenway rescues are the centerpiece.
