# Character: Pythagoras

## Overview
The philosopher-mathematician, secretly Kassandra's biological father and a prior wielder of the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus who used it to seal Atlantis.

## Canon
Kept the Staff for centuries, sealing the Atlantis vault; passed the burden to Kassandra.

## AU Divergence
A precedent Cain studies: a mortal made near-immortal by an Isu artifact, foreshadowing what Kassandra becomes and what the Eclipse can offer chosen members.
