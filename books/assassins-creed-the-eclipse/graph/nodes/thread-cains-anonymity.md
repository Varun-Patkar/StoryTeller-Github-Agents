# Thread: Cain's Anonymity

## Overview
Cain deliberately keeps himself out of history. His only recorded act is the first murder. This anonymity protects him from Abstergo and the Isu, but also functions as self-imposed penance: he saves the world and lets it hate the one thing it knows of him.
