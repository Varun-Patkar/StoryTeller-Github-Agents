# Event: Desmond's Sacrifice

## Overview
In 2012 Desmond Miles chooses to touch the Isu pedestal at the Grand Temple, sacrificing his life to stop a second solar flare, an act that also releases Juno's consciousness.

## Canon
Climax of the modern-day storyline through 2012.

## AU Divergence
The Eclipse foresaw this for millennia. Arc VII wrestles with whether Cain should intervene, and the terrible cost of either choice.
