# Character: Jupiter

## Overview
Member of the Capitoline Triad who, with Minerva and Juno, labored to save both species from the coming solar catastrophe. He recorded the message eventually seen by Desmond.

## Canon
Leader-figure of the Isu who calculates the Catastrophe and searches for a solution; quote: 'less than ten thousand of your kind still lived.'

## Voice & Mannerisms
Weary authority. Speaks in the cadence of a scientist watching the end of the world approach. 'We tried everything. We failed. And still we tried.'

## AU Divergence
Cain, hidden in an Isu city, secretly observes the Triad's doomed attempts, learning what NOT to repeat when he prepares humanity for the Second Catastrophe.
