# Faction: Abstergo Industries

## Overview
The multinational corporation that is the modern face of the Templar Order, developer of the Animus used to read genetic memories.

## Canon
Pursues Pieces of Eden and ancestral knowledge; captured Desmond and Layla-era subjects.

## AU Divergence
Despite its vast resources, Abstergo can never prove the Eclipse exists, because the Eclipse edits the very Animus-readable DNA records Abstergo relies on.
