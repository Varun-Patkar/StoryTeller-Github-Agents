# Event: Abel's Sparing

## Overview
A jealous Cain knocks his brother Abel unconscious and, holding a stolen Apple of Eden in one hand and a knapped stone blade at Abel's throat in the other, is about to kill him. In that instant the modern superfan's consciousness seizes control, staying his hand. Abel is spared and survives.

## Canon
In legend this is the first murder (Cain kills Abel for the Apple). This story diverges: the killing is interrupted at the blade.

## AU Divergence
The inciting event, reframed from a murder into a near-murder that does NOT happen. Abel lives. The Apple, in Cain's grip, triggers both the mind-merge and a vision of the coming First Catastrophe, which redirects Cain from petty fratricide toward a 75,000-year survival plan. The old "first murderer" legend now has no literal basis in-story; see the open thread on `character-cain` for how (or whether) that legend still forms.

## Notes
Hard continuity: Abel is knocked out BEFORE the blade, so he never sees the knife and does not know how close he came. The Apple is in Cain's hand, not Abel's (matches the prologue and chapter 1).
