# Faction: The Eclipse

## Overview
A secret organization founded by Cain tens of thousands of years before the Assassins or Templars. To outsiders it is known only through rumor as 'The Owls.' It pursues Balance, neither Freedom nor Order, and works to preserve human continuity, erase surviving Isu influence, and prepare humanity for the Second Catastrophe.

## Canon
INVENTED for this story. No canon organization predates the Isu-era with this exact remit, though it is designed to slot invisibly behind known events.

## AU Divergence
Creed: 'No truth is absolute. No order eternal.' Symbol: an owl before an eclipsed sun. Recruits by character alone (former Assassins, Templars, pirates, kings, scholars). Grants extended lifespans via refined Isu biotech. Possesses tech to alter genetic/Animus records, fabricate remains, and hide bloodlines, which is why Abstergo never proves it exists.
