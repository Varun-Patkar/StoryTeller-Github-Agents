# Location: The Observatory

## Overview
An Isu facility in Jamaica built by Aita, capable of viewing through the eyes of anyone whose blood is sampled. Site of his failed transfer experiment.

## Canon
A coveted precursor site pursued by pirates, Assassins, and Templars alike (Edward's era).

## AU Divergence
Dangerous surveillance tech the Eclipse wants secured or destroyed, and a mirror of Cain's own upload, tempting and cautionary.
