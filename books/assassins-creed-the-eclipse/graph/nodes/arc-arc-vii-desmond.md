# Arc VII: Desmond

## Overview
The modern Assassin storyline. The Eclipse has protected Desmond since birth without his knowledge, ensuring he reaches the Grand Temple. Cain wrestles with the cost of Desmond's foreseen sacrifice.

## Canon
Overlays Desmond's canon 2007-2012 arc.

## AU Divergence
Personalizes the fix-it dilemma: some sacrifices may be necessary even for a god of preparation.
