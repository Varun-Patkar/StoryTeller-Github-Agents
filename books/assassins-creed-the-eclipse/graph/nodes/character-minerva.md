# Character: Minerva

## Overview
Member of the Capitoline Triad who left holographic messages across time to warn future humans of the returning catastrophe and of Juno's threat.

## Canon
Appears to Ezio to deliver a message meant for Desmond; opposes Juno.

## AU Divergence
A distant, respected adversary-turned-tacit-ally. Cain sometimes preserves or hides her messages so they reach the right people at the right time.
