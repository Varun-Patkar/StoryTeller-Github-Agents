# Character: Connor

## Overview
Born 1756, a Kanien'keha:ka (Mohawk) raised as Ratonhnhake:ton, son of Haytham Kenway and Kaniehti:io. Became a Master Assassin during the American Revolution.

## Canon
Killed his father Haytham; fought Templars through the Revolution; ancestor of Desmond.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Earnest, stubborn, morally absolute in youth. 'I will not compromise. Not for you, not for anyone.'

## AU Divergence
With Edward secretly alive within the Eclipse, three generations, Edward, Haytham, Connor, could operate as legendary Eclipse operatives while history still records their public tragedy.
