# Item: Apple of Eden

## Overview
A Piece of Eden capable of controlling minds and projecting illusions, exploiting the neurotransmitters the Isu built into humans. Made of Isu 'adamant.'

## Canon
Stolen by Adam and Eve; Cain murders Abel for one; sought by Assassins and Templars throughout history.

## AU Divergence
The literal object of the first murder. Securing loose Apples is a perpetual Eclipse mission.
