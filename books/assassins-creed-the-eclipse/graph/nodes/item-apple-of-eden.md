# Item: Apple of Eden

## Overview
A Piece of Eden capable of controlling minds and projecting illusions, exploiting the neurotransmitters the Isu built into humans. Made of Isu 'adamant.'

## Canon
Stolen by Adam and Eve; Cain murders Abel for one; sought by Assassins and Templars throughout history.

## AU Divergence
The Apple Cain took from Abel. In our story it is the foundation of his early power. Established in Chapter 2: Cain uses it to sever the Isu's neurotransmitter "leashes" on enslaved humans and to seize control of thralls himself, turning the masters' own tools against them. Rules established on-page:
- It works only on the leashed (humans with the Anthropos neurotransmitters). It cannot touch the immune (Adam, Eve, other hybrids).
- Severing/seizing a leash costs Cain physically (nosebleed, near-blackout, tunnel vision). It is a knife, not a hammer.
- At long range the controlling Isu can contest him; up close, for a few seconds, Cain can out-muscle a distant handler because he commits everything. He cannot win a sustained long-range tug of war.
- It cannot revive the dead (Chapter 1).

## Notes
This leash-cutting/seizing is Cain's signature early technique. Keep the physical cost and range limits consistent so his power stays earned, not invincible.
