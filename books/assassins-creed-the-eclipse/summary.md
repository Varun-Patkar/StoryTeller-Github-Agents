# Story Summary: Assassin's Creed: The Eclipse

## Arc I: The First Rebellion

- **Chapter 1 (The Weight of the Apple)**: Near the Isu towers on the plain of Eden (c. 75,000 BCE), the modern superfan's consciousness awakens inside Cain one heartbeat too late, his brother Abel already dead by his hand and Abel's Apple of Eden clutched in his fist. Reeling with guilt and flooded with foreknowledge of Assassin's Creed history, Cain discovers his awakened mind can resist the Isu neurotransmitter leash: he refuses the command to carry the Apple to his masters, is noticed by a watching Isu, and flees. Realizing the Apple commands only the living and can never undo Abel's death, he makes his first true choice, keeping the stolen Apple from the Isu and setting out north to find the two humans who defied them and lived: Adam and Eve.
