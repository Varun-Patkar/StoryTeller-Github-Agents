# Story Summary: Assassin's Creed: The Eclipse

## Arc I: The First Rebellion

- **Prologue (The Cost of Living)**: In 2021, Cain Mercer, an orphan-turned-near-billionaire CEO who is universally liked but friendless, plays Assassin's Creed Valhalla and idolizes the deathless Caine from Vampire: The Masquerade. Dying of self-neglect (a heart worn out by chronic overwork and a cardiology appointment he postponed eleven times), he realizes his one unmaintained asset was his own body, and wishes to be Cain. An old voice answers "So be it." He reincarnates into the biblical Cain in 75,000 BCE, standing over his unconscious brother Abel with a stolen Apple of Eden in one hand and a stone blade at Abel's throat.
- **Chapter 1 (The Wrong Cain)**: The two minds merge. Cain realizes he got the wrong Cain (mortal, not the immortal one he wished for) and that there is no god here to reward fratricide. The Apple shows him a vision of the coming First Catastrophe (a solar flare that will burn the Isu and leave under ten thousand humans alive) and of the Isu who cheat death by uploading into machines and meddle across all of history (Juno, Aita's Sages, Odin, Loki, Persephone), plus Desmond's 2012 sacrifice. He resolves to do the same trick, become a deathless data-being, and save himself and his family. He lowers the blade and spares Abel as his parents' dust appears on the trail home.
- **Chapter 2 (The Hardest Pitch of Two Lives)**: Adam and Eve return from the war; Abel wakes and tattles that Cain hit him (he never saw the knife). The parents notice Cain has changed. Cain convinces them of the vision (they believe because the Apple did something beyond their understanding), overriding Adam's wish for the boys to inherit the war. He lays out the survival plan: don't fight the fire, copy the Isu trick first and better. The plan splits the family, they go north for the reincarnation engine, Cain goes west to the deepest vault, and they agree.
- **Chapter 3 (Into the Dark)**: They split at the coast. Cain crosses to the Grand Temple beneath future New York (navigating by map-shape), infiltrates it past its caretaker using his Apple-immunity and foreknowledge, and digitizes himself with a twist: he keeps his body and runs a copy, refusing to become a mere ghost. In the network he finds Juno and Aita already there. Juno can't control him and nearly erases his digital self, but his physical body dismantles her domain switch by switch; the two Cains corner her. He purges most of Juno (a cold sliver escapes into the deep systems, a debt that will resurface at Desmond's 2012 death), deletes Aita entirely (not realizing the Sage seed was already loose), and seizes the whole Temple. The family returns from the north; Cain freezes all four bodies in the vault, powers his digital self down on a one-year timer as the sky burns, and resolves to later hide everything behind a self-made legend (the jealous murderer cursed to walk alone).
