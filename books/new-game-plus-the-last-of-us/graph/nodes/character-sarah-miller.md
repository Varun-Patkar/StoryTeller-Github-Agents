# Character: Sarah Miller

## Overview
Joel's daughter. In canon, she dies on Outbreak Day at age 12, shot by a FEDRA soldier. In this story, the MC saves her — she survives but the bullet wound leaves her unable to have children. She becomes the MC's eventual love interest and co-leader of the community.

## Personality (Canon + Extrapolation)
- **Caring and warm**: Buys Joel a birthday watch with her own money; worries about her dad
- **Brave**: Doesn't panic during the initial outbreak; tries to stay calm
- **Mature for her age**: Takes care of Joel in small ways (making breakfast, managing the house)
- **Sarcastic humor**: Apple doesn't fall far from the tree; has Joel's dry wit
- **Resourceful**: Grows up in the apocalypse and becomes capable and tough
- **Emotional core**: Her near-death and survival shapes her entire arc — she has survivor's guilt and a fierce attachment to those she loves
- **Cannot have children**: This becomes a source of quiet pain that she processes over time; she and the MC eventually adopt orphans

## Age Timeline in Our Story
- **2013 (Outbreak Day)**: 12 years old
- **2018 (5 years later)**: 17 — starting to become a capable survivor
- **2023 (10 years later)**: 22 — fully grown, seasoned apocalypse survivor
- **2033 (20 years later)**: 32 — community co-leader, love interest arc established
- Romance with MC should begin when Sarah is an adult (age 20+)

## Abilities
- **Survival skills**: Learned out of necessity; hunting, foraging, basic medicine
- **Marksmanship**: Joel would have taught her (or the MC does)
- **Community leadership**: Becomes the diplomatic/organizational half while MC is the combat/resource half
- **Soccer**: Pre-outbreak hobby; maybe she organizes games for community kids

## Relationships
- **Joel (father)**: Core relationship. Separated on Outbreak Day; reunion is a massive emotional payoff. Joel will be overwhelmed to find her alive
- **Tommy (uncle)**: Close before the outbreak; reunion similarly emotional
- **The MC**: Starts as "the stranger who saved my life." Evolves through gratitude → friendship → partnership → romance. She's drawn to the MC's optimism and strength
- **Ellie**: Adoptive mother/older sister figure. ~20-year age gap. Sarah relates to Ellie's toughness and humor
- **Community**: Beloved by the settlement; she's the human face of leadership while the MC is the muscle

## Voice & Mannerisms
- **Pre-outbreak quotes** (canon): "I know. That's why it's hard to buy for." (about Joel's birthday)
- Dry, understated humor inherited from Joel
- As an adult: practical, no-nonsense, but deeply caring underneath
- Calls Joel "Dad" or "Daddy" (early); "Dad" (later)
- When angry: quiet and cold rather than loud — she inherited Joel's intensity
- Comforting others: touches shoulders, squeezes hands — physical reassurance
- When scared: hides it behind action ("What do we do next?" instead of "I'm scared")

## Key Canon Quotes (adapted for reference)
- "Dad? What's going on out there?" (Outbreak Night)
- "I'm scared, daddy." (fleeing Austin)
- "I know. That's why it's hard to buy for." (about Joel's watch gift)

## Arc in Our Story
1. **Outbreak Day**: Shot, wounded, left for dead by Joel/Tommy (who believe she's dead). MC finds and saves her
2. **Recovery**: Healing from the gunshot wound; learning the new reality
3. **Growing up in the apocalypse**: Becomes tough and capable alongside the MC
4. **Finding her purpose**: Helping build the community; becoming the heart of the settlement
5. **Reunion with Joel**: One of the story's biggest emotional payoffs
6. **Romance with MC**: Natural evolution from survival partners to something deeper
7. **Adopting orphans & Ellie**: Finding motherhood through choice, not biology
