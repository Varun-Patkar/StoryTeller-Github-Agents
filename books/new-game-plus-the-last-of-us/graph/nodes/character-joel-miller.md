# Character: Joel Miller

## Overview
The protagonist of the original The Last of Us. A former contractor from Austin, Texas, who lost his daughter Sarah on Outbreak Day. In canon, this loss defines him — he becomes a hardened, emotionally closed-off smuggler for 20 years before meeting Ellie. In our story, Sarah's survival (which he doesn't know about) changes his trajectory somewhat, but he still suffers from believing she's dead.

## Personality
- **Hardened survivor**: 20 years of apocalypse have made him ruthless when necessary
- **Emotionally guarded**: Sarah's "death" broke something in him; he keeps people at arm's length
- **Protective to a fault**: When he does care about someone, he'll burn the world down for them
- **Practical and resourceful**: Former contractor; good with his hands, knows how to fix and build things
- **Quiet intensity**: Doesn't talk much; when he does, it matters
- **Violent when provoked**: Has done terrible things to survive (ambushing, torturing for information)
- **Southern charm (buried)**: Texas background; occasionally surfaces in softer moments
- **Musical**: Plays guitar; taught Ellie to play. Loves country and folk music

## Abilities
- **Expert combatant**: Melee and firearms; brutal, efficient, no wasted movement
- **Stealth**: Years of smuggling taught him to move quietly
- **Crafting**: Makes shivs, molotovs, nail bombs, med kits from scavenged materials
- **Interrogation**: Knows how to extract information through violence (taught Ellie this too)
- **Construction**: Pre-outbreak contractor skills; valuable for community building
- **Teaching**: Patient instructor when he wants to be (taught Ellie shooting, swimming, guitar)

## Relationships
- **Sarah (daughter)**: Believed dead for 20 years. Her "death" is the wound that defines him. Finding her alive would be the most emotionally devastating (in a good way) moment of his life
- **Tommy (brother)**: Fell out over the Fireflies; Tommy believed in the cause, Joel didn't. Reconciled by Part I. Tommy runs Jackson
- **Tess (partner)**: Smuggling partner in Boston; romantic undertones. She dies early in Part I
- **Ellie (surrogate daughter)**: The girl who breaks through his emotional walls. He'd kill for her (and does)
- **Marlene**: Firefly leader he killed to save Ellie
- **Abby Anderson**: Daughter of the surgeon Joel killed; she kills Joel in canon. In our story, the MC prevents this

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Texas drawl; drops g's ("somethin'", "nothin'", "goin'")
- Short, clipped sentences when tense
- Uses "kiddo" for people he cares about
- Endearment through gruffness: caring expressed as protectiveness, not words
- When emotional: voice goes quiet, jaw clenches, looks away
- Dark humor when comfortable: "You gonna shoot that thing or get it pregnant?"
- Teaching tone: patient but firm: "A deep breath in, slow breath out. You squeeze the trigger."

## Arc in Our Story
1. **Outbreak Day**: Escapes Austin with Tommy, believing Sarah is dead
2. **20 Years of Hardening**: Becomes the Joel we know — smuggler, survivor, closed-off
3. **Canon events with Ellie**: May proceed similarly or be altered by MC's community's influence
4. **Finding Sarah Alive**: THE emotional climax. Joel discovers his daughter has been alive for years, raised by the MC in a thriving community. This shatters and rebuilds him simultaneously
5. **Joining the Community**: Joel brings his skills (construction, combat) and his family (Ellie) to the MC's settlement
6. **Peace**: For the first time in 20 years, Joel has something to live for beyond mere survival
7. **Protected from Abby**: MC's meta-knowledge prevents the golf club scene; Joel gets to live

## Relevance to Our Story
Joel is the emotional anchor of the original TLOU. In our fix-it fic, his reunion with Sarah is the story's biggest emotional payoff. His arc goes from "broken man who lost everything" to "father who gets a second chance." The MC worships this character and saving him is the ultimate goal.
