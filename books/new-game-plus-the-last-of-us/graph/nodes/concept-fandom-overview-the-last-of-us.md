# Fandom Overview: The Last of Us

## Source Material
- **The Last of Us** (2013) — Naughty Dog, PS3/PS4/PS5
- **The Last of Us: Left Behind** (DLC) — Ellie's backstory with Riley
- **The Last of Us Part II** (2020) — Naughty Dog, PS4/PS5
- **The Last of Us** (HBO TV Series, 2023–present) — Craig Mazin & Neil Druckmann

## Core Premise
A mutated Cordyceps fungus infects human brains, turning hosts into aggressive, zombie-like "Infected." Civilization collapses in 2013. The story follows survivors navigating the brutal post-apocalyptic United States, centered on themes of love, loss, survival, and moral ambiguity.

## Complete Timeline

### September 27, 2013 — Outbreak Day
- Cordyceps mutation spreads from South American crops to humans
- FEDRA (Federal Disaster Response Agency) declares martial law, establishes quarantine zones
- In Austin, Texas: Joel Miller, his daughter Sarah (12), and brother Tommy flee
- A FEDRA soldier shoots Sarah; she dies in Joel's arms
- Joel and Tommy escape Austin

### 2013–2033 — The Quarantine Zone Era (20-year gap)
- Tommy joins the Fireflies (a rebel militia seeking to restore democracy), later leaves
- Joel becomes a smuggler in the Boston Quarantine Zone, partnering with Tess
- Most quarantine zones eventually fall to infection, uprisings, or supply depletion
- Society fragments into: FEDRA-controlled QZs, Firefly cells, hunter gangs, isolated communities

### July 2033 — Left Behind
- Ellie (14) and her friend Riley sneak into an abandoned Boston mall
- Both are bitten by infected; Riley turns, Ellie doesn't — she's immune
- This is the origin of Ellie's immunity and her survivor's guilt

### August 2033 — Joel and Ellie's Journey Begins
- **Day 1**: Firefly leader Marlene asks Joel and Tess to smuggle Ellie out of Boston
- **Day 2**: Group leaves the QZ; Ellie's immunity discovered; Tess is bitten
- **Day 3**: Tess sacrifices herself at the State House; Joel decides to find Tommy in Jackson, Wyoming
- **Lincoln**: Joel and Ellie find Bill, get a car
- **Pittsburgh**: Ambushed by hunters; meet brothers Henry and Sam; Sam is bitten, Henry kills Sam then himself

### Fall 2033 — Jackson, Wyoming
- Joel and Ellie reach Tommy's community in Jackson
- Tommy married Maria; community is self-sufficient, uses a hydroelectric dam
- Joel tries to leave Ellie with Tommy; Ellie's iconic speech: "Everyone I have cared for has either died or left me. Everyone except for you."
- Joel and Ellie continue to University of Eastern Colorado

### Late Fall 2033 — University of Eastern Colorado
- Fireflies have relocated to Salt Lake City
- Joel severely wounded by bandits; Ellie rescues him

### Winter 2033/2034 — David
- Ellie nurses Joel back to health
- Meets David, leader of a cannibal group; David captures Ellie
- Ellie kills David with a machete; Joel rescues her from a burning building

### April 28, 2034 — Salt Lake City (THE pivotal moment)
- Joel and Ellie reach the Firefly hospital
- Marlene reveals: extracting a vaccine from Ellie will kill her
- Joel massacres Fireflies, kills the surgeon (Dr. Jerry Anderson — Abby's father), kills Marlene
- Carries unconscious Ellie out; lies to her about what happened
- They settle in Jackson; Ellie says "Okay" to Joel's sworn lie

### 2035 — Birthday Museum Trip
- Joel takes Ellie to an abandoned museum; teaches her to swim
- One of their last wholesome moments before the truth fractures them

### 2036 — Ellie Learns the Truth
- Ellie returns to Salt Lake City, discovers what Joel did
- She is furious; cuts contact with Joel while both still live in Jackson

### March 1, 2038 — Reconciliation Attempt
- At a dance, Ellie and Dina kiss; Joel defends Ellie from a bigot
- Later, Ellie visits Joel: "I don't think I can ever forgive you for that. But I would like to try."

### March 2, 2038 — Abby Kills Joel
- Abby Anderson (daughter of the surgeon Joel killed) tracked Joel to Jackson
- Joel and Tommy save Abby from infected at a ski lodge; Abby recognizes Joel
- Abby beats Joel to death with a golf club; Ellie witnesses it
- Tommy survives but is severely injured

### Spring 2038 — Seattle (Part II main events)
- Ellie, Dina, Tommy, and Jesse travel to Seattle to hunt Abby
- Abby has joined the WLF (Washington Liberation Front) in Seattle
- Three days of interweaving violence between Ellie's group and Abby's people
- Key deaths: Jesse (shot by Abby), Owen & Mel (killed by Ellie), many WLF/Seraphites
- Abby befriends Lev (young transgender Seraphite outcast)
- Theater confrontation: Abby spares Ellie and Dina after Lev intervenes

### Summer 2039 — Santa Barbara
- Ellie lives on a farm with Dina and baby JJ; has PTSD
- Tommy convinces Ellie to pursue Abby one last time
- Ellie finds Abby and Lev as Rattler prisoners in Santa Barbara
- Final fight: Ellie nearly drowns Abby but lets her go, remembering Joel
- Ellie returns to an empty farm; can't play Joel's guitar (missing two fingers)

## Key Themes Across the Series
- **Love vs. the greater good** — Joel chose Ellie over humanity's cure
- **Cycles of violence** — every act of revenge creates more suffering
- **Found family** — Joel-Ellie, Abby-Lev, the Jackson community
- **Loss of innocence** — Sarah, Ellie, Abby all transformed by violence
- **Forgiveness** — Ellie's final choice to spare Abby
