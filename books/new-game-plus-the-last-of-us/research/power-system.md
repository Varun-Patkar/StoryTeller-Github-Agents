# Power System: Gamer System (Original)

## Overview
The MC's gamer system is a transmigration reward — activated upon entry into the TLOU world. It provides a game-like interface for survival, progression, and resource acquisition. Unlike typical LitRPG systems, this one is grounded in a realistic post-apocalyptic setting, so the power curve is intentionally slow and weighted toward practical survival.

## Core Mechanics

### Points Currency
- **Earned by**: Killing infected (primary source)
- **Point values scale with infected stage**:
  - Runner: ~5-10 points
  - Stalker: ~15-25 points
  - Clicker: ~50-75 points
  - Bloater: ~200-500 points
  - Shambler: ~150-300 points
  - Rat King: ~5,000-10,000 points (if one is ever encountered)
- **Bonus points**: First kills, combo kills, rescue missions, protecting civilians
- **No points from**: Killing humans (system does not reward PvP violence)

### The Mall (System Shop)
The system shop is themed as a "mall" — a nostalgic nod to pre-apocalypse life. Items are categorized by type and priced according to how "abnormal" they are in this world.

#### Pricing Tiers

**Tier 1 — Everyday Survival (Cheap: 1-50 points)**
- MREs / canned food / water purification tablets
- Basic medicine (bandages, antibiotics, painkillers)
- Clothing, blankets, tarps
- Matches, lighters, flashlights, batteries
- Basic tools (hammer, wrench, screwdriver)

**Tier 2 — Practical Hardware (Moderate: 50-500 points)**
- Firearms (handguns, rifles, shotguns) + ammunition
- Melee weapons (machetes, axes, combat knives)
- Generators, solar panels, fuel
- Seeds, farming tools, fertilizer
- Building materials (lumber, concrete, nails, wire)
- Radio equipment, walkie-talkies
- Vehicle parts, tires, engines

**Tier 3 — Advanced Technology (Expensive: 500-5,000 points)**
- Medical equipment (surgical kits, x-ray, defibrillator)
- Electrical infrastructure (wiring, transformers, circuit breakers)
- Water treatment systems
- Industrial tools (welding equipment, power saws)
- Armored vehicles (pre-built)
- Night vision goggles, body armor

**Tier 4 — Peak Human Enhancement (Very Expensive: 5,000-50,000 points)**
- Physical stat boosts (strength, speed, stamina, reflexes) — incremental
- Enhanced senses (hearing, sight, smell)
- Accelerated healing
- Pain resistance
- Disease/poison immunity
- Perfect memory
- Combat instincts (danger sense)

**Tier 5 — Superhuman (Astronomically Expensive: 50,000+ points)**
- "Superman Physique" — full superhuman physical package
- Regeneration (beyond accelerated healing)
- Supernatural durability
- Extreme speed
- These are endgame purchases; the MC won't reach this tier until the community is well-established

### Progression Philosophy
- **Early game (Outbreak to ~Year 1)**: MC scrounges points by clearing infected near Austin. Can afford Tier 1-2 items. Focuses on survival and finding Sarah.
- **Mid game (Years 1-5)**: MC has built up a base, farms infected more efficiently. Can afford Tier 2-3 items. Community building begins. Reaches peak human performance.
- **Late game (Years 5+)**: Community is established. MC starts purchasing Tier 4 enhancements. Becomes noticeably superhuman. Community becomes a regional power.

### System Rules & Limitations
1. **No resurrection** — the system cannot bring back the dead
2. **No mind control** — cannot affect other people's free will
3. **No cure for Cordyceps** — the system shop doesn't sell a magic cure (preserves the world's core tension)
4. **Physical items materialize** — appear in MC's immediate vicinity or in a designated "inventory space"
5. **Stat boosts are permanent** — once purchased, they don't expire
6. **No skill download** — MC doesn't instantly learn how to fight/build; skills must be practiced (system just enhances the body, not the mind's training)
7. **System is private** — only the MC can see the interface; no one else knows about it

### UI Elements (for writing scenes)
- **[System Notification]**: Pops up for kills, achievements, unlocks
- **[Mall]**: Opens the shop interface
- **[Status]**: Shows current stats, point balance
- **[Quest]** (optional): May trigger for major story events (e.g., "Save Sarah Miller", "Establish a Settlement")
- Keep UI elements minimal and non-intrusive in the narrative; avoid walls of stat text

## Gamer System Genre Conventions
- **Power fantasy balanced with stakes**: MC is strong but not invincible, especially early on. The world is still dangerous.
- **Economy creates tension**: Expensive items force meaningful choices (buy a generator for the community vs. save for personal enhancement?)
- **Point farming isn't boring**: Hunting infected is inherently dangerous; even with growing strength, Clickers and Bloaters can kill
- **Community investment**: Points spent on infrastructure benefit everyone, not just the MC
- **Avoid the "too OP too fast" trap**: Keep the MC grounded for at least the first major arc; human enemies and social challenges can't be solved with stats
