# Supporting Cast

## Tommy Miller
- **Role**: Joel's younger brother; eventual community leader of Jackson
- **Personality**: Warmer than Joel; idealistic (joined Fireflies); practical leader
- **Pre-outbreak**: Military background (implied)
- **Key relationship**: Married Maria in Jackson; runs the hydroelectric dam community
- **Voice**: More open and talkative than Joel; Texas accent but softer
- **Quotes**: "Everything you see in our town, everybody pitched in."
- **In our story**: Initially separated from Joel; eventually discovers the MC's community or the MC discovers Jackson. Alliance between communities is possible. His reaction to Sarah being alive mirrors Joel's but with less guilt
- **Skills**: Leadership, combat, community management, knowledge of dam operations

## Tess (Theresa)
- **Role**: Joel's smuggling partner in Boston QZ
- **Personality**: Tough, pragmatic, sardonic; believes in doing what's necessary
- **Death**: Bitten during the escort mission; sacrifices herself to buy Joel and Ellie time at the State House
- **In our story**: May die as in canon (her death motivates Joel) unless the MC's influence reaches Boston early enough
- **Voice**: Sharp, no-nonsense: "There's enough here that I have to think about."

## Marlene
- **Role**: Leader of the Fireflies; knew Ellie's mother Anna
- **Personality**: Idealistic but willing to sacrifice individuals for the greater good
- **Death**: Killed by Joel at St. Mary's Hospital
- **In our story**: Represents the moral counterpoint — is one life worth a potential cure? The MC's community may eventually deal with Firefly remnants
- **Voice**: Calm, measured, diplomatic until pushed: "This isn't about me."

## Abby Anderson
- **Role**: Daughter of Dr. Jerry Anderson (the surgeon Joel killed)
- **Personality**: Driven by grief and vengeance; physically powerful; capable of compassion (befriends Lev)
- **In canon**: Kills Joel with a golf club; this is THE event the MC wants to prevent
- **In our story**: The MC must figure out how to handle the Abby situation — either preventing her from finding Joel, or redirecting her grief. She's not evil; she's a grieving daughter
- **Build**: Muscular, athletic; trained obsessively after her father's death
- **Voice**: Direct, intense, loyal to her friends

## Bill
- **Role**: Survivalist loner in Lincoln; Joel's contact
- **Personality**: Paranoid, grumpy, competent; closeted gay man whose partner Frank left/died
- **Skills**: Trap-making, explosives, vehicle repair
- **In our story**: Could potentially be recruited to the MC's community if the story reaches Lincoln

## Henry & Sam
- **Role**: Brothers in Pittsburgh; Henry is the older protector, Sam is a deaf child (TV) / young boy (game)
- **Tragedy**: Sam is bitten, turns overnight; Henry kills Sam then himself
- **In our story**: If the MC's community expands, these two could potentially be saved — another fix-it opportunity

## David
- **Role**: Leader of a cannibal group; primary villain of the Winter chapter
- **Personality**: Manipulative, predatory, charismatic on the surface
- **In our story**: May appear as a threat to the community or to Ellie; no redemption — this character is genuinely evil
- **Voice**: Soft-spoken, reasonable-sounding, deeply menacing

## Maria Miller (née unknown)
- **Role**: Tommy's wife; co-leader of Jackson
- **Personality**: Practical, protective of Jackson, initially suspicious of outsiders
- **In our story**: Potential ally; the Jackson-MC community alliance could be a major political development

## Dr. Jerry Anderson (Abby's Father)
- **Role**: Firefly surgeon who was going to operate on Ellie
- **Death**: Killed by Joel in the operating room
- **In our story**: If the MC's community offers an alternative to killing Ellie for a cure, this man's fate could change — which would prevent Abby's entire revenge arc

## Lev
- **Role**: Young transgender Seraphite; Abby's companion in Part II
- **Personality**: Brave, skilled archer, devout but questioning
- **In our story**: May appear in later arcs if the story reaches Seattle-era events
