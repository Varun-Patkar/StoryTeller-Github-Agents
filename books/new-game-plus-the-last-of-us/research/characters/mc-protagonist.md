# Character: The MC (Self-Insert Protagonist)

## Overview
A modern-day person (adult mind) who was an avid fan of The Last of Us. After crying once again at Sarah's death scene, they're transmigrated into the body of a 14-year-old boy in the TLOU world on Outbreak Day (September 27, 2013) with a Gamer System. The body belongs to Marcus Webb, an eighth-grader at Travis Heights Middle School in Austin, Texas, whose parents were killed by infected on Outbreak Night. The MC's adult mind in a teenager's body gives them maturity and knowledge that doesn't match their appearance. Their mission: fix the tragedies they wept over — save Sarah, save Joel from Abby, and build something worth living for.

## Personality
- **Initially**: Excited but terrified. They know the source material but being IN the apocalypse is vastly different from playing it on a screen
- **Empathetic**: Cried multiple times at Sarah's death in the game; this emotional core drives their mission
- **Optimistic**: Brings a modern sensibility and hopefulness that contrasts with the grim world
- **Moral**: Clear code — killing bad people (bandits, slavers, cannibals) is acceptable; killing innocents is never okay
- **Adaptable**: Grows into the apocalypse; becomes harder and more capable while retaining core goodness
- **Humorous**: Uses modern references and jokes that no one understands; a coping mechanism
- **Leader (gradually)**: Doesn't start as a leader but circumstances and the system push them into the role

## Arc
1. **Outbreak Day**: Arrives scared, confused, but determined. Finds and saves Sarah after Joel and Tommy leave her body (per the user's timeline — the bullet hits Sarah but the MC intervenes before she dies, or finds her wounded)
2. **Early Survival**: Learning to survive; first infected kills; discovering the system's shop
3. **Sarah's Protector**: Takes care of wounded Sarah; they bond; she can't have children due to the bullet wound
4. **Base Builder**: Finds a dam location (echoing Jackson); starts building a community
5. **Community Leader**: Becomes the dominant figure — not a dictator, but the strongest person in a democratic community
6. **Regional Power**: Community grows to threaten FEDRA and the weakening Fireflies
7. **Reunion**: Eventually crosses paths with Joel and Tommy; brings them into the fold
8. **Ellie**: Joel finds Ellie; MC's community adopts her (Sarah and MC act as surrogate parents)
9. **Preventing Joel's Death**: Uses meta-knowledge to prevent Abby's revenge

## Abilities (via Gamer System)
- **Early**: Normal human with system interface; buys basic supplies
- **Mid**: Peak human physique; enhanced combat skills through practice + physical boosts
- **Late**: Superhuman strength, speed, durability; community's ultimate protector

## Relationships
- **Sarah Miller**: Love interest (once she's older — there's an age-appropriate time skip). Initially a protector-protectee dynamic that evolves into partnership and romance
- **Joel Miller**: Admiration and respect from the MC's side; Joel is wary at first but grateful for Sarah being alive. Father figure energy
- **Tommy Miller**: Practical alliance; Tommy respects the MC's community-building efforts
- **Ellie Williams**: Adoptive daughter figure (alongside Sarah); MC knows her immunity secret
- **Community members**: Various original characters who join the settlement

## Voice & Mannerisms
- Modern slang and references that date them as "not from here" (though people assume it's just weirdness)
- Internal monologue often addresses the system or makes meta-commentary about "canon events"
- Swears when stressed; tries to be encouraging to others
- Uses humor to deflect fear: "Well, on a scale of 'bad day' to 'literal apocalypse'... oh wait."
- When serious: speaks plainly, directly, with conviction

## Relevance to Our Story
The MC is the protagonist and narrator (from First POV sections). Their meta-knowledge, gamer system, and emotional investment in the TLOU cast drive the entire plot. They are the fix-it agent — the person who refuses to let the tragedies of the canon timeline play out.
