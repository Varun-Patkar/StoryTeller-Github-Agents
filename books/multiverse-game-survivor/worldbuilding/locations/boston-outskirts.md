# Boston Outskirts

## Description

Collapsed roads, drowned neighborhoods, abandoned vehicles, and green growth have consumed the approaches to Boston. Human voices are often more dangerous than infected sounds. Old FEDRA routes and informal smuggler trails cut through the ruins.

## History

The region was evacuated and militarized after the Cordyceps outbreak. Peripheral FEDRA facilities have since collapsed, leaving former students, deserters, smugglers, infected, and bandits to compete for shelter and supplies.

## Culture & Customs

There is no unified culture outside the quarantine zone. Travelers conceal resources, avoid noise, and treat unfamiliar groups as threats until proven otherwise.

## Notable Features

- A collapsed overpass overlooking an abandoned service road
- The remains of a small FEDRA preparatory facility
- A nearby bandit camp controlling a useful route toward Boston

## Current State

Alaric arrives here one year before Joel and Ellie begin their westward journey. A bandit camp is preying on travelers nearby.
