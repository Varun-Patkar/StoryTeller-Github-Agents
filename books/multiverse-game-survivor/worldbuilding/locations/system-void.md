# System Void

## Description

A lightless, bodiless space used by the Second Chance System between Alaric's death and his arrival in another world. Interface text provides its only visible structure. The absence of pain makes it feel peaceful rather than empty.

## History

Its origin and relationship to the Second Chance System are unknown.

## Culture & Customs

No society is known to exist here.

## Notable Features

- The system can give Alaric a temporary sense of bodily form.
- Glimpses of possible destination worlds appear at the edge of perception.
- Time and physical distance have no confirmed meaning.

## Current State

Alaric first encounters the system here after his original death. Whether he returns between every world remains unknown.
