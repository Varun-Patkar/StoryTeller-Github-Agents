# Second Chance System

## Overview

The Second Chance System resurrects Alaric Stark and sends him through game worlds where preventable tragedies await. Its name reflects both halves of its purpose: Alaric receives another life, and he gains the means to offer second chances to others. It presents itself as a supportive guide and says it chose him in response to his final wish, but its origin and full nature remain unknown.

## Status & Progression

The status display favors readable qualitative ranks over dense numerical values.

- **Attributes:** Physique, Agility, Endurance, Perception, Intellect, and Will. Each attribute has a named rank and a point counter. Ten points raise an attribute by one rank, so every reward shows exact progress.
- **Skills:** Practical competencies ranked from Novice through Competent, Expert, and Master. A rewarded skill grants aptitude and a foundation, not effortless mastery.
- **Traits:** Passive adaptations or unusual qualities, such as resistance to disease. Traits may improve when repeatedly tested.
- **Items:** Physical rewards and acquired equipment can be stored in an unlimited personal inventory.

Power must be earned. Rewards give Alaric potential, knowledge, or a modest improvement, but he must train and apply them under pressure. His advantage should be satisfying without making the current world's threats irrelevant.

Quest notifications must list exact rewards. Named caches include every item and quantity before Alaric accepts or completes the quest. The system does not hide reward values behind terms such as "progress" or "improvement."

## Quest Structure

- **Main objectives** identify preventable tragedies at the heart of a world's story. They distinguish between foundational events that must already have occurred and later suffering Alaric can still change.
- **Optional quests** offer additional chances to protect people, improve communities, gather resources, or alter lesser tragedies.
- Quests provide a goal and deadline but do not prescribe a method.
- Optional quests carry no system-imposed failure penalty. If one expires or fails, it simply disappears; any consequences that already occurred in the world remain real.
- Completing the main objective unlocks departure. Alaric may remain as long as he wishes before choosing the next world.
- Success is measured by whether the surviving people have a credible better future, not merely whether Alaric wins a final confrontation.

## World Transfer

On entry, the system provides Alaric with a healthy, setting-appropriate body and a plausible local identity. His appearance remains recognizably his, including the white forelock that persists in every incarnation. Transfer does not erase carried skills, traits, attributes, memories, or inventory.

The system selects an arrival point after any tragedy required to create the people and conflict Alaric knows. It does not send him far enough back to erase a protagonist's foundational history. Later, preventable canonical tragedies remain valid targets.

## Inventory

The inventory has no fixed capacity, and time stops for stored objects. Food, medicine, ammunition, and other perishables remain in the condition in which they entered. Living beings cannot be stored. Alaric must physically possess an object before moving it into the inventory, and retrieving equipment still requires deliberate action.

## Recovery

Alaric regenerates faster than an ordinary person, but the rate scales with Endurance. Early recovery is only modestly accelerated; serious wounds still demand treatment, rest, and time, and remain capable of killing him. Regeneration becomes a visible benefit of earned growth rather than an immediate escape from danger.

## Rules & Limitations

1. The system cannot force another person's choices or guarantee that canonical knowledge remains accurate after Alaric intervenes.
2. Rewards cannot replace practice. Knowing the basis of a skill is different from performing it reliably under pressure.
3. There is no automatic reset after failure. Deaths, injuries, damaged relationships, and altered events persist.
4. The system supports Alaric but does not solve objectives for him. It supplies goals, deadlines, rewards, transfer, and status tracking.
5. Foreknowledge loses reliability as changes accumulate. The more successful Alaric becomes, the less he can depend on the original plot.
6. The system can improve Alaric's ability to build and protect a better life, including settlements and communities; its purpose is not limited to combat power.

## History

Nothing is known about the system before Alaric's death. It describes his selection as an answer to his wish for another chance, but whether it was created, discovered, or born from that wish remains an open question.

## Practitioners

Alaric Stark is the only known user. The interface is private unless a later reward or plot development explicitly allows him to reveal it.

## Impact on Society

The system is not publicly known. Its effects appear through Alaric's unusual competence, resources, recovery, and ability to anticipate danger. Communities experience its larger impact indirectly when he uses accumulated power to create safety and lasting alternatives to their canonical tragedies.
