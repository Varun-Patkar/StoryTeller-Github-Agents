# Alaric's Starter Backpack

## Description

A battered school backpack supplied with Alaric's first local identity. It looks used enough to fit the setting and carries no obvious system markings.

## Function, Constraints & Costs

The backpack contains two sealed water bottles, three ration bars, a folding knife, a spare shirt, a basic first-aid kit, and identity papers. The contents are ordinary physical items. Losing the backpack means losing anything not transferred into system inventory.

## History

The Second Chance System provides it when Alaric enters his first world.

## Current State

Alaric carries it on the Boston outskirts while assessing his first optional quest.
