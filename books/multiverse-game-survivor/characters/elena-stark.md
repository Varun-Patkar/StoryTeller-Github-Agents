# Elena Stark

## Appearance

Elena is a slight thirteen-year-old with dark hair she cuts herself whenever it becomes inconvenient. She and Alaric share gray eyes, though hers are more openly expressive. During her final visit, she wears an oversized green coat and carries a school backpack packed for an escape lasting less than an hour.

## Personality & Traits

Quiet around her parents and stubborn everywhere else, Elena is mischievous, expressive, and deeply caring. She pouts when corrected, looks pleased when one of her tricks works, and uses innocent looks to escape trouble. She likes teasing Alaric and pushing rules just far enough to annoy him. She is less interested in games than Alaric but listens closely when he talks about them. Guilt makes her hesitant; love makes her brave enough to disobey their parents and cross the city alone to see him.

## Backstory

Elena grew up in the same wealthy but emotionally distant household as Alaric. Their parents discouraged her from visiting him as his illness worsened, framing distance as protection. She secretly visits his private hospital room on his final night and becomes the only member of the family to say goodbye.

## Motivations & Goals

- **External want:** Say goodbye without breaking down and making Alaric comfort her.
- **Internal need:** Stop waiting for her parents' permission to love people and choose her own life.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Elena speaks like a quiet thirteen-year-old who becomes blunt when upset. She does not give polished speeches. She says what she feels in simple words, even when the words come out awkwardly.

> "Mum said I shouldn't come. I came anyway."

> "You can tell me if you're scared. I won't tell anyone."

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** Elena follows her parents' rules despite knowing those rules have isolated Alaric.
- **Key turning points:** She defies them to visit him and receives his final request that she choose her own life.
- **Ending state:** Her future remains outside Alaric's knowledge, but she leaves determined to act from love rather than permission.
