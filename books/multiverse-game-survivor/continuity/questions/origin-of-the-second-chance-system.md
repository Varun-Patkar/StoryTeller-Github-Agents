## Question

Who or what created the Second Chance System, and why was it able to answer Alaric's final wish?

## Evidence

The system identifies its function but explicitly distinguishes purpose from origin. It promises that Alaric may judge it through its actions.

## Resolution Plan

Reveal the answer gradually across later worlds. Do not let uncertainty make the supportive interface behave arbitrarily or deceptively without tracked cause.
