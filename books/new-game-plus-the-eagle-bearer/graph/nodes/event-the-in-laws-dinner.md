# Event: The In-Laws Dinner

> id: event-the-in-laws-dinner | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
Late in Act One (or its coda), everyone Kassandra lost or was betrayed by sits at one table, whole and reconciled: Nikolaos and Myrrine, Alexios restored, Stentor at peace, Markos and Phoibe as the Kephallonian family, and Theron as the man Kassandra loves. The scene that pays off Kassandra's wound of familial betrayal.

## Canon
No equivalent; canon's family never fully heals like this.

## AU Divergence
A deliberately warm, fix-it capstone to the family theme.

## Notes
Emotional climax of the found-family arc.
