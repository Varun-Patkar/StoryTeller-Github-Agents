# Character: Theron and Kassandra's First Child

## Overview
The elder of at least two children chosen and raised by Theron and Kassandra during the chapter-39 time span. The personal name, gender, vocation, and exact lifespan remain intentionally open for later planning.

## Canon
No canon counterpart.

## AU Divergence
Kassandra's canon Natakas relationship and Elpidios are removed. This child belongs to her chosen marriage with Theron.

## Story Function
Lives a complete mortal life, becomes older than the physically young parents, makes independent choices, and dies naturally. The life helps teach the founders not to impose immortality or hereditary Eclipse authority on descendants.
