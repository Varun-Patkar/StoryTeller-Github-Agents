# Event: The False Oracle

## Overview
Chapter 8 opens the Cult Unveiled arc in Phokis.

## Outcome
Theron and Kassandra tie the forced summit murder to Elpenor, kill him in his cave, and recover his Cult mask, invitation, and the unpaid 1,000-drachma contract balance. They disable the guards and warning network around Praxithea, confirm Cult control of Sparta's oracle and Myrrine's survival, and recruit Herodotos as a public bridge for Isu history and engineering. Theron's ending balance is 1,536 drachmae and 2 obols.

## Continuity
Elpenor is dead. Praxithea remains in Delphi as a willing covert source. Elpenor's mask and invitation provide access to the chapter 9 sanctuary gathering. The Forge Key remains at 46% integrity with 27 captured fragments and four understood.
