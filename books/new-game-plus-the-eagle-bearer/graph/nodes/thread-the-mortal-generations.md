# Thread: The Mortal Generations

## Overview
Chapter 39 follows the mortal household through selective dated jumps. Theron and Kassandra raise at least two children without imposing immortality or hereditary authority. Their children and every other loved one age and die after complete lives while the founders remain physically young.

## Progression
- Parenthood exposes Theron's tendency to turn protection into rules and Kassandra's tendency to make freedom someone else's cleanup.
- Their children grow independent, become older than their parents appear, and die in their own seasons.
- Markos, Phoibe, Myrrine, Nikolaos, Alexios, Stentor, Barnabas, Brasidas, and the rest of the mortal family also pass naturally.
- Accumulated grief makes Theron brooding and old-minded and Kassandra publicly hardline, while private marriage keeps both capable of warmth.

## Payoff
Their experience produces Eclipse bans on dynastic authority and imposed immortality. Saved lives matter because they were fully lived.
