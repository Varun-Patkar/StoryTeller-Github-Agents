# Event: A Ship Out

## Overview
Chapter 5 closes the Kephallonia arc. Elpenor hires Kassandra and Theron to kill Nikolaos, the Wolf of Sparta. They accept to control the encounter and investigate the organization behind Elpenor.

## Events
Theron and Kassandra attack the isolated Cyclops at Kleptous Bay after Phoibe locates his remaining camp. Ikaros scouts the open bay while Glaux scouts the cave and prisoner route. Theron frees Barnabas and disables the harbor; Kassandra kills the Cyclops. Barnabas and his crew reclaim the Adrestia.

## State Changes
- The Cyclops is dead and his Kephallonian network is finished.
- The obsidian eye remains intact as 2,400-drachma capital and 1,760-drachma System barter credit.
- Barnabas joins Kassandra and Theron as captain of the Adrestia.
- The quarry cache is secured with external access disabled.
- Phoibe and Markos remain safe on Kephallonia with the Cyclops records distributed as protection.
- Theron's System balance ends at 462 drachmae and 0 obols.
- Kassandra and Theron sail for Megaris to confront Nikolaos.
