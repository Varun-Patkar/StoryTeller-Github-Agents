# Event: Deprogramming Alexios

> id: event-deprogramming-alexios | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
Alexios (Deimos) was tortured and indoctrinated by the Cult into a weapon. Theron figures out the Isu memory technology (Pyramid / Staff / Animus-adjacent principles) and rewrites the conditioning, freeing Alexios and returning him to Kassandra, Myrrine, and Nikolaos.

## Canon
Kassandra can redeem Deimos through words, but it is fragile; canon leaves scars.

## AU Divergence
Theron achieves a genuine, technological cure of the brainwashing — a clean save. This is a flagship fix-it victory and a proof-of-concept for Theron's science pivot.

## Notes
Depends on Theron's Isu-tech research; sets up the memory-rewriting toolkit reused later.
