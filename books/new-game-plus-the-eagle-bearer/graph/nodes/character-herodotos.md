# Character: Herodotos

> id: character-herodotos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The famed historian, fascinated by the Isu relics threaded through Greek myth. He introduces Kassandra to the Spear's true nature and to the Ancient Forges.

## Canon
Companion and lore-guide throughout Odyssey.

## AU Divergence
Becomes Theron's favourite conversational foil: Theron already knows the answers, so he uses Herodotos to *launder* his foreknowledge into "reasoned deductions" without exposing the System.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Curious, verbose, delighted by mysteries.
- "History, my friend, is stranger than any myth."

## Notes
Cover for Theron's meta-knowledge; Isu-lore conduit.
