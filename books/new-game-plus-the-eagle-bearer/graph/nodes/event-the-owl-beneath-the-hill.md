# Event: The Owl Beneath the Hill

## Overview
Theron and Kassandra return to the old limestone cut after disclosing every assigned risk. They open the fitted wall, survive a degraded Isu security chamber, recover the damaged Forge Key, and revive Glaux.

## Outcomes
- Theron's Forge Key-assisted Isu Sixth Sense awakens as concentrated analytical Eagle Vision.
- Theron teaches Kassandra the sensory method; her stronger Isu heritage produces broad combat Eagle Vision.
- The buried-wall thread is resolved without exposing the cache to Markos or the public.
- Fifteen years of training and repair carry the household to the adult Odyssey opening in 431 BCE.
- Theron's adult balance is 318 drachmae and 4 obols; the Forge Key is 46% intact with 27 captured blueprint fragments, four understood.
