# Character: Xenia

> id: character-xenia | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The imperious pirate queen of Keos who trades information and treasure maps for coin.

## Canon
Sends Kassandra on treasure hunts; once sailed with Myrrine.

## AU Divergence
Her treasure hunts become Theron's income engine — orichalcum and relics to feed the System shop.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Grand, greedy, amused.
- "Bring me treasure, West Wind, and I'll bring you answers."

## Notes
Links treasure-hunting to the shop economy.
