# Ability: Assassin's Creed System

> id: ability-assassins-creed-system | type: ability | canonicity: original

## Overview
The System is the engine of Theron's second life. It appeared the moment he reincarnated and presented a "beginner gift pack," then unlocked a permanent shop.

## The Beginner Gift Pack
1. **Enhanced Talent in All Attributes** — a boost that *grows with age* across every attribute, including meta-attributes: strength, speed, endurance, but also intelligence, charisma, perception, and (crucially) memory. It is why Theron's recall of every Assassin's Creed game is flawless. It is a potential multiplier, not instant power: he still has to train and study, but he learns and improves far faster than any mortal.
2. **1% Isu Blood** — just enough hybrid DNA to touch Isu technology, resist Pieces of Eden control, and (eventually) survive what should kill a normal human.

## The Shop
A vast marketplace containing everything in the Assassin's Creed universe:
- **Expensive tier:** Isu blood (higher concentrations), Pieces of Eden, permanent attribute boosts, raw Isu knowledge/blueprints, Isu longevity.
- **Cheap tier:** food, water, medicine, tools, ordinary gear.
- **Currency:** anything of value. Drachmae and orichalcum at first; later Theron discovers the shop will also *accept Isu artifacts themselves as barter*, letting him trade lesser relics up for knowledge and immortality.

## Design rules (author constraints)
- The shop is the balancing mechanism: the game-changing items are absurdly expensive, forcing clever earning and hard choices. Theron is never handed a win; he buys, barters, and engineers it.
- Foreknowledge + the memory boost mean Theron never loses on information.

## Canon
_Original mechanic; not from source material._

## AU Divergence
Invented for this story. It replaces Kassandra's canonical solo-hero arc with a partnership: Theron's System-bought edges plus Kassandra's raw Isu-blood strength.

## Notes
Later, Theron treats the shop less as a crutch and more as an R&D budget: he buys Isu blueprints and builds his own devices rather than buying finished miracles.
