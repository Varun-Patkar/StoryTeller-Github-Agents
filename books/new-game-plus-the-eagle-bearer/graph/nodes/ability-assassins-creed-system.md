# Ability: Assassin's Creed System

> id: ability-assassins-creed-system | type: ability | canonicity: original

## Overview
The System is the engine of Theron's second life. It appeared the moment he reincarnated and presented a beginner gift pack, then unlocked a permanent shop. In prose it reads like a plain game menu (bracketed panels, concrete numbers), never a glowing mystical vision.

## The Beginner Gift Pack
1. **Enhanced Talent (all attributes)** ΓÇö a growth bonus that compounds with age across every attribute, including the mental ones (intelligence, charisma, perception, willpower, memory). It is potential, not instant power: Theron still trains and studies, he just improves far faster than a normal person. His flawless recall of every Assassin's Creed game comes from the high Memory stat.
2. **1% Isu Blood** ΓÇö just enough hybrid DNA to touch Isu technology, resist Piece-of-Eden control, and (eventually) survive what would kill a normal human.

## Mechanics ΓÇö ALWAYS RENDER WITH CONCRETE NUMBERS
The System is a real LitRPG layer, like the numbers in the writing samples. Do not write it as a vague "pane of light." Show a status screen, a running balance, item prices, and pings.

### Attributes (scale: 10 = a fit, ordinary adult)
STR (strength), AGI (agility), END (endurance), INT (intelligence), PER (perception), CHA (charisma), WIL (willpower), MEM (memory).

**Canonical starting status (age 9, the prologue):**
- STR 5, AGI 6, END 5, INT 13, PER 8, CHA 6, WIL 7, MEM 16.
- MEM is high because the gift unlocked perfect recall; the physical stats are a small boy's and must be earned up.
- Track growth loosely but consistently: by the Kephallonia arc (~age 15) his physicals are trained toward and past 10; never have him suddenly superhuman without on-page training/spending.

### Currency
- Base coin: **1 drachma = 6 obols**. A day's skilled wage is roughly 1 drachma, so obols matter early.
- Larger unit later: **orichalcum** (rare, from treasure/relics), then **Isu artifacts bartered directly**.
- Theron has a **running balance the reader can see.** Show it. Update it when he earns or spends. He starts at **0 drachmae, 0 obols.**

### Prices (concrete; cheap tier is real, top tier is astronomical)
- Bread (loaf) 1 obol ┬╖ Waterskin 2 obols ┬╖ Rope (10 cubits) 3 obols ┬╖ Numbing salve (pot) 8 obols ┬╖ Iron knife 4 drachmae ┬╖ Bandages (roll) 5 obols ┬╖ Sandals 2 drachmae.
- Mid tier: minor consumable stat tonic (temporary) tens of drachmae; a real map/intel a few drachmae.
- Top tier (render as huge, specific figures, not "infinity"): Concentrated Isu Blood 5% = 4,320,000 drachmae ┬╖ Fragment of Eden (minor) = 71,000,000 drachmae ┬╖ Permanent +1 to one attribute = 250,000 drachmae ┬╖ Isu Blueprint = LOCKED (insufficient standing) until he has real Isu artifacts to trade.
- Rule: everyday purchases have exact prices and change his balance; world-breaking items are clearly, painfully out of reach with real digits.

### Presentation in prose
- Panels in brackets on their own lines, e.g. `[ STATUS ]`, `[ SHOP ΓÇö Balance: 12 obols ]`, `[ +1 AGI ]`.
- A ping when a stat rises from training. Keep it matter-of-fact, one line, then move on (like the samples' `[INT +1]`).
- Never let the numbers become the whole scene; drop them in and keep the story moving.

## Design rules (author constraints)
- The shop is the balancing mechanism: game-changers are astronomically expensive, so Theron earns, barters, and engineers his wins. He is never simply handed power.
- Foreknowledge + high MEM mean he never loses on information.

## Canon
_Original mechanic; not from source material._

## AU Divergence
Invented for this story. It replaces Kassandra's canonical solo-hero arc with a partnership: Theron's System-bought edges plus Kassandra's raw Isu-blood strength.

## Notes
Later, Theron treats the shop as an R&D budget: he buys Isu blueprints and builds his own devices instead of buying finished miracles.

## Chapter 10 State
The shop unlocks a one-use Forge interface adapter only after Theron captures an active fabrication-site scan. It costs 1,200 drachmae and leaves a balance of 306 dr 2 ob. The purchase bridges one damaged service circuit and opens one interpretation slot; it does not provide free knowledge or a finished blueprint.
