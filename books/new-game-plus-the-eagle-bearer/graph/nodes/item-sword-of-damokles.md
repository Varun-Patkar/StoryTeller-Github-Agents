# Item: Sword of Damokles

## Overview
An Isu Sword carried by Alexios as Deimos. It amplifies physical force, protects its wielder from the return, and can connect with nearby compatible Isu artifacts.

## Canon
The Sword of Damokles is a Piece of Eden wielded by Deimos.

## Chapter 10 State
Theron's repaired Forge Key resolves three active routes in the Sword. Its artifact link carries a brief memory of Myrrine to Alexios, then returns Cult conditioning pressure through the same connection.
