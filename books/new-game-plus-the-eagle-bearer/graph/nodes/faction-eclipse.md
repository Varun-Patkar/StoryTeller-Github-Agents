# Faction: Eclipse

## Creed
No truth is absolute. No order eternal.

## Doctrine
Chaos enables dissent, adaptation, invention, and progress, but unchecked chaos degrades trust and society. Order enables stability, memory, and coordination, but excessive order creates stagnation, resentment, and tyranny. Eclipse acts against whichever force approaches permanence.

## Methods
Observe, infiltrate, redirect, expose, rescue, or remove pressure points. Never declare open institutional war on Assassins or Templars. History should mistake interventions for coincidence, internal reform, betrayal, or ordinary political change.

## Symbol
An owl crossing a darkened sun.

## Founders
Theron and Kassandra are equal founders. Balance is active judgment, not passive neutrality.
