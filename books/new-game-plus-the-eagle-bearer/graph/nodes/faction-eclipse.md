# Faction: Eclipse

## Creed
No truth is absolute. No order eternal.

## Origin
Theron and Kassandra found the Eclipse in chapter 40 only after decades of marriage, parenthood, bereavement, and tested disagreements. Their children and every mortal loved one have already lived and died, leaving the founders as each other's only permanent contemporaries.

## Doctrine
Chaos enables dissent, adaptation, invention, and progress, but unchecked chaos degrades trust and society. Order enables stability, memory, and coordination, but excessive order creates stagnation, resentment, and tyranny. The Eclipse acts against whichever force approaches permanence.

## Founder Balance
- Theron leans toward order: archives, evidence standards, layered cells, patient strategy, science, and infrastructure.
- Kassandra leans toward chaos: protected dissent, field discretion, disruptive correction, anti-dynastic limits, and the refusal to let abstraction excuse cruelty.
- Every durable rule carries a review or dissent path. Every disruptive power carries an accounting afterward. Neither founder outranks the other.

## Methods
Observe, infiltrate, redirect, expose, rescue, or remove pressure points. Never declare open institutional war on Assassins or Templars. History should mistake interventions for coincidence, internal reform, betrayal, or ordinary political change.

## Safeguards
No hereditary authority, no immortality imposed on descendants, no permanent founder veto, and no doctrine exempt from review. Balance is active judgment, not passive neutrality.

## Symbol
An owl crossing a darkened sun.
