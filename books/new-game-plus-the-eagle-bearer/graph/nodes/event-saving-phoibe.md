# Event: Saving Phoibe

> id: event-saving-phoibe | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
During the Plague of Athens, Phoibe is sent on the errand that gets her killed in canon. Theron, knowing the exact place and time, intercepts the Cult guards (or removes Phoibe from Athens beforehand). She lives.

## Canon
In Odyssey, Phoibe is murdered here; her death radicalises Kassandra.

## AU Divergence
Phoibe survives. This is the tonal turning point that confirms the fix-it premise to the reader.

## Notes
Diverges from canon; downstream, Phoibe joins the found family and the reunion dinner.
