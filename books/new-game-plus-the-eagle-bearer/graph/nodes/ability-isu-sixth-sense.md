# Ability: Isu Sixth Sense

## Overview
The heightened perception and analytical sense granted by Isu triple-helix DNA, expressed in high-DNA humans as Eagle Vision and related gifts.

## Canon
The precursor of the Assassins' Eagle Vision; tied to Isu Knowledge.

## AU Divergence
In chapter 3 the damaged Forge Key forces Theron's inherited sense open as a narrow, high-resolution analytical form. It resolves material seams, mechanisms, energy paths, chemical traces, intent cues, and individual targets at close range. Theron then teaches Kassandra the sensory method; her stronger Isu blood produces a broad form suited to threats, tracking, battlefield geometry, and rapid target discrimination.

Neither form reads minds, reveals facts without evidence, or replaces investigation. Fatigue, overload, unfamiliar machinery, and deliberate masking remain limits.
