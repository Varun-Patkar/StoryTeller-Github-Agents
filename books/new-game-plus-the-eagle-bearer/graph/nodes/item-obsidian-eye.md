# Item: Obsidian Eye

## Overview
A polished black-and-gold eye bought by the Cyclops as a display of wealth.

## Chapter 4 State
Theron and Kassandra recover it intact from the Kranioi warehouse. System appraisal: open-market value 2,400 drachmae; barter credit 1,760 drachmae; no Isu compatibility; ownership disputed. They retain it as capital and leverage rather than selling or damaging it.
