# Item: Obsidian Eye

## Overview
A polished black-and-gold eye bought by the Cyclops as a display of wealth.

## Chapter 4 State
Theron and Kassandra recover it intact from the Kranioi warehouse. System appraisal: open-market value 2,400 drachmae; barter credit 1,760 drachmae; no Isu compatibility; ownership disputed.

## Chapter 5 State
Kassandra uses the intact eye to hold the Cyclops's attention during the attack at Kleptous Bay, then recovers it after his death. It leaves Kephallonia locked aboard the Adrestia as Theron and Kassandra's capital.
