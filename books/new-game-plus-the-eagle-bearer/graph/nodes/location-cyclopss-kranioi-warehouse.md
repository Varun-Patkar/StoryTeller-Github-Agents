# Location: Cyclops's Kranioi Warehouse

## Overview
A guarded storehouse below Kranioi where the Cyclops consolidates collected money and seized property.

## Chapter 4 State
Glaux scouts its interior. Theron enters with the collection seal, divides the guards with a controlled rack drop, opens the office lock, and removes the ledger and eye while leaving named debtors' money. Kassandra secures their exit through the east wall.
