# Character: Ikaros

## Overview
Golden eagle companion bonded to Kassandra before the adult Odyssey opening. He scouts open terrain and shares sight with Kassandra.

## Canon
Ikaros is Kassandra's eagle companion in Assassin's Creed Odyssey. Pythagoras sent him to watch over her, though Kassandra does not initially know that history.

## Chapter 3 State
Ikaros arrives from the Kephallonian mountains during Kassandra's adolescence and chooses to remain with her. His daylight and open-sky scouting complements Glaux's night and enclosed-ruin role.

## Behavior
Intelligent animal companion. He does not speak or deliver exposition.
