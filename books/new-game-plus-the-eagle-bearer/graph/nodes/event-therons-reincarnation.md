# Event: Theron's Reincarnation

> id: event-therons-reincarnation | type: event | canonicity: original

## Overview
The Prologue. A bored modern salaryman plays videogames to numb his life. One night, watching the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla, a mundane accident at home kills him. He wakes as a nameless orphan boy on a beach in Kephallonia, and the Assassin's Creed System boots up in his mind with a "beginner gift pack."
