# Character: Hermes Trismegistus

## Overview
The legendary sage for whom the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus was forged around 2200 IE, granting immortality and command over Isu systems.

## Canon
First wielder of the Staff; the artifact passes down through Pythagoras, Kassandra, Layla, and Basim.
