# Event: The First Catastrophe

## Overview
A massive coronal mass ejection (solar flare) around 75,000 BCE that devastated Earth, killing most humans and Isu. Jupiter noted 'less than ten thousand of your kind still lived.' Historically misattributed to the Toba supervolcano.

## Canon
The event that ended Isu civilization and nearly ended humanity.
