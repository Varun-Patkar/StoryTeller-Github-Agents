# Event: The Marriage Vows

## Overview
In chapter 33, before the full family gathering, Theron and Kassandra exchange private vows and enter the household as spouses. The marriage preserves equal standing and the chapter-25 rule that love grants neither command over the other.

## Consequences
They face Atlantis, System disclosure, immortality, parenthood, and bereavement as a married pair. The vows replace no canon ceremony; they formalize this story's original slow-burn relationship.
