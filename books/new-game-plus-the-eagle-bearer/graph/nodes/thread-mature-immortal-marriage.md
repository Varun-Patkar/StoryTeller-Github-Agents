# Thread: Mature Immortal Marriage

## Overview
After the slow burn resolves, Theron and Kassandra build a marriage that survives operational conflict, parenthood, immortality, and repeated bereavement. Their equality is practical: neither commands the other, plans disclose assigned risks, field judgment remains free, and both owe an accounting afterward.

## Milestones
- Chapter 25: terms of equal partnership are spoken.
- Chapter 28: first couple's spat repairs the risk-disclosure promise through changed procedure.
- Chapter 33: private marriage vows.
- Chapters 34-38: System disclosure and separate immortal choices test full trust.
- Chapter 39: children, spats across years, and family deaths leave each as the other's only permanent contemporary.
- Chapter 40: Theron opens from brooding in his wife's presence; Kassandra sets down public hardness with her husband.

## Guardrail
Their dependence is freely renewed companionship, not contempt for mortal family and not relationship angst.
