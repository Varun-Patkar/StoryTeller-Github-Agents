# Character: Perikles

> id: character-perikles | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Athens' foremost statesman, host of the famous symposium, and a Cult target during the plague.

## Canon
Assassinated by Deimos/Alexios during the Plague of Athens.

## AU Divergence
Theron knows Perikles is doomed. Whether Perikles can be saved is a hard case (his death is historically fixed and heavily guarded); at minimum Theron uses foreknowledge to protect Phoibe and turn the same night into the start of Alexios's rescue rather than pure tragedy.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Statesmanlike, patient, tired.
- "In Athens, sometimes you must play the role to get what you want."

## Notes
Introduces the Athens political cast.
