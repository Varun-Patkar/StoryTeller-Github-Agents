# Character: Phoibe

> id: character-phoibe | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A quick, fearless orphan girl who idolises Kassandra. Part of the Kephallonia family; later runs errands for Aspasia in Athens.

## Canon
In Odyssey, Phoibe is murdered by the Cult in Athens during the plague, one of the story's gut-punch deaths.

## AU Divergence
**Phoibe does NOT die.** Theron knows the exact time and place of her canonical death and engineers her survival (getting her out of Athens, or intercepting the Cult guards before the Odeon). She lives to be part of the found family and the late reunion dinner. This is one of Theron's flagship "save everyone" victories.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Chirpy, blunt, hero-worshipping, wise beyond her years.
- "When I grow up, I'm going to be a misthios just like you!"
- "You look tired. You always look tired now."
- "I carved you an eagle. Don't lose it."

## Notes
Her survival is a tonal pivot: the moment readers realise this really is a fix-it story.
