# Item: The Adrestia

> id: item-the-adrestia | type: item | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The trireme captained by Barnabas; the crew's mobile home, naval-combat platform, and island-hopping vehicle. Later a testbed for Theron's Isu-tech tinkering.

## Canon
Kassandra's ship throughout Odyssey.

## AU Divergence
Becomes shared crew for the Theron/Kassandra partnership and, in Act Two, a canvas for "impossible" upgrades.

## Notes
Base of operations for the Odyssey span.
