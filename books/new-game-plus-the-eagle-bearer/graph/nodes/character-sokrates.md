# Character: Sokrates

> id: character-sokrates | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Athens' gadfly philosopher, forever asking uncomfortable questions about right action.

## Canon
Recurring conscience-figure in Athens.

## AU Divergence
Theron enjoys sparring with him — a modern mind against a classical one — and quietly steals a few of Sokrates's own ideas back at him.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Probing, ironic, relentless.
- "But is it just, what you did? Or merely effective?"

## Notes
Thematic foil for the "saving everyone" ethic.
