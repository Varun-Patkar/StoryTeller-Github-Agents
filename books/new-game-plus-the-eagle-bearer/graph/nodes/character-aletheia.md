# Character: Aletheia

## Overview
An Isu who served as Dikastes (judge) of Atlantis and lover of Loki. Her consciousness guides those who wield the Staff of Hermes; she ran the simulations of the Fate of Atlantis.

## Canon
Voice within the Staff guiding Kassandra and later Layla; her consciousness was stored by Loki so she could reunite with his reborn self.
