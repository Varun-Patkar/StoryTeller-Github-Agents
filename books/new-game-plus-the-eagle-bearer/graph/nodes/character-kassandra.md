# Character: Kassandra

## Overview
Born 458 BCE, Kassandra is a Spartan-descended misthios, granddaughter of Leonidas, wielder of the Spear of Leonidas, and the Eagle Bearer. She carries strong Isu DNA and becomes immortal through the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus. In this story she is the second protagonist, Theron's equal partner, wife, and permanent counterweight.

## Canon
Daughter of Myrrine and Pythagoras, half-sister of Deimos/Alexios. Thrown from Mount Taygetos as a child, raised by Markos on Kephallonia, she rises as a misthios, destroys the Cult of Kosmos, and becomes the Keeper guided by Aletheia.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Blunt, wry, and direct, with a soldier's tenderness beneath practical severity. Use malaka only when she is actively cursing in anger, never as routine punctuation.
- "By the gods, you talk too much for a dead man."
- "I have buried everyone I loved. Do not ask me to do it again and mean it."
- "You think too far ahead, Theron. Sometimes the knife in front of you is the whole world."
- "Leave her, malaka!"
- "Family is a wound for me. Be careful where you press."

## AU Divergence
- Raised beside Theron. They grow from siblings-in-arms to equal partners, lovers, spouses, parents, and twin immortals.
- Her canonical romance with Natakas and son Elpidios are cut entirely. She and Theron later choose at least two children together.
- Her family is healed: Alexios is deprogrammed, Nikolaos and Stentor reconcile, Phoibe lives, and Brasidas survives Amphipolis.
- She inherits the Staff and becomes immortal, but Theron shares eternity with her.

## Arc & Growth
- Chapters 11-16: she breaks procedure when a plan abandons people, establishing the disruptive judgment that balances Theron's systems.
- Chapters 17-25: she hardens against coercive institutions while preserving compassion for individuals. She accepts love only with explicit equality and field autonomy.
- Chapters 26-33: Darius, Alexios, and Aspasia teach her that destruction without memory allows domination to regrow. She repairs conflict with Theron and marries him.
- Chapters 34-38: Elysium, the Underworld, and Atlantis expose the danger of both imposed order and power without restraint. She chooses immortality freely and helps kill Aletheia.
- Chapter 39: she raises mortal children, outlives every child and loved one, and becomes a public hardliner who destroys coercive systems before they metastasize.
- Chapter 40: her mature form is a chaos-leaning Eclipse founder. She protects dissent, field discretion, emergency disruption, and anti-dynastic rules. Her severity targets domination rather than disagreement.

## Mature Marriage Dynamic
Kassandra mellows in private with Theron. She yields space, admits uncertainty, and lets tenderness interrupt certainty when they are alone. She is the person who can draw him out of brooding isolation; he is the person before whom she can set down public hardness. Their spats are real but never threaten the underlying choice to remain equal partners.

## Chapter 10 State
Kassandra upgrades the Spear through inherited compatibility at Andros. She holds back during the Alexios clash because defeating him safely is not yet possible, then chooses the prepared withdrawal after a memory of Myrrine briefly reaches him.
