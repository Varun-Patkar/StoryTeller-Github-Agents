# Character: Duris

## Overview
A pottery merchant in Sami who owes Markos for an oil shipment.

## Canon
Kassandra is sent to collect Markos's inflated demand and may intimidate Duris or forgive the debt.

## AU Divergence
Theron proves that the agreed debt was 100 drachmae. Duris pays that amount, his wares remain intact, and the account closes without violence.
