# Character: Darius

> id: character-darius | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The mythic assassin who first used a hidden blade to kill Xerxes, now an aging fugitive fleeing the Order of the Ancients through Greece.

## Canon
Central to the Legacy of the First Blade DLC, alongside his son Natakas; canonically Kassandra romances Natakas and bears Elpidios.

## AU Divergence
**The Natakas romance and the child Elpidios are cut entirely** — Kassandra loves Theron, not Natakas. Darius is reframed as an optional ally and mentor: the man who invented the first hidden blade fascinates Theron (the tech-minded protagonist), and the Order of the Ancients remains a threat the pair help fight. No forced-family subplot.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Weary, lethal, haunted.
- "I have buried everyone I loved. Do not ask me to bury more."
- "The blade is not the weapon. Patience is."

## Notes
Bridges Odyssey to the deep lore of the Assassins/Hidden Ones without the canon romance.
