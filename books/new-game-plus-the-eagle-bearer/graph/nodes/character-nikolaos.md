# Character: Nikolaos

> id: character-nikolaos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
"The Wolf of Sparta." Kassandra's step-father, who raised her as his own before Spartan law and the Cult's manipulation forced him to drop her from Mount Taygetos.

## Canon
Kassandra confronts and (canonically) spares him in Megaris; he later helps Stentor from the shadows and seeks to repair his family.

## AU Divergence
His redemption is deepened and he survives to the warm family reunion dinner (Nikolaos, Myrrine, Kassandra, Alexios, Stentor, plus Theron as the son-in-law figure).

## Voice & Mannerisms
Gruff, formal, weighed down by guilt and Spartan duty.
- "I did what Sparta demanded. It does not mean it was right."
- "You are not my blood. You were always my daughter."

## Notes
Central to healing Kassandra's betrayal wound.
