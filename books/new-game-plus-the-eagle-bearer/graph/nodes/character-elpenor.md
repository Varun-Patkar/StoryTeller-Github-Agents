# Character: Elpenor

> id: character-elpenor | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A disguised Cult of Kosmos agent who manipulates Kassandra into her first big contract and opens the door to the Cult conspiracy.

## Canon
Hires Kassandra to kill Nikolaos, then tries to have her kill more family; assassinated at Delphi.

## AU Divergence
Theron recognises the setup immediately and plays along to harvest Cult intelligence early, accelerating the campaign against the Cult.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Oily, superior, evasive.
- "I am the reason you left that island alive."

## Notes
Onramp to the Cult arc.
