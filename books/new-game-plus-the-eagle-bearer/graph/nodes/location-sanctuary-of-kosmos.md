# Location: Sanctuary of Kosmos

## Overview
A circular Cult chamber hidden beneath the Temple of Apollo at Delphi and built inside an older Isu memory assembly. A western Greek preparation room conceals the main stair, while an Isu service shaft reaches the Pyramid housing.

## Chapter 9 State
The chamber contains six branch recesses organized around the Pyramid. Kassandra enters by the masked stair and Theron by the service shaft. Their fire, smoke, and structural escape expose the gathering without destroying the sanctuary.
