# Location: Ancient Forge

## Overview
An Isu fabrication chamber built inside the eastern ridge of Andros. Its surviving machinery reads inherited genetic compatibility, routes artifact power, repairs access paths in compatible weapons, and exposes service interfaces to authorized tools.

## Chapter 10 State
The forge upgrades Kassandra's Spear through her inherited compatibility. Theron records its machinery, power routing, and genetic access behavior, then uses a one-use System adapter to bridge the damaged Forge Key to the active service network.
