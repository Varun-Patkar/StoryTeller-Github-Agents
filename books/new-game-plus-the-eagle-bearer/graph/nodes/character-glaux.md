# Character: Glaux

## Overview
Small Isu-engineered female owl found near death in the quarry cache and restored by Theron through a repaired Forge Key circuit in chapter 3.

## Chapter 3 State
Glaux recovers over the following month. After years of training she shares sight with Theron for scouting at night and inside ruins. Her role complements Ikaros rather than replacing him.

## Voice and Behavior
Glaux remains an intelligent animal companion. She never speaks or becomes an exposition device.

## AU Divergence
Theron's counterpart to Ikaros. Her silhouette becomes the Eclipse symbol.
