# Concept: Pieces of Eden

## Overview
Isu artifacts made of 'adamant,' named for the city of Eden. Types include Apples (mind control), Staves, Swords (energy and charisma), Shrouds (healing), Spears, and Rings. They exploit the control-systems the Isu built into humans.

## Canon
The MacGuffins driving most of the Assassin-Templar conflict.
