# Location: Kleptous Bay

## Overview
A defended inlet on western Kephallonia used by the Cyclops as his final camp after his local collection network collapses.

## Chapter 5
The Cyclops holds Barnabas, part of the Adrestia's crew, and his remaining records here. Kassandra and Theron attack the camp, free the prisoners, kill the Cyclops, and reclaim the Adrestia.
