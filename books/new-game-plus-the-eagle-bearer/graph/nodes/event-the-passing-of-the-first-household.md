# Event: The Passing of the First Household

## Overview
Chapter 39 spans the complete mortal lives and natural deaths of Theron and Kassandra's children, family, and companions. The immortal spouses remain physically young while every loved one reaches an ending in a distinct season.

## Meaning
This is accumulated grief, not a sequence of disposable tragedies. Theron and Kassandra saved people so they could live, not so mortality would be abolished. Their children choose independent lives and are not forced into immortality.

## Consequences
Theron becomes brooding, patient, and old-minded. Kassandra becomes a public hardliner. Each softens with the other, now the only person who remembers every shared generation firsthand.
