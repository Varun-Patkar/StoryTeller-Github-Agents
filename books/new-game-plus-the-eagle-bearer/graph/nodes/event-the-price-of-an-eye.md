# Event: The Price of an Eye

## Overview
Phoibe warns of the Cyclops's collectors and serves as a controlled signal runner. Theron divides the vineyard terrain and Kassandra defeats the crew. They use the captured seal and route list to raid the Kranioi warehouse, recover the 2,400-drachma obsidian eye, expose the network, and return seized property.

## Contract Outcome
Duris pays the honest 100-drachma principal without damage to his pottery. Theron spends 4 dr 3 ob on preparation, receives a 12 dr 3 ob contract share, and ends at 326 dr 4 ob.

## Consequence
The Cyclops enters Chapter 5 isolated and provoked. Markos's vineyard fraud is documented, and the intact eye remains capital and leverage.
