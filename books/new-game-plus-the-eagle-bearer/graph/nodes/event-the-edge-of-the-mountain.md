# Event: The Edge of the Mountain

## Overview
Chapter 7 closes the Megaris arc. Kassandra confronts Nikolaos above Fort Geraneia and spares him by her own choice while Theron captures two hired blades positioned to force his death.

## Established State
- Nikolaos reveals that he is not Kassandra's birth father and directs her toward Myrrine.
- Kassandra withholds forgiveness while requiring Nikolaos to live with and repair the harm he caused.
- Theron tells Nikolaos to shadow Stentor and intervene before the adopted siblings meet as enemies later.
- The unsigned attack order points Kassandra and Theron back toward Elpenor in Phokis without proving his role yet.
- Theron spends 6 drachmae and 2 obols on bronze wire and rope, ending at 546 drachmae and 2 obols.

## AU Divergence
The story locks the spare outcome, prevents outside interference from deciding it, and turns Nikolaos's survival into the first concrete step toward the later family reconciliation.
