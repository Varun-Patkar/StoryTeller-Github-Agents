# Location: Andros

## Overview
A rocky Aegean island containing an active Isu fabrication site inside its eastern ridge.

## Canon
Kassandra visits Andros to use the Ancient Forge and upgrade the Spear of Leonidas.

## Chapter 10 State
Kassandra, Theron, and Herodotos reach the island aboard the Adrestia. Alexios follows them and confronts Kassandra outside the forge before their prepared withdrawal.
