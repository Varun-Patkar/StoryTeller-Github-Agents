# Character: Markos

> id: character-markos | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A well-meaning, scheming, chronically indebted merchant of Kephallonia who took Kassandra under his wing after she washed ashore, and (in this story) took in Theron too. He buys vineyards with borrowed money, over-promises, and somehow keeps his little family fed.

## Canon
In Odyssey, Markos is Kassandra's early employer and father-figure on Kephallonia, whose debts to the Cyclops kick off her mercenary career.

## AU Divergence
In this story Markos raises Theron alongside Kassandra. He becomes a cornerstone of the "found family" that heals Kassandra's wound of familial betrayal, and survives to attend the warm reunion dinner late in the story.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Fast-talking, optimistic, always one deal away from being rich, sheepish when caught.
- "Trust me! Have I ever steered you wrong? ...Don't answer that."
- "It's an investment! A vineyard practically prints drachmae."
- "You two are the best thing I ever swindled my way into."

## Notes
Anchor of the Kephallonia arc and the found-family theme.
