# Character: Stentor

> id: character-stentor | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A rigid, loyal Spartan officer, adopted son of Nikolaos, who resents Kassandra for the supposed death of their father.

## Canon
Antagonistic through Megaris and Boeotia; reconciles once Nikolaos reveals he lives.

## AU Divergence
Reconciliation is smoothed by Theron's mediation; Stentor joins the found-family dinner.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Barking, proud, brittle under the armour.
- "The gods are punishing me with your presence."
- "You expect me to trust a misthios?"

## Notes
Family-reunion payoff character.
