# Character: Brasidas

> id: character-brasidas | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
A principled Spartan general who fights the Cult of Kosmos from within Sparta's structures, preferring justice to vengeance.

## Canon
Ally in Korinth, Arkadia, Sparta, and the Cult hunt; dies at Amphipolis in canon.

## AU Divergence
Theron aims to keep him alive at Amphipolis (part of "no meaningless deaths"): foreknowledge lets him break the ambush that kills Brasidas in canon.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Measured, honourable, dryly warm.
- "Kill him if you must. But do it cleanly, and for the right reason."
- "Sparta is not the Cult. Remember that."

## Notes
Save-target; embodiment of the balance theme.
