# Character: Kleon

> id: character-kleon | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The populist firebrand who seizes Athens after Perikles, secretly a Cult Sage.

## Canon
Rules Athens post-Perikles; killed at Amphipolis.

## AU Divergence
Theron exposes and out-maneuvers him earlier, folding his defeat into the Amphipolis climax where the family is reunited.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Loud, populist, sneering.
- "The people love me. That is a kind of power you will never buy."

## Notes
Political face of the Cult.
