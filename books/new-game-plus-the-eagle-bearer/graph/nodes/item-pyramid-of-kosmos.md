# Item: Pyramid of Kosmos

## Overview
An Isu memory device kept at the center of the Cult sanctuary beneath Apollo's temple. The Cult uses blood contact to retrieve and return selected memory patterns, treating the results as prophecy and authority.

## Chapter 9 State
Kassandra's blood and Alexios's stronger response expose the device's recognition, retrieval, pressure, and feedback sequence. Theron's Forge Key captures three raw architecture fragments at 0% comprehension. The Pyramid briefly links Alexios, Kassandra, and Theron through the Key before Theron disconnects.

## Limits
The captured architecture does not identify every Cultist, explain the machine, or provide a cure. It is the first technical lead toward restoring Alexios's agency.
