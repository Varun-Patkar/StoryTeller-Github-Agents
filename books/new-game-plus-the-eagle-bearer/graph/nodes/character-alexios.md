# Character: Alexios

## Overview
Kassandra's younger half-brother, taken by the Cult of Kosmos as an infant after Mount Taygetos and tortured into their living weapon, "Deimos." Carries strong Isu DNA like Kassandra.

## Canon
Believed dead after being dropped from Mount Taygetos, he was secretly raised and indoctrinated by the Cult. As Deimos he serves as their enforcer, wielding the Sword of Damokles (a Piece of Eden). Canonically he can be redeemed or lost depending on the player's choices, and any redemption remains fragile and scarred.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Cold, wounded, oscillating between arrogance and buried confusion.
- "My name is Deimos."
- "You threw me from that mountain. Do not speak to me of family."
- "The Cult made me a god. What did the world ever make you?"
(Post-cure, warmer, halting:) "I don't... remember how to be your brother. Teach me."

## AU Divergence
- **He is genuinely saved.** Rather than a fragile talking-down, Theron reverse-engineers the Isu memory technology (the Pyramid, Animus-adjacent principles, and later the Staff) to actually undo the Cult's conditioning and repair the trauma-driven brainwashing (see event: Deprogramming Alexios). Alexios is restored to himself and to the family.
- **Restored family member.** He reconciles with Myrrine, Nikolaos, Stentor, and Kassandra, and takes his place at the In-Laws Dinner. No longer a tragedy — a rescue.
- His deprogramming is the proof-of-concept that launches Theron's full science pivot.

## Notes
Flagship "save everyone" target; his cure demonstrates Isu-tech-as-technology and seeds the memory-rewriting toolkit reused later.
