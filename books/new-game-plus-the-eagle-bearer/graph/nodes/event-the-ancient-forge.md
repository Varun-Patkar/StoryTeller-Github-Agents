# Event: The Ancient Forge

## Overview
Chapter 10. Kassandra upgrades the Spear at the Andros forge while Theron spends 1,200 drachmae on a one-use interface adapter, raising the Forge Key to 51.6% integrity with 36 captured and 5 understood fragments.

## Outcomes
- The forge records machinery, power routing, and genetic access behavior.
- A prepared clash proves Kassandra cannot safely overpower Alexios and Theron cannot yet undo his conditioning.
- A family memory reaches Alexios, but Cult pressure returns through the same artifact path.
- The group withdraws by design and sails for Athens.
- Theron's ending balance is 306 dr 2 ob.
