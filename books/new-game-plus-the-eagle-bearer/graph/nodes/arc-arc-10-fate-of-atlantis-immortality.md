# Arc: Arc 10, Fate of Atlantis and Immortality

## Overview
Chapters 34-40 carry the artifact gates, Staff, compressed simulations, System revelation, twin immortality, Aletheia's death, mortal generations, and founding of the Eclipse.

## Chapter Span
Chapters 34-40.

## Progression
- Chapters 34-37 test the newly married pair against imposed order and predatory disorder.
- Chapter 38 completes the System revelation, twin immortality, and killing of Aletheia.
- Chapter 39 uses selective dated jumps for children, family aging, natural deaths, accumulated grief, and final personality maturation.
- Chapter 40 founds the Eclipse from Theron's order instinct and Kassandra's chaos instinct, with each checking the other.

## Boundary
End with the mature founders ready for the next era without specifying a destination.
