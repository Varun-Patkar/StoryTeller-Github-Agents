# Event: Recovery at the Old Limestone Cut

## Overview
In Chapter 2, Theron and Kassandra recover six oil jars stolen from Markos by three thieves.

## Outcome
Theron scouts the quarry, buys rope and bandages through the secret System, and sets a tripline. Kassandra defeats the thieves as they are separated. Theron uses his new knife without seriously injuring anyone. All six jars return intact enough to sell, and Markos pays each child 3 drachmae and 4 obols.

## Character State
Theron's balance ends at 5 drachmae and 1 obol. He and Kassandra agree that future plans must disclose any risk Theron assigns himself.

## Canonicity
Original event created by Theron's AU presence during Kassandra's otherwise unrecorded childhood on Kephallonia.
