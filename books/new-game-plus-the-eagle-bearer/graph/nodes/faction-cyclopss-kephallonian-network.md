# Faction: Cyclops's Kephallonian Network

## Overview
The Cyclops's island system of debt tablets, collectors, warehouse guards, confiscated goods, and paid officials.

## Chapter 4 State
Theron and Kassandra take its route list, ledger, seal, collateral records, and obsidian eye. Phoibe distributes route copies, debtors reclaim property, the eye seal is broken, and the network fails before the Cyclops can respond.
