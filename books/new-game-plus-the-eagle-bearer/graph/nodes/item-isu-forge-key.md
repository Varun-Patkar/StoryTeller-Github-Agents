# Item: Isu Forge Key

## Overview
Damaged Isu engineering bracer recovered by Theron and Kassandra from the quarry cache in chapter 3. Its surviving functions are material analysis, energy-path tracing, partial Isu-language translation, blueprint capture, and access to compatible machinery.

## Chapter 3 State
The Key begins at 17.4% integrity and 2.1% charge. Theron restores it through fifteen years of physical study and repair to 46% integrity. It contains 27 captured blueprint fragments, only four of which he understands well enough to build.

## Limits
It does not create matter, grant free knowledge, solve puzzles, or replace System purchases. Theron must repair, power, understand, and build from its records.

## AU Divergence
Provides Theron a technology-path artifact equal in narrative importance, but not identical power, to Kassandra's Spear of Leonidas.
