# Event: The Twin Immortals

> id: event-the-twin-immortals | type: event | canonicity: au

## Overview
The payoff of Act One: Kassandra inherits the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus (as in canon) and Theron secures his own immortality through the System (concentrated Isu blood + Isu longevity, bartered for with recovered artifacts). Both now immortal and in love, they set out to wander the mortal world, fix the future, and end the Isu threat.

## Canon
Kassandra becomes immortal via the Staff; she is alone.

## AU Divergence
Theron becomes immortal too, and they face eternity together.

## Notes
Bridge from power-fantasy Act One to slice-of-life Act Two (ch 71+).
