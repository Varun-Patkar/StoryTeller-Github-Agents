# Event: The Twin Immortals

## Overview
In chapter 38, Kassandra accepts the Staff of Hermes Trismegistus with informed safeguards. Theron barters accumulated artifacts and data, then pays the exact System price for concentrated Isu blood and engineered longevity. They become physically young immortals by separate, freely chosen routes.

## Canon
Kassandra becomes immortal via the Staff and continues alone.

## AU Divergence
Theron becomes immortal too. They face Aletheia as spouses, expend irreplaceable resources to kill her, and refuse to found an order until lived decades test their conclusions.

## Consequences
They raise mortal children, outlive every loved one, and remain each other's only permanent contemporary. Immortality creates grief and responsibility rather than making mortal lives disposable.
