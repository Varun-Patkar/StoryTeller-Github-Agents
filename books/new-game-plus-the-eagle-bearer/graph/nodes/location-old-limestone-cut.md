# Location: Old Limestone Cut

## Overview
An abandoned quarry less than an hour north of Sami, reached by a cart path and a western goat track.

## Chapter 2 State
Three thieves used the quarry to hold Markos's stolen cart and six oil jars. A straight drainage channel exposes darker fitted stone beneath the later limestone workings, suggesting an older buried structure.

## Canonicity
Original local location on Kephallonia.
