# Character: Theron and Kassandra's Second Child

## Overview
The younger of at least two children chosen and raised by Theron and Kassandra during the chapter-39 time span. The personal name, gender, vocation, and exact lifespan remain intentionally open for later planning.

## Canon
No canon counterpart.

## AU Divergence
Kassandra's canon Natakas relationship and Elpidios are removed. This child belongs to her chosen marriage with Theron.

## Story Function
Lives a complete mortal life distinct from the elder sibling's, disagrees with the immortal parents in age-appropriate ways, and dies naturally. The life prevents the household from becoming a dynastic shortcut for the Eclipse.
