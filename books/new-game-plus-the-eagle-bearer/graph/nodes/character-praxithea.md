# Character: Praxithea

## Overview
The current Pythia at Delphi, forced by the Cult of Kosmos to control which questions and answers pass through Apollo's oracle.

## Canon
The Pythia is held under Cult pressure and provides Kassandra information about the conspiracy beneath Delphi.

## Chapter 8 State
Kassandra and Theron disable the warning network at her guarded house. Praxithea confirms that the Cult authored the oracle against Leonidas's bloodline, that Myrrine survived Delphi, and that the Cult meets beneath the Temple of Apollo. She chooses to remain near her family and feed false information to the Cult's messenger.

## Voice and Mannerisms
Careful, direct, and practiced at speaking around danger. She makes her own decisions once given room to do so.

## AU Divergence
Becomes a covert source after Theron and Kassandra break the immediate Cult control around her house.
