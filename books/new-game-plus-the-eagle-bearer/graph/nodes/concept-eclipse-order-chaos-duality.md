# Concept: Eclipse Order-Chaos Duality

## Overview
The Eclipse is balanced internally by its founders rather than by an abstract midpoint. Theron leans toward order and Kassandra leans toward chaos.

## Theron's Pole
Archives, evidence standards, layered cells, long strategy, science, continuity, and institutions capable of surviving their founders.

## Kassandra's Pole
Protected dissent, field discretion, disruptive correction, anti-dynastic limits, and decisive action against coercive structures.

## Mutual Checks
Every durable rule has review and dissent. Every disruptive authority has accountability afterward. Theron prevents chaos from wasting memory and trust; Kassandra prevents order from becoming permanent control.

## Origin
The duality develops progressively from Athens through Atlantis, parenthood, and bereavement. It is fully realized when they found the Eclipse in chapter 40.
