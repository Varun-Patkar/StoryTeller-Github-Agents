# Event: Founding of the Eclipse

## Overview
In chapter 40, after decades of marriage, parenthood, and bereavement, Theron and Kassandra establish the Eclipse as equal founders.

## Structure
Theron creates archives, evidence standards, layered cells, and continuity safeguards. Kassandra creates protected dissent, field discretion, disruptive authority, and anti-dynastic limits. Neither founder outranks the other.

## Creed
No truth is absolute. No order eternal.

## Boundary
The event completes both mature character forms and closes Act One without specifying the next destination.
