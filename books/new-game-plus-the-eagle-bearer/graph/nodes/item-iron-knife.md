# Item: Iron Knife

## Overview
A plain iron utility and fighting knife purchased by Theron from the System shop in Chapter 2 for 4 drachmae.

## Story State
Theron carries it in a leather sheath beneath his tunic. Kassandra has begun teaching him how to hold, retain, and use it safely. During the oil recovery, he used it to cut a thief's sandal strap and later loosen the tripline knot.

## Canonicity
Original story item. It is a common tool rather than a unique artifact.
