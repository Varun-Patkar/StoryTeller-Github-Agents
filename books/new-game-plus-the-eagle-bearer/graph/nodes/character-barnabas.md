# Character: Barnabas

> id: character-barnabas | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
The warm, superstitious captain of the ship Adrestia, rescued by Kassandra from the Cyclops and devoted to her ever after.

## Canon
Kassandra's ship captain and friend throughout Odyssey.

## AU Divergence
Becomes crew to the Theron/Kassandra partnership; his tall tales are often accidentally true, which Theron finds endlessly useful.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Booming, pious, story-loving.
- "The gods favour us today, misthios! Or they're setting a trap. One of the two."

## Notes
Enables travel across the map.

## Chapter 10 State
Barnabas executes Theron prepared offshore extraction plan at Andros and sails the group safely toward Athens after the clash.
