# Character: Aspasia

> id: character-aspasia | type: character | canonicity: canon

## Overview
Perikles's sharp, charismatic partner who mentors Phoibe and aids Kassandra — and is secretly the Ghost of Kosmos, founder and hidden head of the Cult of Kosmos.

## Canon
Revealed as the Ghost after the Cult is dismantled; believed the Cult could bring order but lost control of it. Kassandra chooses whether to kill or spare her.

## AU Divergence
Theron knows her identity from the start. He plays a long game: using her to dismantle the Cult from within while protecting Phoibe (whom Aspasia unknowingly endangers), and confronts the Ghost reveal on his terms. Her fate is a deliberate choice, not a shock.

## Voice & Mannerisms
Silken, philosophical, always three moves ahead.
- "Order is a kind of mercy. Most people cannot be trusted with freedom."
- "You think you are unravelling my work. You are completing it."

## Notes
Primary human antagonist of the Odyssey span; the Cult's true head.
