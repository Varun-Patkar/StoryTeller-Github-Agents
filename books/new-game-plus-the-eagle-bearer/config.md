| Synopsis | A bored modern gamer dies at his desk mid-way through the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla and wakes as a nameless orphan on the beaches of Kephallonia, taken in by the merchant Markos alongside a fierce girl named Kassandra. He has no Isu blood of his own, only a cheat: the Assassin's Creed System, whose beginner gift is a talent boost that grows across every attribute (including a perfect memory of every game he ever played) and one percent Isu blood, plus a vast shop that sells anything in the universe to anyone who can pay in drachmae, orichalcum, or the artifacts of dead gods. Armed with foreknowledge and a modern imagination, Theron sets out to break the story: to save Phoibe, to cure Alexios of the Cult's brainwashing, to spare the people the games doomed, and to win the heart of the Eagle Bearer the slow, honest way. By the seventieth chapter he and Kassandra are immortal and in love, an Isu consciousness lies dead by his hand, and the real work begins: two deathless people wandering the mortal world, out-thinking the First Civilization, and racing to save humanity from the sun. A fix-it power fantasy that grows into an eternity-long slice of life. |
