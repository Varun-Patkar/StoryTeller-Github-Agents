| Synopsis | A bored modern gamer dies at his desk mid-way through the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla and wakes as a nameless orphan on the beaches of Kephallonia, taken in by the merchant Markos alongside a fierce girl named Kassandra. His Assassin's Creed System grants a talent boost that grows across every attribute, perfect memory of every game he played, one percent Isu blood, and a concrete shop fueled by drachmae, orichalcum, and artifacts. Beneath Kephallonia he recovers a damaged Isu Forge Key and bonds with the owl Glaux, setting him on a mental and technological path equal to Kassandra's raw power. Armed with foreknowledge and modern imagination, Theron sets out to save Phoibe, cure Alexios, spare the people the games doomed, and win the Eagle Bearer's heart the slow, honest way. By chapter forty they are immortal and in love, Aletheia is dead, and they have founded the Eclipse, a hidden order that keeps freedom and control in balance while preparing humanity to survive the sun. A fix-it power fantasy that grows into an eternity-long slice of life. |
