# Story Plan: New Game Plus: The Eagle Bearer

> Fanfiction, Assassin's Creed (Odyssey era, then the wider timeline).
> Mode: Here for the Ride. POV: Third Person Limited (Theron). Pacing: Long (3000-5000 chars).
> Style target: natural conversational webnovel prose, varied rhythm, grounded detail. **No em dashes** in story text (use commas, periods, ellipses).

## Logline

A modern gamer reincarnates as a Kephallonian orphan beside young Kassandra, armed with the **Assassin's Creed System**, perfect memory of every game, and a talent that compounds with age. Beneath an abandoned quarry he claims a damaged Isu Forge Key and bonds with the owl Glaux, giving the future Eagle Bearer an equal partner on the mental and technological path. Together they compress the tragedy of Assassin's Creed Odyssey into a fix-it story: saving the doomed, curing the broken, and falling in love slowly. By chapter 40 they stand immortal over the remains of an Isu god and establish the Eclipse, a hidden order that keeps freedom and control from consuming each other.

## Two-Act Shape

- **Act One, Power Fantasy to Long Life (prologue + ch 1-40):** The AC Odyssey main story and DLC beats, compressed and re-steered by Theron's foreknowledge. Childhood ends in chapter 3. Chapters 4-38 move fast, skipping routine travel and minor contracts; chapters 39-40 use controlled time jumps to show the marriage, children, mortal family, accumulated grief, and final maturation that make the Eclipse's doctrine earned. Act One ends with both leads immortal, Aletheia dead, their mortal generations laid to rest, and the Eclipse founded. Tone: rising and warm, then reflective without becoming hopeless.
- **Act Two, The Long Road (ch 41-540, to be continued):** Two immortals guide history from the shadows, recover Pieces of Eden, keep the future Assassins and Templars in dynamic balance, out-think surviving Isu, and race the coming solar catastrophe. Tone: cosy, episodic, occasionally vast. Only skeletoned here.

## Compression Rules

- **Chapters 1 and 2 are locked canon. Do not rewrite, retcon, summarize over, or renumber them.** Chapter 3 must begin from Theron's exact ending balance of **5 drachmae, 1 obol**, the quarry wall, his iron knife, his training under Kassandra, and his promise to disclose risks assigned to himself.
- **Childhood ends in chapter 3.** Resolve the buried-wall thread, establish the artifact, owl, and both forms of Eagle Vision, then use clean time jumps to reach the adult Odyssey opening before the chapter ends.
- **Every chapter from 3 onward has a specific job below.** Writers may rearrange scene order inside a chapter, but may not move a major payoff into a later chapter without updating this plan first.
- **Compress connective canon.** Fold minor quests, clue collection, sailing, conquest battles, and routine Cultist kills into transitions or montages. Preserve only choices, relationships, discoveries, reversals, and costs that change the story.
- **Act One ends in chapter 40.** Phoibe, Brasidas, and the core family live; Alexios is cured; the Cult falls; Kassandra and Theron become immortal; Aletheia dies; the Eclipse is founded.

## Protagonist Rules (hard constraints)

- **Never loses on information.** Theron has played every AC game; the System boosted his memory (MEM stat) to perfect recall. He always knows what is coming.
- **Genuinely OP, not a tag-along.** Theron is powerful in his own right and grows fast. His Enhanced Talent trait compounds with age, so his attributes climb past normal human limits over the arc, and his 1% (later higher) Isu blood sharpens his reflexes and mind. He wins through preparation, tech, and System-bought edges AND through real personal capability. He can be tricked or physically overwhelmed by something stronger, but he always has a backup plan and always overcomes; losses are temporary and set up a comeback. Do not write him weak or helpless.
- **Equal standing by the adult story opening.** Kassandra enters chapter 4 with the Spear of Leonidas, Ikaros, immense hybrid strength, and broad combat-focused Eagle Vision. Theron enters with the damaged Isu Forge Key, Glaux, trained physical stats, concentrated analytical Eagle Vision, and years of recovered technical knowledge. Kassandra remains the raw physical powerhouse; equal standing means equal narrative agency, unique capability, and importance, not identical strength.
- **Tech path; Kassandra is brute force.** Theron's lane is science, planning, Isu tech, gadgets, and preparation. Kassandra is the raw physical powerhouse. He is the mind and the toolmaker; she is the scalpel. Even early, lean into this division rather than making him out-muscle people.
- **Never written as stupid.** He uses pre-knowledge wherever it plausibly helps and hides what he cannot explain (laundering it through Herodotos, Sokrates, Hippokrates, "hunches," or quiet preparation).
- **The System is a budget, not a genie, and it has REAL NUMBERS.** Render the System as a concrete LitRPG layer (status screen, visible drachmae/obol balance, itemized prices, pings), exactly like the writing samples, never as a vague glowing pane. See the `ability-assassins-creed-system` node for canonical stats, currency, and prices. Game-changers cost astronomical, specific sums; everyday purchases have exact prices and update his running balance. He earns, barters, and engineers his wins.
- **The Forge Key is a damaged tool, not a second System.** It is an Isu engineering bracer recovered in chapter 3. Its surviving functions are material analysis, energy-path tracing, Isu-language translation, blueprint capture, and access to compatible machinery. It does not create matter, grant free knowledge, solve puzzles by itself, or replace System purchases. Theron must repair, power, understand, and build from what it records.
- **The System is a SECRET until ch 38.** No in-world character knows Theron has a System, a shop, stats, or any "game" until he chooses to tell **Kassandra**, late, near their immortality. The concept of a LitRPG interface or modern technology does not exist in their world, so no one can even guess it. Characters may notice he "goes somewhere" (stares, tunes out) or "says strange things," and will write it off as daydreaming or eccentricity, never as a system. Theron never opens the shop, reads stats, or uses modern/gamer language out loud where it could be understood. All System beats stay in his internal narration.
- **His feelings hide even from himself.** The romance is slow and self-concealed. Theron does not consciously recognize his own feelings for Kassandra for a long time, and once he does, he hides them well. Early chapters are found-family and partnership, not pining. Other characters (especially Kassandra) must NOT read romantic or knowing looks off him early on. She may tease him for being odd or distant, never for looking at her "a certain way."
- **No romantic or sexual subtext with anyone except Kassandra.** Bonds with everyone else (Markos, Phoibe, Barnabas, allies) are plainly platonic. With Phoibe (a child) it is purely protective/older-sibling. NEVER use possessive or ownership framing for these bonds (no "keep her," "hers to keep," "mine"); write plain protective language instead.
- **Final-character forms are progressive and complete by chapter 40.** Theron increasingly favors durable rules, records, institutions, and controlled contingencies. Immortality and repeated bereavement make him mentally old and brooding in the Nicolas Flamel mold while his body stays young; he opens, jokes, and shows warmth most freely with his wife. Kassandra increasingly becomes the hardliner who will break a rotten structure or make the irreversible field decision. Her public severity and affinity for disruptive freedom mellow in private with her husband. Neither change may arrive as a chapter-40 personality switch.
- **Marriage is durable, not frictionless.** Once they become a couple, Theron and Kassandra have occasional real spats over control versus improvisation, preservation versus decisive destruction, and how much danger either may carry alone. They repair conflict through changed behavior and practical compromise. Across mortal generations they become each other's only permanent contemporary, without treating their children or loved ones as disposable.
- **Do not repeat behaviours, lines, or motifs.** Say an idea ONCE. Theron states the intent to save people at most once, not every chapter. Do not reuse signature phrases or gestures as refrains (no recurring "be in the room," "reading ahead," "grain doesn't vanish," "warm and cold in his chest," "new save file"). Vary how his cleverness, foreknowledge, and care are shown each time.
- **Chapter budget is fixed at 40 for Act One; skip boring stuff.** Cut dull travel and connective filler (Skip/Light weight). A chapter can cover several canon quests or weeks when only the result matters. Never pad to preserve an old arc count.

## Maturation Spine, Chapters 11-40 (hard constraints)

- **Chapters 11-16, instincts under pressure:** Theron begins converting foreknowledge into repeatable systems rather than personal rescues; Kassandra becomes willing to violate a bad plan when living people require it. Their Athens disagreements establish the order/chaos split as complementary judgment, not ideology or romance drama.
- **Chapters 17-25, chosen principles:** Theron's ethical rules for memory work, evidence, and political intervention harden. Kassandra becomes less forgiving toward coercive institutions while remaining protective of individuals. Love is acknowledged only after both have seen the other's approach save what their own would have lost.
- **Chapters 26-33, partnership becomes a household:** Darius, the Cult campaign, Alexios's cure, and Aspasia force them to combine durable structure with disruptive action. Their first couple's spat has a concrete operational cause and a concrete repair. By chapter 33 they choose marriage with no ambiguity about equal standing.
- **Chapters 34-38, immortality and doctrine:** Atlantis tests Theron's attraction to systems and Kassandra's attraction to decisive freedom. Each recognizes the danger of their own extreme because the other names it. They face Aletheia as spouses and become immortal together, with the order/chaos balance already practiced rather than newly invented.
- **Chapter 39, the mortal generations:** Structured time jumps cover their children being born, growing, disagreeing with them, aging, and dying, followed by the natural deaths of every mortal loved one. Grief makes Theron inward, old-minded, and brooding; it makes Kassandra publicly harder. Their private life moves the other back toward warmth, and occasional spats prove they can bend without breaking.
- **Chapter 40, mature founders:** Theron fully embodies patient order without tyranny; Kassandra fully embodies disruptive judgment without cruelty. They found the Eclipse as equal counterweights and as the last people who remember every shared generation firsthand.

## Eagle Vision, Glaux, and the Forge Key

- Theron's 1% Isu blood and MEM/PER-heavy build produce **concentrated Eagle Vision**: narrow range, exceptional resolution. He can isolate material seams, mechanisms, energy routes, chemical traces, intent cues, and individual targets. It supports engineering and planning, not prophecy or omniscience.
- Kassandra's stronger Isu heritage produces **broad Eagle Vision**: wide-range threat awareness, tracking, battlefield geometry, and rapid target discrimination. Theron helps her consciously awaken and control it in chapter 3, but the power is hers.
- Neither form reads minds, reveals unknown facts without evidence, or replaces investigation. Fatigue, sensory overload, unfamiliar Isu systems, and deliberate masking remain limits.
- **Glaux** is a small Isu-engineered owl found dormant in the quarry cache. Theron restores her through the Forge Key. She scouts by night and in enclosed ruins, can share sight with Theron after training, and becomes his counterpart to Ikaros. She is intelligent but remains an animal companion, not a speaking exposition device.
- Glaux's owl silhouette later becomes the Eclipse symbol. Do not add other owl familiars casually; owl imagery belongs specifically to Theron, Glaux, and the Eclipse.

## The Eclipse

- Theron and Kassandra formally establish the Eclipse in chapter 40 after seeing freedom and imposed order fail in both human and Isu hands.
- **Creed:** **"No truth is absolute. No order eternal."** Preserve this wording exactly.
- **Doctrine:** Chaos makes dissent, adaptation, invention, and progress possible. Unchecked chaos degrades trust and society. Order gives stability, memory, and coordination. Excessive order creates stagnation, resentment, and tyranny. The Eclipse prevents either force from becoming permanent.
- **Position:** The Eclipse is a third order above the Assassin-Templar binary in perspective, not an authority that openly commands either faction. It will observe, infiltrate, redirect, expose, rescue, or remove pressure points so neither freedom nor control wins forever.
- **Secrecy:** The Eclipse never declares open institutional war on Assassins or Templars. Individual members of either order may become enemies when they threaten the balance, but history should mistake Eclipse interventions for coincidence, internal reform, betrayal, or ordinary political change.
- **Symbol:** Owls, rendered most often as an owl crossing a darkened sun. Assassins inherit the eagle and Templars the cross; the Eclipse keeps the owl.
- **Founders:** Theron leans toward order, supplying doctrine, long strategy, science, records, and hidden infrastructure. Kassandra leans toward chaos, supplying judgment, field command, disruptive correction, and the refusal to let abstraction excuse cruelty. Each founder checks the other's excess; neither outranks the other.
- **Guardrail:** Balance is not passive neutrality or equal support for every side. The Eclipse acts against whichever force is becoming absolute and judges outcomes by human freedom, stability, adaptability, and survival.
