# Story Plan: New Game Plus: The Eagle Bearer

> Fanfiction — Assassin's Creed (Odyssey era, then the wider timeline).
> Mode: Here for the Ride. POV: Third Person Limited (Theron). Pacing: Long (3000-5000 chars).
> Style target: natural conversational webnovel prose, varied rhythm, grounded detail. **No em dashes** in story text (use commas, periods, ellipses).

## Logline

A modern gamer reincarnates as a Kephallonian orphan beside young Kassandra, armed with the **Assassin's Creed System** and perfect memory of every game. He turns the tragedy of Assassin's Creed Odyssey into a fix-it story: saving the doomed, curing the broken, winning the Eagle Bearer's heart the slow way, and by chapter 70 standing immortal beside her over the corpse of an Isu god. Then the two of them walk out into eternity to save humanity from the sun.

## Two-Act Shape

- **Act One — Power Fantasy (prologue + ch 1-70):** The AC Odyssey main story and DLC beats, compressed and re-steered by Theron's foreknowledge. Ends with both leads immortal, the family healed, Aletheia dead. Tone: rising, triumphant, warm.
- **Act Two — Slice of Life / Big-Idea (ch 71-540, to be continued):** Two immortals wander the mortal world across centuries, recovering Pieces of Eden, out-thinking surviving Isu, and racing the coming solar catastrophe. Tone: cosy, episodic, occasionally vast. Only skeletoned here.

## Protagonist Rules (hard constraints)

- **Never loses on information.** Theron has played every AC game; the System boosted his memory to perfect recall. He always knows what is coming.
- **OP but beatable.** He can be tricked or physically overwhelmed, but he ALWAYS has a backup plan and ALWAYS overcomes. Losses are temporary and set up a comeback.
- **Never written as stupid.** He uses pre-knowledge wherever it plausibly helps and hides what he cannot explain (laundering it through Herodotos, Sokrates, Hippokrates, "hunches," or the System).
- **Physical first, science later.** Early wins lean on System boosts, cheap shop gear, and Kassandra's muscle. Over time he pivots to Isu tech and modern imagination; by Act Two he is the brain, Kassandra the scalpel.
- **The System is a budget, not a genie.** Game-changers (Isu blood, Pieces of Eden, immortality, Isu knowledge) are absurdly expensive. Everything is earned through treasure, bounties, orichalcum, and (late) bartering recovered Isu artifacts.
- **The System is a SECRET until ch ~68-69.** No in-world character knows Theron has a System, a shop, stats, or any "game" until he chooses to tell **Kassandra**, late (around ch 68-69, near their immortality). The concept of a LitRPG interface or modern technology does not exist in their world, so no one can even guess it. Characters may notice he "goes somewhere" (stares, tunes out) or "says strange things," and will write it off as daydreaming or eccentricity, never as a system. Theron never opens the shop, reads stats, or uses modern/gamer language out loud where it could be understood. All System beats stay in his internal narration.
- **His feelings hide even from himself.** The romance is slow and self-concealed. Theron does not consciously recognize his own feelings for Kassandra for a long time, and once he does, he hides them well. Early chapters are found-family and partnership, not pining. Other characters (especially Kassandra) must NOT read romantic or knowing looks off him early on. She may tease him for being odd or distant, never for looking at her "a certain way."

# Act One — Chapter Beats (1-70)

Chapter counts per arc are targets, not straitjackets. Graph node ids are referenced for grounding.

## Arc 0 — The Player (Prologue) · `prologue.md` (unnumbered)
`arc-arc-0-the-player-prologue` · `event-therons-reincarnation` · `ability-assassins-creed-system`
- **Prologue (`chapters/prologue.md`):** A modern salaryman, hollow and bored, games to numb himself. Watching the Valhalla ending late at night, a stupid domestic accident kills him. He wakes coughing seawater on a Kephallonian beach as a small boy. The **System** boots up: beginner gift pack (growing all-attribute talent boost incl. perfect memory; 1% Isu blood) and a shop he cannot yet afford anything good in. Markos finds him. Establish voice: modern, wry, quietly terrified, already cataloguing what game he is in and who he must save.
- The prologue is a standalone, unnumbered chapter; numbered chapters begin at ch 1 with Kephallonia.

## Arc 1 — Kephallonia · ch 1-9
`arc-arc-1-kephallonia` · `character-markos` `character-phoibe` `character-kassandra` `character-elpenor`
- Growing up under Markos with Kassandra (a few years older, fierce) and later little **Phoibe**. Siblings-in-arms, no romance yet; deep trust forms.
- Beats mirror Odyssey's opening: Markos's debts, the **Cyclops of Kephallonia**, stealing the obsidian eye, small mercenary errands. Theron wins by preparation and cheap shop tools; starts earning drachmae.
- Theron privately maps the future and marks his save-list (Phoibe, Alexios, Brasidas...). He plants the seed of "we should learn everything about the old ruins," foreshadowing the tech pivot.
- **Elpenor** hires them; Kassandra learns "the Wolf of Sparta" is her father. They leave on the **Adrestia** with **Barnabas**. Theron already knows Elpenor is Cult and is playing along.

## Arc 2 — Megaris & the Wolf · ch 10-15
`arc-arc-2-megaris-the-wolf` · `character-nikolaos` `character-stentor`
- Fighting for Sparta in Megaris; meeting **Stentor**; the reveal that the target, the Wolf, is **Nikolaos**, Kassandra's step-father.
- Theron steers the confrontation toward sparing Nikolaos and plants the first thread of eventual reconciliation. Nikolaos reveals Kassandra is not his blood and to seek Myrrine.
- Theron begins reading Isu ruins as engineering, not myth (first Ancient-Forge foreshadow).

## Arc 3 — The Cult Unveiled · ch 16-23
`arc-arc-3-the-cult-unveiled` · `faction-cult-of-kosmos` `concept-sages` `character-alexios`
- Phokis, Delphi, the manipulated Pythia; hunting and killing **Elpenor**; disguised infiltration of the **Sanctuary of Kosmos**.
- The Pyramid reveals **Deimos is Alexios**. Theron, who knew, uses the moment to start quietly studying the Pyramid as Isu memory technology (seed of the deprogramming cure).
- Establish the Cult as the human-scale antagonist; Theron's foreknowledge lets them start mapping Sages faster than canon.

## Arc 4 — Athens · ch 24-31
`arc-arc-4-athens` · `character-perikles` `character-aspasia` `character-sokrates` `character-herodotos` `character-hippokrates`
- Perikles's symposium and the Athenian cast. Theron uses **Herodotos/Sokrates/Hippokrates** as cover to voice foreknowledge as reasoning.
- **Aspasia** enters. Theron already knows she is the Ghost of Kosmos and begins a long con: use her to gut the Cult while shielding Phoibe.
- First real science outlet: Theron feeds **Hippokrates** hygiene/quarantine insight, setting up the plague arc and Phoibe's survival.

## Arc 5 — The Plague & Saving Phoibe · ch 32-38
`arc-arc-5-the-plague-saving-phoibe` · `event-saving-phoibe` `character-phoibe` `character-perikles`
- The Plague of Athens. The centerpiece: **Theron prevents Phoibe's canonical murder** (extract her before the errand, or intercept the Cult guards at the Odeon). The tonal turning point that confirms the fix-it premise.
- Perikles's fall is the hard case (historically fixed, heavily guarded). Theron cannot save everyone yet, and the near-miss with Phoibe teaches him the cost of foreknowledge and the limits of the System this early. (An original ally can fall here to keep stakes real.)
- They flee Athens toward Myrrine.

## Arc 6 — Finding Myrrine · ch 39-45
`arc-arc-6-finding-myrrine` · `character-myrrine` `character-xenia`
- Argolis, Korinth, Keos, Naxos: Chrysis, the Monger, **Xenia** the pirate queen (Theron turns her treasure hunts into shop income / orichalcum).
- Reunion with **Myrrine**. The first brick of the healed family. Learn a Spartan king is a Cult Sage; set course for Sparta.

## Arc 7 — Sparta & the Kings · ch 46-52
`arc-arc-7-sparta-the-kings` · `character-nikolaos` `character-brasidas`
- Return to Sparta, the trial, exposing the Peloponnesian Sage, reclaiming citizenship and the family home. **Brasidas** ally established (and flagged for saving later).
- Family knitting continues; Theron and Kassandra's bond deepens through shared grief and victory (romance moves from unspoken to acknowledged).

## Arc 8 — Cutting the Branches · ch 53-58
`arc-arc-8-cutting-the-branches` · `thread-the-cult-of-kosmos-campaign` `character-darius` `faction-order-of-the-ancients`
- The systematic Cult hunt across Greece, accelerated by foreknowledge. Sage by Sage, branch by branch.
- **Darius** introduced as an optional ally/tech-mentor (the first hidden blade fascinates the tech-minded Theron); the **Order of the Ancients** is the parallel threat. **No Natakas romance, no Elpidios** (ledger #6).
- Theron's science pivot accelerates: he is now buying Isu knowledge in the shop and building devices.

## Arc 9 — Reuniting the Family · ch 59-64
`arc-arc-9-reuniting-the-family` · `event-deprogramming-alexios` `character-aspasia` `character-brasidas` `event-the-in-laws-dinner`
- **Battle of Amphipolis:** Theron breaks the ambush that kills Brasidas in canon (**saves Brasidas**); Kleon falls.
- **Deprogramming Alexios:** Theron uses reverse-engineered Isu memory tech to truly cure Deimos. Alexios restored.
- **The Ghost of Kosmos reveal (Aspasia)** on Theron's terms, not as a shock. Cult broken.
- **Mount Taygetos catharsis** and the **In-Laws Dinner**: Nikolaos, Myrrine, Kassandra, Alexios, Stentor, Markos, Phoibe, and Theron at one table. Payoff of the found-family and betrayal-wound threads.

## Arc 10 — Fate of Atlantis & Immortality · ch 65-70
`arc-arc-10-fate-of-atlantis-immortality` · `character-pythagoras` `location-atlantis` `item-staff-of-hermes-trismegistus` `character-aletheia` `event-killing-aletheia` `event-the-twin-immortals`
- The Atlantis / Fate of Atlantis span: **Pythagoras**, the sealing of Atlantis, the simulations (Elysium / Underworld / Atlantis), and the truth of the Isu, the Human-Isu War, and the Great Catastrophe.
- Kassandra inherits the **Staff of Hermes Trismegistus** (immortal). Theron secures his own immortality via the **System** (concentrated Isu blood + Isu longevity, paid for by bartered artifacts).
- **Ch ~70 climax:** learning the Isu intend to shape humanity's future again, Theron **kills Aletheia** and draws the line: humanity's fate belongs to humanity. Act One ends with two immortals, a healed family behind them, and the sun's threat ahead.

# Act Two — The Long Road (ch 71-540) · TO BE CONTINUED
`arc-arc-11-the-long-road-tbc` · `thread-remove-the-isu-save-humanity-from-the-sun` · `thread-therons-science-pivot`

Skeleton only; to be planned when we reach it.

- **Premise:** Two immortals wander the mortal world across centuries. Cosy, episodic slice-of-life punctuated by big-idea sci-fi problems. Kassandra is the physical legend; Theron is the mind who beats the Isu because they are frozen in ancient genius and he is not.
- **Recurring engine:** recover/neutralise Pieces of Eden and Isu vaults; out-think surviving Isu consciousnesses (Juno and others); build human technology from Isu blueprints; quietly seed the future (the Hidden Ones / the Creed).
- **Endgame:** engineer humanity's survival of the coming solar catastrophe (the Second Catastrophe / the sun) — the ultimate "remove the Isu, save humanity from the sun."
- **Tone:** the romance is settled and warm; the drama is problems and wonder, not relationship angst. Occasional returns to the (mortal, aging, then gone) found family give the immortality weight.

## Running Reminders

- **Read the writing samples in `.github/agents/writing-samples/` once at the start of every writing session** and match their craft: confident exposition delivered in clean blocks then moved past, dry wit under the surface, varied sentence and paragraph rhythm, system/LitRPG beats dropped in matter-of-factly.
- **No lazy negatives.** Never define a thing by stacking what it is NOT ("not this, not her, not any of them"). State what IS. One clean positive line beats three negations.
- No em dashes in prose. Keep Theron's cleverness earned and shown, never narrated as "he was smart."
- Kassandra's speech: blunt, wry, Greek oaths ("malaka"), soldier's tenderness. Theron's: modern, dry, self-deprecating internally, warm outwardly.
- Every saved life must cost something (planning, a trade, a risk), so the fix-it never feels free.
- Update `summary.md` after each chapter and log any new divergence in the Continuity Ledger above.
