# Story Summary: New Game Plus: The Eagle Bearer

## Prologue: Continue?
A bored 31-year-old office worker dies in a trivial fall at home while replaying the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla. He wakes as a nine-year-old boy on a beach on **Kephallonia**. The **Assassin's Creed System** boots as a plain game menu: a **STATUS** screen (STR 5, AGI 6, END 5, INT 13, PER 8, CHA 6, WIL 7, MEM 16), the **Enhanced Talent** trait (all attributes gain a compounding growth bonus with age, potential not power), and 1% Isu blood. A **SHOP** shows a **0-drachmae balance** with concrete prices (bread 1 obol, rope 3 obols, salve 8 obols; six obols to a drachma) climbing to astronomical, locked figures (Isu blood, Fragments of Eden). He recognizes he is inside AC Odyssey with full memory of the games, and clocks that the growth trait plus future Isu blood/tech is a path to real power. The merchant **Markos** finds and rescues him. He takes the name **Theron**.

## Chapter 1: The House of Markos
Five months after arriving on Kephallonia, Theron (age 9) is established in Markos's household alongside Kassandra (12). His STATUS shows STR 6, AGI 7, END 5, INT 13, PER 9 (PER ticked up this chapter), CHA 6, WIL 7, MEM 16. Balance ends at 6 dr 5 ob after two debt-collection jobs: eight drachmae recovered from a south-road farmer named Dorokles (via a payment structure Theron invented on the spot) and seventeen drachmae from a harbor creditor named Adrastos (Kassandra's physical presence closing the gap when negotiation alone was not enough). Markos pays them their shares. The knife purchase is set for the next day. Theron's internal save-list is established: Phoibe (not yet in Sami), Alexios (already taken, decades of work required), Brasidas (Amphipolis is twenty years out). Kassandra is shown already drilling on her own, building toward something she can feel without naming it. The two establish their working dynamic: she is the physical presence, he reads the situation and shapes the terms.
