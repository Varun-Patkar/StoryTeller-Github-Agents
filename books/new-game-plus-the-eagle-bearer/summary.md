# Story Summary: New Game Plus: The Eagle Bearer

## Prologue: Continue?
A bored 31-year-old office worker dies in a trivial fall at home while replaying the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla. He wakes as a nine-year-old boy on a beach on **Kephallonia**. The **Assassin's Creed System** boots as a plain game menu: a **STATUS** screen (STR 5, AGI 6, END 5, INT 13, PER 8, CHA 6, WIL 7, MEM 16), the **Enhanced Talent** trait (all attributes gain a compounding growth bonus with age, potential not power), and 1% Isu blood. A **SHOP** shows a **0-drachmae balance** with concrete prices (bread 1 obol, rope 3 obols, salve 8 obols; six obols to a drachma) climbing to astronomical, locked figures (Isu blood, Fragments of Eden). He recognizes he is inside AC Odyssey with full memory of the games, and clocks that the growth trait plus future Isu blood/tech is a path to real power. The merchant **Markos** finds and rescues him. He takes the name **Theron**.

## Arc 1: Kephallonia

- **Chapter 1, The House of Markos:** Five months after arriving, Theron (9) and Kassandra (12) recover Markos's debts through Theron's negotiation and Kassandra's physical presence. Theron's status reaches STR 6, AGI 7, END 5, INT 13, PER 9, CHA 6, WIL 7, MEM 16, and his balance reaches 6 dr 5 ob.
- **Chapter 2, The Better Tool:** Theron buys a four-drachma iron knife, rope, and bandages through the secret System, then begins knife training under Kassandra. They recover six stolen oil jars from three thieves at an abandoned limestone cut by combining Theron's scouting and tripline with Kassandra's fighting; Theron promises to disclose his personal risk in future plans. His balance ends at 5 dr 1 ob, and a precisely built wall beneath the quarry opens a thread for later ruins research.
- **Chapter 3, The Owl Beneath the Hill:** Theron and Kassandra honor their risk-disclosure promise, survive the failing Isu cache beneath the quarry, recover the damaged Forge Key, revive Glaux, and awaken complementary forms of Eagle Vision. Fifteen years of training and repair bring them to the adult Odyssey opening as equal partners; Theron's Key reaches 46% integrity, his balance reaches 318 dr 4 ob, and Phoibe arrives with urgent news.
- **Chapter 4, The Price of an Eye:** Phoibe helps Theron and Kassandra defeat the Cyclops's collectors, dismantle his Kephallonian debt network, and recover his 2,400-drachma obsidian eye as capital and leverage. They settle Duris's honest debt without destroying his stock, expose the fraud against Markos's vineyard, and end with Theron's balance at 326 dr 4 ob as a mainland contract arrives.
- **Chapter 5, A Ship Out:** Elpenor hires Kassandra and Theron to kill Nikolaos, the Wolf of Sparta. They kill the isolated Cyclops at Kleptous Bay, free Barnabas and his crew, reclaim the Adrestia, secure the quarry cache, and leave Phoibe and Markos protected on Kephallonia before sailing for Megaris; Theron's balance ends at 462 dr 0 ob.
