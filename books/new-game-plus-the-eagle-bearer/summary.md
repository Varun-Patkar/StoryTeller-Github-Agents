# Story Summary: New Game Plus: The Eagle Bearer

## Prologue: Continue?
A hollow, bored modern salaryman games late at night to numb himself, watching the ending of Assassin's Creed Valhalla. A trivial domestic accident (a slipped power strip, a table corner) kills him mid-scene; his last thought is that he never saved. He wakes as a small boy, coughing seawater on a Kephallonian beach. The **Assassin's Creed System** boots up and distributes its Beginner's Gift Pack: a growing all-attribute talent boost (including a perfect, now-unlocked memory of every game he ever played) and 1% Isu blood, plus a vast, mostly unaffordable shop. Realizing he is inside AC Odyssey with full foreknowledge of who is doomed, he resolves to save them all this time. The merchant **Markos** finds and rescues him. The boy takes the name **Theron** and asks about a sharp older girl named **Kassandra**. ("New save file. Let's not get everyone killed this time.")

## Chapter 1: The Terror of Kephallonia
Six years later (431 BCE). Theron has grown up on Kephallonia under Markos alongside **Kassandra** (older, fiercely protective, carries Leonidas' broken spear) and little **Phoibe**. Established: their sibling bond, Theron's careful policy of "say nothing, do everything, be in the room" because he knows Kassandra's future wounds; his nightly ritual of standing before the **System shop** doing math against impossible prices (Isu blood/Pieces of Eden/Isu knowledge all unaffordable), so he instead stockpiles cheap life-savers (poultices, rope) one obol at a time, having learned people die in the small gaps. Phoibe arrives to summon them: Markos has bought a **vineyard** (with money he doesn't have) and owes Kassandra a horse. Theron privately renews his vow to save Phoibe (whose canonical death he knows the date and street of) and everyone else. The trio heads off to collect the debt as the larger game (the Cyclops, Elpenor, the Cult) stirs, unseen by Kassandra. Kassandra only notices he "goes somewhere" when he stares off, and teases him for saying strange things; she has no inkling of the System (which stays entirely in Theron's head). Theron: "Good day for it." Kassandra: "You say the strangest things, little brother."

## Chapter 2: The Obsidian Eye
At the **vineyard**, Markos admits he borrowed the purchase money from the **Cyclops**, and sends Theron and Kassandra to **Sami** to collect a debt from Duris in exchange for Kassandra's promised horse (**Phobos**). Theron, having quietly pre-bought rope and numbing salve that morning on a "feeling," insists they hurry; Kassandra has learned to trust his instincts. A harbor boy intercepts them: the Cyclops's thugs have taken **Phoibe** to **Ktimene Beach**. Theron talks Kassandra out of a headlong charge and leads them down a goat path to hit the thugs from the dark side of the beach; the rescue is clean, Theron positioning himself to trip/rope the runner so Kassandra handles the rest. **Phoibe is saved** (the canonical-death thread quietly beaten). Interrogation reveals the Cyclops hired the mercenary **Talos the Stone Fist** to kill Kassandra, and that Markos's whole vineyard is mortgaged to the Cyclops. Theron proposes stealing the Cyclops's prized **obsidian eye** to force a trade for the debt, handing Kassandra the plan an hour before Markos will "invent" it himself, keeping his foreknowledge hidden. They head home, Phoibe asleep on Theron's back. (System stays entirely internal; no romance beat, just found-family.)

## Chapter 3: The Man With One Eye
Theron and Kassandra infiltrate the **Cyclops's estate** over the seaward wall during the watch change and steal the prized **obsidian eye** (Theron's edge is preparation: pine pitch on his palms for grip, foreknowledge of the guard rotation, not raw power). Markos proposes the sell-it-back plan (which Theron had already fed him), but the Cyclops moves first, returning to the island and torturing a prisoner at **Kleptous Bay** to bait Kassandra out. Theron steers her to do it "the smart way," fighting from position with his rope while Kassandra does the heavy work; the Cyclops folds fast. Kassandra humiliates him by feeding the obsidian eye to a goat. The freed prisoner is **Barnabas**, captain of the **Adrestia** (the ship the Cyclops had stolen), who pledges his ship and joins them, thanking the gods for his own kidnapping. The crew now has a ship and a road off Kephallonia toward the mainland, the letter-writing **Elpenor**, and "the Wolf of Sparta." (System internal; deepening trust between Theron and Kassandra, not romance.)

## Chapter 4: The Wolf of Sparta
**Elpenor of Kirrha** hires Kassandra, first with a test: recover **Penelope's shroud** from Odysseus' palace on **Ithaka**. On the way they also save **Odessa** (another quiet fix-it turn-aside). Theron privately recognizes Elpenor as a Cult agent and the whole path as the story's fixed road, but lets it play out because the road runs through Elpenor to the people worth saving. Elpenor then names the real contract: assassinate the **Wolf of Sparta** in **Megaris**. Theron knows the Wolf is **Nikolaos**, Kassandra's step-father, and agonizes (internally, secretly) over whether to warn her, deciding he cannot without cracking the six years of trust he's built and revealing what he can't explain. His plan: let Kassandra reach that choice with her own hand so Nikolaos can be spared and the family can begin to heal, while he stays beside her. They sail for Megaris; **Barnabas** reveals the Wolf is the Spartan general Nikolaos, and Kassandra realizes it is her father. Theron: "Then we'd better find out what kind of man he is now, before you decide what to do about it." (System internal; heavy emotional beat, no romance.)

## Chapter 5: Earning the Wolf
Arriving in **Megaris** (through an Athenian blockade), they meet **Stentor**, the hard young Spartan officer who is Nikolaos's adopted son (and, unknown to both, Kassandra's step-brother). He forces them to earn an audience with the general by breaking the Athenian forces harrying his hills. Over two months Theron proves his value the quiet way, reasoning out that missing Spartan supply trains are not Athenian raids ("grain doesn't vanish") but an inside job, and exposing the rival mercenary **Hyrkanos**, which earns Stentor's grudging respect. Kassandra, who could have taken the coin and stayed quiet, refuses to; Stentor notices something about her he can't place. Late at night Kassandra confronts Theron about how he "always knows" (the goat path, Phoibe, Hyrkanos); Theron deflects honestly-but-incompletely ("I pay attention... somebody has to think three steps ahead"), protecting the System secret; she chooses to accept it. She asks what she'll do when she faces her father; Theron refuses to decide it for her ("That's yours. I'll be standing next to you either way"). A runner arrives: the Athenians are broken, and the Wolf will see her. (System internal; found-family/trust deepening, no romance.)

## Chapter 6: A Fate Worse Than Death
Kassandra meets **Nikolaos** alone in his tent (Theron waits just outside, refusing to steal the moment). She confronts him about Mount Taygetos; he offers no defense, explains the ephors/Delphi decree and his own cowardice, and kneels to accept her knife. By her own choice Kassandra **spares him**, sentencing him instead to live and carry the guilt ("Death is not the fate you have earned"). Nikolaos reveals he is **not her blood father** (Myrrine carried her before him), tells her **Myrrine is alive** and to seek her, and asks her, when she finds her brother, to tell him it was never his curse but Nikolaos's cowardice wearing a prophecy for a mask. Outside afterward, Kassandra grapples with whether it was mercy or cruelty; Theron: "It was both. That's why it was right." First real crack toward healing the family: father spared and alive, mother confirmed living, brother still to be saved. They resolve to sail at first light to find Myrrine. (System internal; the sparing is Kassandra's own choice per plan; no romance, only steadfast presence.)
